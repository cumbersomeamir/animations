# Werbens — 30s portrait promo · Storyboard

Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 900 frames, H.264 + AAC. Style: editorial paper-cutout world + expressive stickman rig.
Palette (restrained, from the approved icon): Werbens violet `#6236F5`, lilac `#D2A5F0`, ink `#1B1433`,
paper `#F4EFE6`, one energy accent coral `#FF6B5A` (used only for "chaos" + creator cap).

Product claims used (all from `assets/listing.json`): *discover creators*, *manage brand campaigns, briefs and
conversations*, available on Google Play (Android). Phone screens are **explicitly stylized illustrations**,
not real Werbens UI. No metrics, prices, endorsements or outcome guarantees.

Characters: **B** = brand manager (ink stickman, hair bun, violet tie, holds the brief).
**C1** = creator (coral backwards cap, phone). **C2** = creator (lilac headphones). **C3** = creator (yellow beanie, camera).
Recurring motif: the **violet ribbon** from the icon's "W" — it is the cause of every fix.

Music: original, procedurally synthesised, 120 BPM (1 beat = 15 frames). Hesitant pizzicato motif in act 1, a
"womp" on the crash, riser + silence into the phone slam, full groove (D–Bm–G–A) from 0:08, final sting at 0:26.5.
Narration ducks music by ~7 dB.

Safe zones: captions in y 250–560 (top band, clear of status bar), stage action y 560–1500, nothing critical below
y 1560 (platform UI overlays). Text min 40 px, captions 74–84 px.
