## 7. Creative decisions + skill adjustments (accepted recipe; saved for future generation)
- Same renderer/studio/caption treatment/CTA as hindi-03 (`src/` copied, then only the episode objects changed).
- Hero objects: a flat black knit sweater (extruded outline, rib texture) with 520 instanced lint specks; two mint adjective
  tiles that land on it and change nothing (lint puffs and settles back); a fictional lint roller (white sticky sheet, teal end
  caps + handle) that rolls in, is set at the neckline and makes **one pass over the right half only**, so before and after
  share one frame (labels पहले / बाद में, mint dashed divider); the specks it passes hop onto the roll, whose lint skin fills in;
  the roller lifts, yaws and turns its lint-covered sheet to camera ("सबूत"); three mint rule notes on their words; a glossy
  "beauty filter" pane slides over the clean half on "फ़िल्टर" and is pulled away with an ink ✕ on "नहीं"; CTA as accepted.
- Reusable lessons for future Hindi episodes: (1) the one-pass split is a cheap, honest before/after inside one frame; (2) check
  English loanwords in eSpeak hi before scripting (लिंट lost its n; use फ़ for /f/); (3) the Hindi voice stem is ~4.6 dB hotter
  than the old English blend — keep voice gain ×0.82 to preserve the accepted mix ratios.
