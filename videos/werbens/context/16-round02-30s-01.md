## 5. Creative decisions (accepted recipe applied; only colours/logo corrected)
- Hero: one extruded arch (procedural ExtrudeGeometry) in the brand primary; touchpoints: white logo tile on a base, paper
  bag with handle, post sign — each with the printed arch and the name PLIMM. Contrast shapes: star (#AEA2F4), circle
  (#8C93A1), triangle (#101114). Covers in brand grey. Clean #F7F8FA sweep, soft key/rim, contact-shadow decals.
- Colour calibration: 3D base colours pre-compensated (#362AEC etc.) so the lit front face renders ≈ #573EEC under the
  accepted ACES renderer (measured #5F3CEB vs plain #573EEC → #8758EB). The official icon face is unlit (exact #6435FC).
- Captions: accepted treatment, pill in app ink #101114, highlights #AEA2F4. CTA: official Play icon tile (+25 % size) +
  werbens.com lockup (circle + half-circle mark, "Werbens" in Fahkwang Bold, black) + small grey series line.
- Sound: one held pad per scene, event-tied piano notes; the 3-note stamp figure returns only at the restamp (an echo of the
  lesson, not a loop); out-of-key notes on the swaps; foley per action (thud, clack, stamp, card, peel, zip, flip, snap…).
