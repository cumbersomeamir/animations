# 09 — Educational Pilot 01: "Why identical products can feel different in different packaging"

## One concept
**Packaging works as a cue: it sets an expectation before the product is experienced, and that expectation can colour how the
product is judged.** Takeaway: make the packaging promise what is really inside. (Sources + mapping: `education/pilot-01/sources.md`.)

## Props (fictional, original)
- **Plain box**: light, squat, soft-cornered matte grey carton, stamped "COOKIES" label. Lid hinged on its right edge.
- **Luxe box**: heavy, tall, angular hexagonal prism, glossy deep violet, white foil band, lavender lid that pops off, star emblem
  and spaced "C O O K I E S". Same word on both boxes: same product, different packaging.
- **Cookies**: 3 per box, every one an instance of the same geometry and material (identical by construction).
- **Overhead boom inspection lamp** with a pull cord (set dressing from frame 1; pays off as the "X-ray" in S4).
- **Thought bubble** = Quibble's expectation (small grey cookie vs big glowing cookie).
