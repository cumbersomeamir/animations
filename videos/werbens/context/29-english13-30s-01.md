## 7. How the context/27 rules were applied (creative decisions)
- **Thumbnail → hook:** "SAME BOX. / PAY MORE?" (4 words, Space Grotesk 700, hero gradient) over the hook image: two identical
  teal boxes, the left one with a hex key in front and the taller bid stack, plus the app.werbens.com logo and wordmark. Frame 0
  of the video already has both boxes falling into place.
- **One real question:** "why pay more for one?"
  - The super hook names the only difference, "you built it".
  - The build-up shows IKEA turning that into a deal: "we do our part, you do yours".
  - The proof shows the study and then its boundary: unfinished or undone, the boost vanished.
  - The payoff answers once ("Effort adds value when it ends in success") and gives the action.
- **One visual idea at a time, revealed in sequence:**
  1. Two identical boxes and an "=".
  2. Bid coins stack 5 vs 3.
  3. The coins clear; the left box half-unfolds and snaps back while a hex key turns.
  4. Lateral move to a flat pack: "WE", then "YOU", and the pack opens into a net that folds into a box.
  5. Back to the study set: the left box lies flat, then folds up side by side with the hex key; its stack rises; the identical
     ready-made box is handed in and gets the shorter stack.
  6. Two sides drop (unfinished), then the rest fold flat (taken apart); the extra coins vanish (stacks level).
  7. The box folds back up; the last side clicks in on "success" (check mark, glow, coins return).
  8. The front side drops as "A REAL STEP" and snaps up on "finish"; "NOT BUSYWORK".
  9. The CTA tile.
- **Labels:** "WE", "YOU", "A REAL STEP", "NOT BUSYWORK" — large, brief, one at a time. No source chips, no paragraph cards, no
  decorative lines.
- **Reusable lessons:**
  1. Fold-up nets (four hinged sides) make "build", "unfinished" and "taken apart" readable as states of one object.
  2. Keep the right box out of the left box's unfolding reach. The super-hook replay keeps the facing side up; the ready-made
     box is handed in further right.
  3. Clear the hook's coins before the super hook so the box is the only idea on screen.
  4. Set illustrative ratios to the measured ratio (5 : 3), not a round 2 : 1.
  5. When Whisper drops a possessive, fix the token text, not the times.
