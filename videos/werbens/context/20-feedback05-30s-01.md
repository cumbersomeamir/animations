## 7. Approved style notes + skill adjustments (saved for future generation)
- Same renderer/studio/caption treatment/CTA as hindi-03/04 (`src/` copied; only the episode objects changed).
- Hook from frame 1: clutter stickers (Devanagari adjectives, drawn stars, amber/teal/ink/mint) slap onto a white ad board
  on an easel, flying in from the camera side with squash; "?" beside it. On "अब" they peel and fall away; three dashed
  numbered slots appear; a 3D thali slides onto its stand; on "फ़ोटो" a shutter flash fills the photo slot with a **live render
  of that same thali** (render target) — the honest product proof; ₹250 tag stamps on "कीमत"; "अभी ऑर्डर करें" button pops on
  "काम" and is pressed. Comparison: a cluttered copy slides in; amber gaze path wanders over it (brand header dims on "ब्रांड"),
  teal path goes 1→2→3 on the clean ad (labelled as an illustration). Rule: badges 1/2/3 + labels beside each element.
- Reusable lessons: (1) render-to-texture lets the ad's "photo" be the real object in the scene; (2) say prices as explicit
  numbers ("दो सौ पचास"), not fractions; (3) check caption group ranges against the word counts; (4) keep labels beside, never
  over, the element they name; (5) loanwords ending in "-ेंट/-िंग" need the "-न्ट/-न्ग" respelling for eSpeak hi.
