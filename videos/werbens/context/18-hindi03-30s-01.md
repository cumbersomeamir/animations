## 3. Hindi voice: what was available, tested and chosen (facts)
- Installed: Kokoro-82M v1.0 ONNX (Apache-2.0, cached) + kokoro-js 1.2.1 voice packs, which include the official Hindi voices
  **hf_alpha, hf_beta** (female) and hm_omega, hm_psi (male). kokoro-js' bundled eSpeak WASM has English data only, so Hindi
  G2P came from **eSpeak NG 1.51** (Ubuntu archive package `espeak-ng`, `-v hi`), post-processed exactly like misaki's
  `EspeakG2P` (stress kept, tie "^", E2M map, ties/hyphens removed — read from the misaki 0.7.4 wheel source; not executed).
  All Hindi IPA symbols produced are in Kokoro's vocabulary (checked). No cloud TTS, no cloning; the English blend is not used.
- "Listening" = Hindi ASR with Whisper large-v3-turbo (onnx-community, MIT; language=hindi). Scores = script words found in
  the transcript after spelling-equivalence normalisation (nukta, chandrabindu≈anusvara, nasal+halant≈anusvara):
  | Test | Result |
  |---|---|
  | hf_alpha vs hf_beta, 72-word draft, speed 0.98 | 63/74 vs 58/74 → **hf_alpha** |
  | speed 1.15 vs 1.22 (67-word script) | 55/69 vs 56/69 (same) → **1.22** (0.98 gave 31.7 s of speech; 1.22 gives 25.5 s) |
  | final narration (with "-इये" glides) | 57/69 (qa/asr-narration.json) |
  Typical residual "misses" are spelling variants (शांदार/शानदार, ज्यादा), "-इए" verbs heard as "-ए" (बनाए/जुकाए/दिखाए),
  "के"→"की", "विशेषण"→"विशेशन", "ब्रांड्स"→"ब्रांस" — the words are recognisable; no line was lost.
- Pronunciation edits (spoken only; captions keep standard spelling): "-इए" imperatives respelled "-इये" (बनाइये, झुकाइये,
  पूछिये, दिखाइये, समझिये, लीजिये) so eSpeak emits the natural glide [-ɪjeː]. No numbers, dates or currency are spoken.
- Speech/caption alignment: no offline Hindi word-timestamp model, so `src/words_hi.mjs` maps punctuation phrases to the
  measured voiced segments (10 ms envelope) and splits each by eSpeak phoneme counts; caption groups change at real pauses.

## 6. Creative decisions (accepted recipe; palette/logo from context/17)
- Hook: three white/mint word tiles (शानदार · मज़बूत · बेहतरीन, Devanagari on the faces) stack into a wall that hides a
  covered product; "?" beside it. Reveal: the wall topples and slides out, the white/teal packaging hood tips back → a
  navy non-stick tawa on a stove ring, a white plate with a mint rim. Demo: "बिना तेल" note, batter spreads and browns, the tawa
  tilts, the dosa slides off the rim and drops onto the plate (squash, dust). Memory: push-in, teal glow ring on "याद".
  Technique: the tag "नॉन-स्टिक तवा" lights up (the adjective), "कैमरे पर कैसे दिखाऊँ?" note, viewfinder brackets +
  "रिकॉर्ड" dot, the same test replays (second dosa lands on the first). Real test: a cardboard mock-up dosa pops up and falls
  flat ("नकली"); "असली टेस्ट" on the real plate. CTA: props slide off; the app.werbens.com logo tile lands; Space Grotesk
  gradient wordmark + Hindi series line "ब्रांड्स कैसे बेचते हैं, समझिए".
- Captions: accepted pill/word-group treatment in Noto Sans Devanagari 800 (52/74 px), pill #0F172A, highlights #7FE7DC.
- Audio: accepted recipe (one held pad per scene + event-tied piano notes, foley per action); voice gain scaled ×0.82 so the
  hotter Hindi stem (−16.3 LUFS vs English −20.9) keeps the accepted voice/music/foley ratios.
