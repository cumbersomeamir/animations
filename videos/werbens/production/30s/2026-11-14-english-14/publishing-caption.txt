£2.30 or £2.80: which mayo is the better buy?

Same brand. Now switch the unit. Per 100 grams: 107p versus 52p. About double. Research shows we focus on the number, not the unit. Listing snacks in kilojoules, not calories, raised healthy snack choices. Shoppers: compare every option in the same unit. Sellers: show unit prices clearly, in one unit.

Follow Werbens to decode how brands sell.
