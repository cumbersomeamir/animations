Why does free shipping make you add one more thing?

Your basket is 40. Shipping costs 6, but it's free from 50. Pay 46 as it is, or add a 10 item and pay 50. Four more, and you get an item instead of a fee. In one retailer's data, threshold offers drove extra sales. But lost shipping revenue made them unprofitable there. So add only what you'd buy anyway. Sellers: check the margin.

Follow Werbens to decode how brands sell.

The basket, products and amounts in the video are a made-up illustration (no currency, no real shop); the arithmetic is exact: 40 + 6 = 46, 40 + 10 = 50, 50 − 46 = 4. The study figures are not shown; movements show direction only.
