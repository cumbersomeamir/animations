Why do shoppers keep more extras when they start full?

One builds up from basic. One trims down from full. In three studies, trimming down kept more options, at a higher total price. Across price levels and product types. But for shoppers barely set on buying, starting full put them off. Where a choice starts steers where it ends. Start committed buyers full, but every paid extra needs their own yes.
