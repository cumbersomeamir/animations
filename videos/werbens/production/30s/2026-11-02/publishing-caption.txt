A feature is not yet a benefit.

A feature says what a product has. A benefit says why it matters to the person using it.
Example (fictional backpack): "water-resistant fabric" is the feature; "your notebook stays dry on a drizzly commute" is the benefit.

In an ad: show the situation, connect the feature to the outcome, and support the claim.
And match the wording to the evidence: water-resistant is not the same as waterproof.
