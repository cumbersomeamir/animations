Why can free pull people away from the better treat?

Two scoops, one coin apart. Now cut both by one coin. The gap stays the same, but one is now free. In experiments, far more chose the free one, and far fewer the better one. Free didn't just cost less; it seemed to add value. The effect was strongest for treats, weaker for practical items. So save free for things people enjoy, where it pulls hardest.
