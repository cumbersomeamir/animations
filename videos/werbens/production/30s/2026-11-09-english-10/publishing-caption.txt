Learn more... about what?

Here are two buttons for a fictional meal kit service. 'Learn more' hides what happens next. 'See the 3 plans' says it before you tap. Web accessibility guidelines from the W3C say a link's purpose should be clear from its text. So name the next step: a verb plus what they'll get, like 'Compare 3 plans' or 'Book a 15-minute call'. No guessing what comes next.
