Why did people search the cooler for one bottle?

Share a Coke swapped the logo for popular first names. It started in Australia, with 150 names. So every cooler became a search: is my name here? Research suggests our own name grabs attention when others slip by. Spot a friend's name, and it becomes a gift. Give shoppers something to find, and someone to give it to.
