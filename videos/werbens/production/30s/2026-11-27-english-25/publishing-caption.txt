Why does IKEA walk you through rooms first?

Its store guide says: start with the showroom. Whole rooms, furniture in action. Flat packs come later. Alone, a sofa is a size and a price. In a room, it's your evening. Studies suggest picturing yourself using a product can make you want it more. At the shelves, you're finishing a room. Don't just show the product. Show the life around it.
