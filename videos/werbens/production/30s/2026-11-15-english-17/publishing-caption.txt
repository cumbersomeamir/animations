Why can empty space make a product look more premium?

Same bottle, two displays: packed tight, or given room. In lab and field studies, more space made products seem more valuable. Items looked more attractive, and the store more prestigious. Not scarcity, not tidiness: the space itself. Less can look like more, but it's no guaranteed sale. Give your best product room: fewer items, space around each.
