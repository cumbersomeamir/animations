Why can one option you'll probably skip change what you pick?

Here are two made-up plans: Basic and Plus. Add a more expensive Premium, and Plus becomes the middle choice. We judge options against their neighbors, not alone. In consumer studies, middle options gained appeal; extremes lost it. So one extra tier reshapes the whole menu. Use it honestly: build every tier for a real customer, not as bait.
