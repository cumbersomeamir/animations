Why would anyone post a brand's card for free?

Each year, Spotify Wrapped turns your listening into your story. Top songs, top artists, minutes played, on a ready-to-share card. You're not posting an ad. You're posting you. Studies suggest we often share to shape how others see us. Each card carries the brand to that person's friends. Give customers a story about themselves worth sharing.

Follow Werbens to decode how brands sell.
