Is a 320-rupee sandwich expensive?

Beside 150-rupee snacks, it can look pricey. Add a 1,200-rupee platter above it, and it can look fair. That's anchoring: the high price sets the reference. In a 2006 study, one extreme price in a catalog raised what people would pay for other items. So list a real premium item next to it, and never fake a 'was' price.
