24 jams? Or just 6?

In a 2000 study, shoppers were more likely to buy from a table of 6 jams than from 24. But a review of 50 experiments found the average effect was nearly zero. It depends: overload grows when options are hard to compare. So make choosing easy: group your options, and say who each one is for. Like: for kids, for cheese boards, for baking.
