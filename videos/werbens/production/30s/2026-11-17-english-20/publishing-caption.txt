Why do prices end in 99, not a round number?

Only a cent less, and the first digit drops. In studies, 2.99 felt smaller than 3.00. But 3.59 versus 3.60? No effect: same first digit. In field tests, nine endings lifted demand, most for new items. So use a nine where it flips the first digit. For feel-good purchases, a round price can feel right.
