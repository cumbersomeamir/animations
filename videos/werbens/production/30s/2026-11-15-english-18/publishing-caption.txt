Can a sound make the same drink seem fizzier?

Same water, two glasses. Only the sound changes. In lab tests, turning the fizz up made the water seem more carbonated. Held closer to the ear, the same water seemed fizzier. But in the mouth, sound changed nothing; touch and tingle took over. So sound sets the promise before the first sip. Let them hear the fizz, then make the drink deliver it.
