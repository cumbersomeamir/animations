Same reward, same eight washes. Which card gets finished?

In a 2006 car wash study, both cards needed eight washes. The one that started with two free stamps was completed more often, and faster. That's the endowed progress effect: a head start feels like you've already begun. So give customers a real head start: a welcome stamp, or a checklist with the first step done.
