Why can a free sample sell what a perfect ad can't?

An ad asks you to believe; a sample lets you check. In a 1983 study, opinions from trying predicted buying better than opinions from ads. Field tests found samples brought in new buyers, with effects up to a year later. Results varied widely between brands. So if one taste proves it, let people try. Make the sample prove your main claim.

Follow Werbens to decode how brands sell.
