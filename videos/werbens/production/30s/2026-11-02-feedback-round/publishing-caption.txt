Why would a menu include a bad deal?

A medium drink at 280 ml for Rs155 sits right next to a large at 300 ml for Rs160. Paying Rs5 more for 20 ml makes the large feel like the obvious upgrade. That medium is a decoy: an option that can make another look better by comparison.

Before paying extra, ask how much you actually want. If you only want 150 ml, the large means paying Rs70 more for 150 ml you didn't need.

Menu prices are illustrative. Research finds the effect can be strong when options are compared as numbers and weaker when people judge products by look or taste.
