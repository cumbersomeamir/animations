Same product. New perception.

So why does one feel expensive? In 1960, ice cream makers gave a luxury brand a made-up name: Häagen-Dazs. Then backed it with a rich recipe. But can a name change the taste? Coke and Pepsi are chemically nearly identical. Yet in a study, knowing one drink's brand shifted preferences. A brand shapes expectations before the first bite. Signal a real strength, then deliver it.

Follow Werbens to decode how brands sell.
