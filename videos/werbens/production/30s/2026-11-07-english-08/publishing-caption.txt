Same cookies. Why do the last two look more tempting?

In a 1975 study, cookies from a nearly empty jar were rated more desirable. Cookies that ran low because others wanted them were rated higher still. That's scarcity: what's running out can seem more valuable. So show real limits: true stock counts and real end dates. But a fake 'only 2 left' is a known dark pattern.
