Identical boxes. Why pay more for one?

One difference: you built it. IKEA's pitch: we do our part, you do yours. In a 2012 study, people who assembled an IKEA box bid more than people handed one ready-made. But when they couldn't finish, or took it apart, the boost vanished. Effort adds value when it ends in success. So give customers a real step they can finish, not busywork.

Follow Werbens to decode how brands sell.
