# Werbens — 15 s Three.js style test · Storyboard

**Medium:** real-time WebGL (Three.js r180) rendered deterministically offline — one continuous camera take along
`CatmullRomCurve3` paths, physically-based dimensional cards (rounded boxes, clearcoat, RoomEnvironment reflections),
an `InstancedMesh` field of 420 creator tiles, emissive accents through `UnrealBloomPass`, lavender fog for depth.
This is deliberately unlike the paper-cutout stickman production: no characters, no flat drawing, no cuts.

**Palette (sampled from the icon):** violet `#6234FB`, lavender `#CCC0F7`, pale lavender `#F0EAFC`, white `#FEFEFD`; ink `#1B1433` for text only.
**Claims:** listing only — discover creators; manage brand campaigns, briefs and conversations; Google Play.
Phone screen + cards are **stylized UI illustrations**, not real Werbens screens.
