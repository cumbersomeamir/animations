## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Paper or phone? You're stopped at a traffic check, and your licence is at home. India's motor vehicle rules say: you
can show it in electronic form, in DigiLocker, or the mParivahan app. Those count like the original papers. Once an
officer verifies it electronically, the paper can't be demanded, even if there's an offence and documents need seizing.
So add your licence to DigiLocker before you drive. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law explained."
- **TTS spellings:** "Diji Locker" and "M Pari-vaahan" ("DigiLocker" was voiced as "Diggy Locker").
- **Rewording:** "Stopped at a traffic check" became "You're stopped at…" (the ASR heard "Stop to").

## Citations [VERIFIED; full text in production notes/LEGAL-SOURCES.md]
- CMVR r.139 as amended by G.S.R. 1081(E), 2 Nov 2018: "in physical or electronic form". The amending text was read via
  a reproduction; the MoRTH PDF was not retrievable (checkpoint).
- MoRTH letter RT-11036/64/2017-MVL, 8 Aug 2018 (official copy annexed to Kerala Police Circular 25/2018, sha256
  4a0dd38a…c3d): DigiLocker/mParivahan records "at par with the original documents"; impounding recorded
  electronically.
- PIB 26 Sep 2020 (ID 1659408; G.S.R. 584(E), w.e.f. 1 Oct 2020): validated documents "shall not be demanded" in
  physical form, "including in cases where there is an offence made out necessitating seizure".
