## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Thirty grams missing. Legal? Your one kilo pack holds nine hundred seventy grams. The rules allow a small error:
fifteen grams for a one kilo pack. This is double that. Complain. A Legal Metrology officer can test it at the shop and
seize short packs. Marked 'when packed'? Environmental loss can be excused. Weigh just the contents, keep the bill, and
complain to Legal Metrology. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law explained."

## Citations [VERIFIED; full text in production notes/LEGAL-SOURCES.md]
- Packaged Commodities Rules 2011 (DoCA Gazette scan, read visually):
  - r.6(1)(c): net quantity of the commodity contained in the package
  - r.21(1)(i), (2), (3) with the "when packed" proviso, and (4)
  - First Schedule Table I: 500–1000 g → 15 g
- No amendment through G.S.R. 826(E) of 21 Sep 2026 changes these. None was found after it.

## Pronunciation and wording (for future English episodes)
- "Complain, and a Legal Metrology officer" was heard as "an illegal metrology officer", so it became "Complain. A
  Legal Metrology officer…". Avoid "and a Legal…" before "Legal".
- `textnorm.py` gained "one"→"1", "thirty"→"30" and "nine hundred seventy"→"970" for WER.
- Captions longer than ~50 characters with long words can overflow two lines at the minimum size and throw in
  `layoutCaption`. Split them at a natural pause.
