## Legal basis [VERIFIED FACT — details and links in production notes/LEGAL-SOURCES.md]
- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (G.S.R. 462(E), 23 Jul 2020): Rule 6(3) (marketplace sellers) and
  Rule 7(4) (inventory e-commerce entities) — no refusal to take back or refund goods that are defective, deficient,
  spurious, not as advertised/agreed, or delivered late (force majeure excepted for late delivery).
- Return/refund/exchange policy must be displayed: Rules 5(3)(c), 6(5)(g), 7(1)(a). Change of mind is not a trigger
  in 6(3)/7(4), so it depends on that displayed policy.
- Amendment Rules 2026 (G.S.R. 789(E), 9 Sep 2026) come into force 1 Jan 2027; per PIB + clause-by-clause analyses
  they add disclosure duties and do not change 6(3)/7(4). Checkpoint: re-check the official Gazette text.
