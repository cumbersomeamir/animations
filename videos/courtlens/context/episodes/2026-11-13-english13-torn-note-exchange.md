## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Torn note. Worthless? The bank says: no account, no exchange. But RBI rules say every branch must exchange notes,
customer or not. Torn in two, nothing missing? That's soiled: exchanged free at the counter. A piece gone? It's still
accepted, and paid by what's left: full, half, or nothing. Refused? Make a written complaint. No reply in thirty days?
Go to the RBI Ombudsman. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law explained."

## Citations [VERIFIED; full text in production notes/LEGAL-SOURCES.md]
- **RBI Master Direction – Facility for Exchange of Notes and Coins** (RBI/DCM/2026-27/395, 1 Apr 2026, updated
  16 Oct 2026):
  - paras 1.1, 1.3, 1.7: all branches, customers and others, no discrimination
  - para 3: soiled, including a two-piece note pasted together with nothing missing
  - para 4.1: mutilated, presented at any branch and adjudicated under the NRR
  - para 6.1.1: up to 20 notes / ₹5,000 a day exchanged free over the counter
  - para 9: complaint to the branch → Ombudsman after 30 days or rejection
- **RBI (Note Refund) Rules 2009, as amended 2018**, rule 8(2) for ₹50 and above:
  - full value: largest piece > 80%
  - half value: 40–80%
  - otherwise rejected
- **RB-IOS 2026** (in force 1 Jul 2026; replaces RB-IOS 2021), RBI FAQ: first approach the bank with proof; escalate
  after 30 days or an unsatisfactory reply, within 90 days.
