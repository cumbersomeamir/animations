## Final script (74 words, 27.01 s of speech) [VERIFIED]
"The bottle says MRP twenty rupees. The shop wants twenty-five, because it's chilled. Is that legal in India?
Packaged goods must carry a maximum retail price, and it already includes all taxes. A shop can't sell above it,
chilled or not. Restaurants and hotels serving you are a different case. Overcharged? Keep the bill, snap the label,
and contact the National Consumer Helpline: 1 9 1 5. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law explained."
("1 9 1 5" is written as separate digits so the voice reads "one nine one five", not "nineteen hundred fifteen".)

## Citations [VERIFIED; full text in production notes/LEGAL-SOURCES.md]
- Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (G.S.R. 202(E)):
  - Rule 18(2), no sale of a packed commodity above its retail sale price (Gazette scan from DoCA).
  - Rule 2(m), MRP is the maximum price inclusive of all taxes (G.S.R. 629(E), 2017).
  - Rule 6(1)(e), the MRP must be printed (as amended 2017/2021).
  - Checked through the latest amendment, G.S.R. 826(E) of 21 Sep 2026. None changes these rules.
- Supreme Court, CA 21790/2017, 12 Dec 2017, para 17: the Legal Metrology Act doesn't stop sale of mineral water
  above MRP in hotels and restaurants, because that is service, not a simple purchase. The video says only
  "a different case".
- NCH contact page: 1915 / 1800-11-4000, 08:00–20:00 except national holidays; WhatsApp/SMS 8800001915; portal, app, UMANG.
