## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Your snack. Their rule? The cinema says no outside food. The Supreme Court says they can: cinemas set their own entry
rules. But cinemas told the court drinking water is free. They also said a child's food is allowed, in reasonable
amounts. On a medical diet? The court asked cinemas to consider it, case by case. Ask for free water, pack your child's
food, and show the manager a doctor's note. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law explained."
