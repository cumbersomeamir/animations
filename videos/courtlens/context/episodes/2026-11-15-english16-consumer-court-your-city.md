## Topic history check and decision [VERIFIED 2026-11-15]
- **Proposed:** "Can a shop refuse a refund for a defective product in India?" (a NO REFUNDS receipt vs a broken
  appliance, defective vs change of mind).
- **Already covered:**
  - 01: an online NO REFUNDS on a broken item (E-Com Rules 6(3)/7(4); defective vs change of mind)
  - 04: a shop bill "no refund" on a defective kettle (CPA s.2(47)(viii); bill's period or 30 days)
  - It was also declined as a duplicate in batch 13.
- **Chosen:** the genuinely fresh next step in the same genre. The product is faulty, the company won't reply and is
  in another state: must you fight them in their city?
  - You can file where you live or work (CPA s.34(2)(d)).
  - You can file yourself on e-Jagriti (s.35(1)(a) + e-filing proviso; PIB 2025).
  - You can appear in person; a lawyer is optional (NCDRC reg. 3(4)).
  - The deadline is two years (s.69).
  - The remedies are repair, replacement, refund or compensation (s.39(1)).

## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Their city, or yours? Faulty phone, no reply, and the company's in another state. Under the Consumer Protection Act,
you can file where you live or work. File it yourself on e-Jagriti, and appear in person. A lawyer is optional. The
deadline: two years from when the problem arose. The commission can order a repair, replacement, refund or
compensation. Keep your bill, and every reply, then file with your district commission. Follow Courtlens for everyday
Indian law explained."

## Citations [VERIFIED; full text in production notes/LEGAL-SOURCES.md]
- CPA 2019 (official DoCA PDF): ss.34(2)(d), 35(1)(a) + proviso, 39(1)(a)–(d), 69(1)–(2).
- Jurisdiction Rules 2021 (G.S.R. 912(E)): District Commission up to ₹50 lakh.
- NCDRC Procedure Regulations 2020, reg. 3(4): in person, agent or counsel.
- PIB 23 Dec 2025: e-Jagriti launched 1 Jan 2025 and operational in all states and UTs (file, pay, virtual hearings).
- Fee (notes only): nil up to ₹5 lakh, per the 2023 amendment as reproduced by a legal database. The official
  e-Gazette was unreachable.

## Pronunciation and wording (for future English episodes)
- "e-Jagriti" is spoken close to the name; ASR spells it "eJagridi" (proper-noun spelling). Respellings ("Jaagriti",
  "Jaagritee") were worse.
- "Keep the bill and every reply" was heard as "bill in every reply", so it became "Keep your bill, and every reply,".
- Long lists need few commas to stay short. The remedies line was tightened to fit 27.4 s of speech.
