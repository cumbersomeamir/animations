- **Chosen instead:** pre-payment charges on loans. It is the same genre (one everyday money situation, a conditional

  2025, updated 1 Jul / 1 Oct 2026. The answer is conditional and not a universal invented right: floating-rate
  non-business loans carry no charge; fixed-rate loans may carry a disclosed charge.

## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Pay early, get fined? You want to close your loan early. The lender adds a two percent charge. RBI rules say: on a
floating rate loan you took for anything but business, banks and finance companies can't charge you for paying early.
Part or full, even with money from a new lender. Fixed rate? A charge may apply, if your loan papers disclose it. Before
you prepay, check floating or fixed in your sanction letter. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law explained."
(l5 originally said "banks and NBFCs". The ASR heard "NBFC", dropping the plural, so it became "banks and finance
companies"; the screen shows "BANKS & NBFCs".)
