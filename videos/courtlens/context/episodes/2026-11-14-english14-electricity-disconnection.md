## Jurisdiction
All of India: the Electricity Act, 2003 is a central Act. Theft and unauthorised-use cases (ss.126/135) are separate
and are noted, not narrated.

## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Wrong bill. Lights out? The company says: pay it all, or we cut you off. But the Electricity Act requires fifteen
clear days' written notice first. Disputing the bill? Deposit, under protest, the lower amount: what they claim, or your
six-month average for each month. Then they can't cut you off while the dispute is pending. So put your dispute in
writing, pay the lower amount, and keep the receipt. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law explained."
