## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Your car. Your risk? The valet ticket says: parked at owner's risk. The Supreme Court ruled in twenty nineteen: once a
hotel's valet takes your keys, the car is in its care, even if the valet service is free. Lost or damaged? The hotel
must prove it took proper care. An owner's risk line can't excuse its own negligence. Parked it yourself? Then this
rule doesn't apply. Keep the ticket. Report any damage right away, in writing. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law
explained."
