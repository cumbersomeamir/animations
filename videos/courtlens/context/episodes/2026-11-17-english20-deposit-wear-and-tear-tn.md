## Jurisdiction decision [VERIFIED]
- India has no single national tenancy statute; the Model Tenancy Act 2021 is a model for states. The series already
  verified the Tamil Nadu Act (episodes 11–12), and its Gazette text answers the question directly. The video therefore
  names **Tamil Nadu**. The Act extends to its urban areas (s.1(2)), with the s.3 exclusions noted in the sources file.

## Final English script (as spoken) [VERIFIED]
"Deposit gone, for wear and tear? You moved out. The landlord blames faded paint. Tamil Nadu's tenancy law says: keep
the flat as you got it, except normal wear and tear. Repainting? The landlord's job, whatever the agreement says.
Damage you cause, like a broken window, can be deducted. The rest is due when the landlord takes the empty flat back.
Photograph every room, moving in and out. Get deductions in writing. Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law
explained."

## Citations [VERIFIED; full text in production notes/LEGAL-SOURCES.md]
- TN Act 42 of 2017 (Gazette copy via PRS, sha256 09a11942…cc1a91):
  - s.11(2) as amended by Act 39 of 2018 s.9: refund at the time the landlord takes over the vacant premises, after
    due deduction of the tenant's liability
  - s.15(1): notwithstanding any contract; as good as at the start except normal wear and tear; Second Schedule split
  - s.16(a): no intentional or negligent damage
  - Second Schedule Part A item 2: whitewashing walls and painting doors and windows = landlord
- No 2023–2026 amendment found. Act 19 of 2022 doesn't touch these sections, and its commencement is unconfirmed.
