## 1. Tamil Nadu Regulation of Rights and Responsibilities of Landlords and Tenants Act, 2017 (TN Act 42 of 2017)
- Source PDF (Tamil Nadu Government Gazette pages, with amending Acts 39 of 2018, 22 of 2019, 3 of 2020 and 19 of
  2022 appended): https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/tamil-nadu/2017/2017TN42.pdf
  (sha256 `09a11942f56720463ac19b4c007bc806531601840e632b8e7c8904e86ccc1a91`; same file as english-11). The official TN
  hosts did not connect from this environment.
- **s.17, "Entry with notice" (verbatim, as enacted):**
  > (1) A landlord or the property manager may enter a premises in accordance with written notice given to the
  > tenant at least twenty-four hours before the time of entry under the following circumstances, namely:-
  > (a) to carry out repairs or replacement or do or get work done in the premises;
  > (b) to carry out an inspection of the premises for the purpose of determining whether the premises is in a
  > habitable state;
  > (c) for any other reasonable reason for entry specified in the tenancy agreement.
  > (2) The written notice shall specify the reason for entry, the day and time of entry between 7.00 A.M. to 8.00 P.M.
- **Amendment:** TN Act 19 of 2022, s.5, changes s.17(1) to "may enter the premises let out on rent after serving a
  notice to the tenant, in writing or through electronic mode". That Act comes into force on a notified date; the
  notification was not confirmed. The narration says "written notice", which is true under both versions; an
  electronic notice is still written.
- **s.34 (as numbered after the 2018 amendment), Rent Court jurisdiction:** "only the Rent Court and no Civil Court
  shall have jurisdiction, except the jurisdiction of Rent Authority under section 31, to hear and decide the
  applications relating to disputes between landlord and tenant and matters connected therewith and ancillary thereto
  under this Act". Under s.31 (2018), the Rent Authority's powers cover ss.9, 10, 14, 15 and 20, not s.17. So an entry
  dispute is for the Rent Court.
