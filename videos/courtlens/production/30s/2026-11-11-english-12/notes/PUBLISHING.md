## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Their house. Your home? Can your landlord just let themselves in?

In Tamil Nadu, the tenancy law draws the line. They may enter for repairs, an inspection, or a reason in your
agreement, with written notice at least 24 hours ahead, saying why, which day, and a time between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m.

No notice? Ask them to come back with one. If it keeps happening, the Rent Court can hear it.
