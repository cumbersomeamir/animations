## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Wrong bill. Lights out? The company says: pay it all, or we cut you off.

But the Electricity Act requires 15 clear days' written notice first. Disputing the bill? Deposit, under protest, the
lower amount: what they claim, or your six-month average for each month. Then they can't cut you off while the dispute
is pending.

So put your dispute in writing, pay the lower amount, and keep the receipt.
