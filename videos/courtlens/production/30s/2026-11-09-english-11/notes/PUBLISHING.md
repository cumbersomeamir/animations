## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Your landlord wants you out, and cuts the power to your flat. Is that allowed in Tamil Nadu?

The state's tenancy law says no landlord shall cut off essential supplies, electricity included. Eviction needs a
Rent Court order, on grounds the Act lists.

So complain to the Rent Authority. It can order the power restored at once, pending its inquiry. Attach rent
receipts, photos and dates. Other states have their own rules.
