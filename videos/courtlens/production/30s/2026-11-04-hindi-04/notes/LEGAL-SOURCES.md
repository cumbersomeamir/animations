Status key: **VERIFIED-PRIMARY** = official Gazette/Act text from the Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA) host;

## Why this is not a repeat of episode 01
Episode 01 (2026-11-02, English) covered an **online** seller's "NO REFUNDS" banner under the **Consumer Protection
(E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** (Rules 6(3)/7(4)). This episode covers a **physical shop's printed bill**
("बिका माल वापस नहीं होगा / रिफ़ंड नहीं") under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s.2(47)(viii)**. That provision
carries a different, concrete lesson: the refusal is judged against the period written on the bill, or **30 days**
if the bill states none, so the bill is both the clock and the key evidence. The audience is Hindi. The user asked for
this topic; it was kept and made distinct rather than replaced.

## 1. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Act 35 of 2019) [VERIFIED-PRIMARY]
Official PDF from DoCA "Consumer Protection Act" page (https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/consumer-protection-acts):
https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/CP%20Act%202019_1732700731.pdf
(sha256 `0ae7f82ab5b77d50087cd4cd2dd2dbc12755c0bdb31371fdefd042b020d50756`)

- **s.2(10) "defect":** "any fault, imperfection or shortcoming in the quality, quantity, potency, purity or standard
  which is required to be maintained by or under any law … or under any contract, express or implied or as is claimed
  by the trader … in relation to any goods or product".
- **s.2(47)(viii), unfair trade practice (verbatim):**
  > refusing, after selling goods or rendering services, to take back or withdraw defective goods or to withdraw or
  > discontinue deficient services and to refund the consideration thereof, if paid, within the period stipulated in
  > the bill or cash memo or receipt or in the absence of such stipulation, within a period of thirty days;
- **s.2(47)(vii):** "not issuing bill or cash memo or receipt for the goods sold" is also an unfair trade practice
  (context for "keep the bill"; not narrated).
- **s.35(1):** a complaint may be filed with the District Commission by the consumer, including one "who alleges
  unfair trade practice".
- **s.39(1), District Commission orders** (it "shall issue an order … directing him to do one or more of the following"):
  - (a) remove the defect
  - (b) replace with new goods free from defect
  - (c) return the price with interest
  - (d) compensation
  - (g) discontinue the unfair trade practice
- **s.69(1):** complaint within two years of the cause of action (notes only).

## 2. Change of mind [VERIFIED-PRIMARY reading]
s.2(47)(viii) covers **defective** goods (and deficient services). No provision of the Act creates a general right to
return non-defective goods because the buyer changed their mind. Return then depends on the shop's own policy.
Narration: "मन बदला हो, तो वापसी दुकान की नीति पर है।"

## 3. Printed "goods once sold will not be taken back" notices [SECONDARY]
- LiveLaw report: the Ernakulam District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission held such printed conditions on
  bills to be an unfair trade practice under the CPA 2019 and directed their removal.
  https://livelaw.in/consumer-cases/ernakulam-district-commission-swiss-time-house-unfair-trade-practices-return-exchange-253580
- Reported Kerala government direction to traders to stop printing it. → **CHECKPOINT A**: read the order and any
  appeal before citing in copy. Not used on screen; the video rests on the Act text.
