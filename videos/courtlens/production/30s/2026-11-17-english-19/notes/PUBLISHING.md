## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Eighteen rupees for their bag? The billing counter just added it to your bill.

The National Consumer Commission said: shops can sell bags, but buying one must be your choice. Printed with the shop's
name or logo? Then they can't charge for it. They can't stop you bringing your own empty bag. And notices at the
entrance must tell you before you shop.

Logo bag? Ask for it free. Plain bag? Say no, or bring your own.
