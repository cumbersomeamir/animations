## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
The cashier says: no phone number, no bill. Can they insist?

Consumer law lists not issuing a bill for goods sold as an unfair trade practice. And GST invoice rules don't ask for
your phone number at all.

Loyalty points or an e-bill are extras; sharing your number for those is your choice.

Say politely: a printed bill without my number, please. Still refused? Call 1915.
