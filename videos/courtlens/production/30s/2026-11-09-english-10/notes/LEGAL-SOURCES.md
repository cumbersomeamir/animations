## 1. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (Act 35 of 2019) [VERIFIED-PRIMARY]
- Official PDF from DoCA: https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/CP%20Act%202019_1732700731.pdf
  (sha256 `0ae7f82ab5b77d50087cd4cd2dd2dbc12755c0bdb31371fdefd042b020d50756`; same file as episode hindi-04)
- **s.2(47), "unfair trade practice", clause (vii) (verbatim):** "not issuing bill or cash memo or receipt for the
  goods sold or services rendered in such manner as may be prescribed;"
- **Clause (ix) (verbatim):** "disclosing to other person any personal information given in confidence by the
  consumer unless such disclosure is made in accordance with the provisions of any law for the time being in force."
  Context for why the number is your choice; not narrated.
- Narration uses: "Consumer law lists not issuing a bill for goods sold as an unfair trade practice." The provision
  includes the words "in such manner as may be prescribed". The narration states only that the Act lists it, and
  does not claim a specific prescribed format.

## 2. GST tax invoice particulars [VERIFIED-PRIMARY]
- CBIC, "Invoice Rules → 1. Tax Invoice" (the content of CGST Rules, rule 46):
  https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html (page sha256 `312a7b45bfea28682c751024463edb1f9682e4059640f152c1a1f0c272b1095a`)
- Required particulars include:
  - the supplier's name, address and GSTIN
  - serial number and date
  - the recipient's name, address and GSTIN or UIN if registered
  - "name and address of the recipient and the address of delivery, along with the name of State and its code, if
    such recipient is un-registered and where the value of taxable supply is fifty thousand rupees or more"
  - HSN code, description, quantity, values, rates and amounts of tax, place of supply, and signature
- **No particular asks for the recipient's mobile or phone number.** The only "mobile" on the page is an app-download
  link.
- GST Council flyer "Tax Invoice and other such instruments" (same ₹50,000 rule):
  https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/e-version-gst-flyers/Tax_Invoice_and_other_new.pdf
  (sha256 `e7aada81c167ba16bfcc5eed5fef9611aaf12bae025f8abeafd6d3d10d6fed40`)
- The current consolidated rule 46 page (taxinformation.cbic.gov.in) failed TLS verification from this environment
  and returned 503 through the web fetcher, so the CBIC rules page above was used.
- Narration uses: "And GST invoice rules don't ask for your phone number at all." (Spelled "G S T" in the TTS input so
  the voice says the letters.)

## 3. Remedy: National Consumer Helpline [VERIFIED-OFFICIAL]
- Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 4041, answered 12.08.2026 (Ministry of Consumer Affairs): NCH is the single point of
  access for grievance redressal at the pre-litigation stage, "through a toll-free number 1915" and other channels.
  https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU4041_1jbDze.pdf
- Narration uses: "Still refused? Call 1915."

## 4. 2023 Consumer Affairs "mobile number" advisory [SECONDARY — not narrated]
- News reports (Business Today and LawBeat, 25 May 2023) say the Department of Consumer Affairs advised retailers
  and industry bodies (CII, FICCI) not to insist on customers' mobile numbers for billing. The reports attribute this
  to Secretary Rohit Kumar Singh and say it followed NCH complaints.
- The advisory's own text could not be found on PIB or consumeraffairs.gov.in. Searches covered PIB, DoCA and CCPA
  advisories, the DoCA 2023 year-end review (PIB 1988681) and the National Consumer Day backgrounder 2025. It is
  therefore not quoted. Some reports also claim an IT Act s.72-A "offence"; that is unverified and deliberately not
  used.

## Distinction shown
- **The bill:** due for goods sold; the invoice has no phone field.
- **Loyalty points / e-bill:** extras the shop may offer; sharing a number for them is the customer's choice.
- **Polite response:** "A printed bill without my number, please." If refused: NCH 1915.
