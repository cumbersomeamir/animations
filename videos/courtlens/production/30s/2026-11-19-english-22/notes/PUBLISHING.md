## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Your car. Your risk? The valet ticket says: parked at owner's risk.

The Supreme Court ruled in 2019: once a hotel's valet takes your keys, the car is in its care, even if the valet
service is free. Lost or damaged? The hotel must prove it took proper care. An owner's risk line can't excuse its own
negligence. Parked it yourself? Then this rule doesn't apply.

Keep the ticket. Report any damage right away, in writing.
