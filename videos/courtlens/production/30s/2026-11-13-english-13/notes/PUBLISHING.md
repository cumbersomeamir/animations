## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Torn note. Worthless? The bank says: no account, no exchange.

But RBI rules say every branch must exchange notes, customer or not. Torn in two, nothing missing? That's soiled:
exchanged free at the counter. A piece gone? It's still accepted, and paid by what's left: full, half, or nothing.

Refused? Make a written complaint. No reply in 30 days? Go to the RBI Ombudsman.
