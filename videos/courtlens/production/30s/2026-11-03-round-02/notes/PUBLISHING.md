## Post caption (ready to paste)
MRP ₹20 on the bottle, ₹25 at the counter "because it's chilled". Is that legal in India?

Packaged goods must show an MRP, and it already includes all taxes. Under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities)
Rules, 2011 (Rule 18(2)), no shop or other seller may sell a packaged item above its retail sale price. Cooling
doesn't change the printed MRP.

Restaurants and hotels serving you are a different case: the Supreme Court (2017) held that the Legal Metrology Act
doesn't stop them selling bottled water above MRP as part of their service.

Overcharged at a shop? Keep the bill, photograph the MRP label, and contact the National Consumer Helpline: call
1915 or 1800-11-4000, WhatsApp 8800001915, or consumerhelpline.gov.in.
