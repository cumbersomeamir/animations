## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Flight cancelled. Voucher only? The airline offers a voucher instead of your money.

DGCA rules say: you choose: another flight, or a full refund. A credit shell is your call, not their default.
Cancelled with under a day's notice, and no flight that suits you? Refund plus up to ten thousand rupees, in cash or
bank transfer. Except for causes beyond their control, like weather.

Offered a voucher? Ask for your refund in writing.

Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law, explained.

(Compensation is ₹5,000–₹10,000 by flight length, or the one-way basic fare plus fuel charge if lower. The flight shown
is illustrative.)
