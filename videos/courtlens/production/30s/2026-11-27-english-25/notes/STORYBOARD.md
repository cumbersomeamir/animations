## Mechanism and visuals
- **Setting:** an airport gate at dusk: a window with a parked plane, and a one-row departures screen "FL 214 BOM
  CANCELLED".
  - It differs from episode 08's split-flap train board and from batch 24's vault.
- **One concrete mechanism:** the airline's gold VOUCHER card flips into your two choices, NEW FLIGHT or FULL REFUND.
- **Conditions, one per shot:**
  - credit shell switch (off unless you turn it on; "DEFAULT" struck out)
  - < 24 h clock + an unsuitable alternate ✕
  - refund + compensation bars ₹5,000 / ₹7,500 / ₹10,000 by flight length, paid by bank transfer
  - weather: the compensation coins get ✕ while the refund keeps its ✓
  - payoff: the voucher pushed away and the refund requested in writing
- **Text:** captions only in y≈280–460, plus one short pill per beat (YOUR CHOICE, YOUR CALL, < 24 HOURS, CASH OR BANK,
  NO COMPENSATION, REFUND, IN WRITING).
- **Thumbnail = frame zero:**
  - the dusk gate, the boarding pass stamped CANCELLED, and the gold VOUCHER card pushed over its corner
  - "FLIGHT CANCELLED. VOUCHER ONLY?"
  - voice at 0.05 s
