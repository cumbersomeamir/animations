## Provisions used (verbatim)
- **Part IV 3.3.1:**
  > airline shall inform the passenger of the cancellation at least two weeks before the scheduled time of departure
  > and arrange alternate flight/refund as acceptable to the passenger. In case the passengers are informed of the
  > cancellation less than two weeks before and up to 24 hours of the scheduled time of departure, the airline shall
  > offer an alternate flight or refund the ticket, as acceptable to the passenger.
- **Part IV 3.3.2:**
  > Passengers who have not been informed as per the provisions contained in Para 3.3.1 … the airlines shall either
  > provide alternate flight as acceptable to the passenger or provide compensation in addition to the full refund of
  > air ticket … a) INR 5,000 or booked one-way basic fare plus airline fuel charge, whichever is less for flights
  > having a block time of up to and including 01 hour b) INR 7,500 … more than 01 hour and up to and including 02
  > hours. c) INR 10,000 … more than 02 hours.
- **Part IV 3.3.4 with 1.4–1.5:** no compensation where cancellations occur due to extraordinary circumstances beyond
  the airline's control. Para 1.5 names "Air Traffic Control (ATC), meteorological conditions, security risks"; para
  1.4 names natural disaster, flood, strikes and others.
- **Part IV 3.3.5:** "The refund of air ticket shall be made in accordance with CAR Section 3, Series M, Part II."
- **Part IV 3.7.1:**
  > The compensation referred to in Para 3.2.2 and 3.3.2 shall be paid in cash, by bank transfer or with the signed
  > agreement of the passenger in the form of travel vouchers.
- **Part II clause 3(f)** (DGCA portal copy):
  > The option of holding the refund amount in credit shell by the airlines shall be the prerogative of the passenger
  > and not a default practice of the airline.
- **Part II 3(a)–(d)** (notes only): card refund within 7 days; cash immediately; agent/portal refunds remain the
  airline's onus (14 working days in the final 2026 revision per News On AIR); statutory taxes and fees always
  refunded.

## Scope notes (kept out of the narration)
- Part IV applies to the operating airline. Foreign carriers pay compensation as per their country's regulations or
  this CAR (3.6.1).
- No compensation is payable where the passenger gave no contact details (3.3.3); airport facilities (3.8.1) are not
  narrated.
- The ₹ bars show the three caps; actual compensation is the lower of the cap and the one-way basic fare plus fuel
  charge.
- No claim is made about any particular airline or any timeline beyond the CAR text.

## Fact classification
