## 1. Refund Rules and TDR Filing (IRCTC, current edition) [VERIFIED-PRIMARY]
- "Refund Rules and TDR Filing w.e.f. 12-NOVEMBER-2015". Authority: Railway Board letter No.
  TCII/2003/2015/Refund Policy/1 dated 06-Nov-2015, and the Gazette of India notification dated 04-Nov-2015 (the
  Railway Passengers (Cancellation of Ticket and Refund of Fare) Rules, 2015).
- URL: https://contents.irctc.co.in/en/RefundCancellationRules.pdf (read 2026-11-06). The PDF metadata title is
  "RefundCancellationRules_19-01-2026", created 19 Jan 2026. This edition already includes the January 2026
  amendment for Vande Bharat Sleeper / Amrit Bharat II (clause 1(f)–(g)), so it is the consolidated text as of
  January 2026. sha256 `94c77cb372dd60544266830ff235b23abd0caff7b69e509eed1f8c9a14994f49`.
- **Clause 6, "Train Running More than Three hours Late and passenger not travelled" (verbatim):** "No cancellation
  charge or clerkage shall be levied and full fare shall be refunded to all passengers holding reserved, RAC and
  wait-listed tickets, if the journey is not undertaken due to late running of the train by more than three hours
  of the scheduled departure of the train from the station commencing journey subject to the condition that (a) In
  case of e-ticket, the TDR is filed online before the actual departure of the train for availing full refund. (b) In
  case of i-ticket, the ticket surrendered upto the actual departure of the train. (c) In case the ticket is
  cancelled or surrendered or if the request for refund of fare is filed online after the actual departure of
  train. No refund of fare shall be admissible."
- **Note (5):** "No refund will be granted in case of train running more than three hours late if TDR is filled with
  this reason after actual departure of the train."
- **TDR table, row 2 (verbatim):** "Train Late More Than Three Hours and Passenger Not Travelled" — "TDR shall be filed
  Before the Actual Departure of the train for availing full refund".
- **Who decides:** "the decision about the acceptance / rejection or amount of refund will be taken by the concerned
  Zonal Railways as per extant Refund rules". The narration therefore says "you can claim", not "you will get".
- **Clause 1(d), ordinary cancellation of a confirmed ticket (January 2026 edition):** "No refund of fare shall be
  admissible on the ticket having confirmed reservation in case ticket is not cancelled or TDR not filed online upto
  four hours before the scheduled departure of the train." For Vande Bharat Sleeper / Amrit Bharat II the cut-off
  is eight hours.
- **Clause 5 (contrast, not narrated):** for a cancelled train, e-ticket refunds are automatic, with no TDR needed.

Narration uses:
- "if a train runs more than three hours late and you don't travel, you can claim the full fare, with no
  cancellation charge" (clause 6)
- "An hour before departure, that can return nothing" (clause 1(d): no refund on a confirmed ticket cancelled within
  4 hours, and within 8 hours under the 2026 slabs)
- "file a TDR online for the late train, before it actually leaves" (clause 6(a), TDR row 2)

## 2. 2026 changes to cancellation slabs [CHECKPOINT]
- Railway Board Commercial Circular 08 of 2026 (notification effective 16 Jan 2026): Vande Bharat Sleeper /
  Amrit Bharat II, no refund within 8 hours. This is reflected in the IRCTC January 2026 edition above.
- News reports (Deccan Chronicle, 24 Mar 2026, and others) say the 8-hour no-refund cut-off was extended to **all
  trains** from 1–15 Apr 2026, with new 72 h / 24 h / 8 h slabs. None of these reports mentions any change to the
  late-running rule (clause 6).
- Unresolved: the official 2026 circulars on indianrailways.gov.in/railwayboard returned HTTP 404 from this
  environment, so the post-April text could not be read. The narration does not depend on which slab applies: an
  hour before departure, a confirmed ticket gets no refund under both the 4-hour and the 8-hour cut-off. Re-check
  clause 6 in the next IRCTC edition before reuse.
