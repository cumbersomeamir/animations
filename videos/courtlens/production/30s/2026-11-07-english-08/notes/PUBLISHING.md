## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Your train is running 4 hours late, and the trip no longer works. Is your ticket money gone?

Railway refund rules say: if a train runs more than 3 hours late and you don't travel, you can claim the full fare,
with no cancellation charge.

The catch: don't just press cancel. An hour before departure, that can return nothing. Instead, file a TDR online for
the late train, before it actually leaves.
