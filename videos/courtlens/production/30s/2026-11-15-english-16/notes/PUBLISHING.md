## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Their city, or yours? Faulty phone, no reply, and the company's in another state.

Under the Consumer Protection Act, you can file where you live or work. File it yourself on e-Jagriti, and appear in
person. A lawyer is optional. The deadline: two years from when the problem arose. The commission can order a repair,
replacement, refund or compensation.

Keep your bill, and every reply, then file with your district commission.
