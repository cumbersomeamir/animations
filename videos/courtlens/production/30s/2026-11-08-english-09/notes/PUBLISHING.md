## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
The hotel wants your original Aadhaar as a deposit till checkout. Do you have to leave it?

Aadhaar's own rules let a hotel verify it, by scanning its QR code with your consent. It shouldn't collect or store
your Aadhaar number, and any copy it keeps must be masked.

So showing your card is normal; leaving the original isn't part of the process. Ask them to scan it and hand it back,
or offer another ID they accept.
