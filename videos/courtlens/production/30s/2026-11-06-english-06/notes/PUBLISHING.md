## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Your restaurant bill just added a 10% service charge. Can they make you pay it?

CCPA guidelines, upheld by the Delhi High Court in 2025, say it can't be added by default, made compulsory, or
charged under another name. Like a tip, a service charge is your choice. GST is a government tax, which you do pay.

Ask them to remove it. Refused? Keep the bill and call 1915.
