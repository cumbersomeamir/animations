## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
The ATM gave no cash, but ₹2,000 left your account. Can the bank keep it?

RBI rules say a failed ATM withdrawal must be credited back within 5 days. After that, your bank owes you ₹100 a day,
without you asking. Three days late? That's ₹300, even at another bank's ATM.

Not back? Complain to your bank, and after 30 days, to the RBI Ombudsman.
