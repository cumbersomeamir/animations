# Storyboard and timing: courtlens-30s-english21-01 (English, India)

Batch 21. Follows the 11 Nov rules, the 15 Nov first-frame addendum and the **18 Nov feedback** ("too much information
at once, too much text, motion graphics can be more appealing, super cool thumbnail"). Built on the english-20
engine. The topic was changed from the proposed change-of-mind return, which repeats episodes 01 and 04 (see
`LEGAL-SOURCES.md`).

## How the 18 Nov feedback is applied
- **One central object:** the loan, drawn as an iron "LOAN" ball chained to a gold padlock, with a red "2% FEE" tag. It
  is locked in the hook, blocked in the escalation and springs open in the answer. Short inserts make one condition
  each.
- **Less text:**
  - one 1–3-word pill per beat (FLOATING RATE, NOT FOR BUSINESS, BANKS & NBFCs → ₹0 CHARGE, ANY SOURCE, IF
    DISCLOSED, CHECK FIRST)
  - no sub-lines, no law text
- **Motion that explains:**
  - "floating" is literally a "%" buoy bobbing on layered waves
  - "fixed" is the "%" set in a concrete block that slams down
  - the fee tag is torn off; the shackle springs; the chain falls; the ball rolls free
  - a part payment knocks blocks off a debt tower, and a new lender's money clears the rest
  - a magnifier checks FLOATING / FIXED on the sanction letter
- **Thumbnail = frame zero:**
  - the padlocked LOAN ball and chain, with the red 2% FEE tag and the cash ready
  - a soft cyan halo
  - "PAY EARLY. GET FINED?" (4 words)
  - voice at 0.05 s
