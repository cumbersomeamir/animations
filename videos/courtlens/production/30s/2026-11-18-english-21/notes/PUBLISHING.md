## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Pay early, get fined? You want to close your loan early. The lender adds a 2% charge.

RBI rules say: on a floating rate loan you took for anything but business, banks and finance companies can't charge you
for paying early. Part or full, even with money from a new lender. Fixed rate? A charge may apply, if your loan papers
disclose it.

Before you prepay, check floating or fixed in your sanction letter.

Follow Courtlens for everyday Indian law, explained.

(The 2% is illustrative. Rules as of RBI's directions updated in 2026.)
