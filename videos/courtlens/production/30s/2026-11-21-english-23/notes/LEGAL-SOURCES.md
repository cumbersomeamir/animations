     physical or electronic form" are inserted
   - clause (ii) adds the Pollution Under Control certificate to the list (registration, insurance, fitness, permit,

## Scope notes (kept out of the narration)
- **The duty to produce stays:** the narration does not say you can refuse to show a licence. You show it, on the
  phone or on paper.
- **Validation:** the "not demanded" rule applies once an enforcement officer validates the details electronically
  (PIB). The video says "Once an officer verifies it electronically".
- **Other documents:** registration, insurance, fitness, permit and PUC can also be produced electronically
  (r.139). The video shows only the licence.
- **Not claimed:** whether a screenshot or photo in a phone gallery is acceptable. The MoRTH letter names DigiLocker
  and mParivahan only, and the video names only those two.
- **Later law:** searches on 2026-11-21 found no reversal. The 2020 PIB release reaffirmed and extended the
  2018 position.

## Fact classification
- **Rules and official statements:** as quoted above.
- **Illustrative:** the checkpoint, the generic licence card (not an official design), the generic app tiles (names
  only, no logos), the handheld checking device, the "OFFENCE / RECORDED" screen and the database icon.
- **Practical suggestion:** add the licence to DigiLocker before driving.
