## How the feedback is applied
- **One tangible object transformation:** a paper/plastic licence becomes the phone licence, which is verified.
- **One condition per shot:** electronic form; DigiLocker / mParivahan; same as original; verified → no paper demanded;
  offence → recorded online; add it now.
- **Text:** captions only in y≈280–460. One short pill per beat (ELECTRONIC FORM, SAME AS ORIGINAL, NO PAPER DEMANDED,
  RECORDED ONLINE, ADD IT NOW); the app tiles carry their names.
- **Thumbnail = frame zero:**
  - the windshield view of a night POLICE barricade, with red/cyan light glow, the road and a cone
  - the dashboard (foreground depth): an empty wallet slot "?" and a phone glowing with the licence
  - "PAPER OR PHONE?" (3 words)
  - voice at 0.05 s
