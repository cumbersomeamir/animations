## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Paper or phone? You're stopped at a traffic check, and your licence is at home.

India's motor vehicle rules say: you can show it in electronic form, in DigiLocker, or the mParivahan app. Those count
like the original papers. Once an officer verifies it electronically, the paper can't be demanded, even if there's an
offence and documents need seizing.

So add your licence to DigiLocker before you drive.
