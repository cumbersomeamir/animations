## Post caption (ready to paste)
Your online order arrives broken and the seller says "no refunds". Is that legal in India?

Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, a seller can't refuse to take back or refund goods that are
defective, deficient, spurious or not as described (Rule 6(3) for marketplace sellers, Rule 7(4) for sellers that
own their inventory). A "no refunds" label doesn't change that.

Changed your mind? That's different: it depends on the return policy the seller has to display.

What to do: keep the invoice, photograph the problem, and ask for a refund in writing. If it isn't resolved, write to
the platform's grievance officer (they must acknowledge within 48 hours) or contact the National Consumer Helpline:
1915 / consumerhelpline.gov.in.
