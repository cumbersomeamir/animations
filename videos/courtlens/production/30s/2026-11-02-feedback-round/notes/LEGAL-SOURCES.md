## 1. Core rule: Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 [VERIFIED-PRIMARY]
- Notification **G.S.R. 462(E)**, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution (Department of Consumer
  Affairs), New Delhi, **23 July 2020**. Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II, Sec. 3(i), No. 358,
  ID CG-DL-E-23072020-220661. Made under s.101(1)(zg) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (35 of 2019).
  In force on publication (Rule 1(2)).
- Text read from the Gazette PDF as reproduced by LiveLaw:
  https://www.livelaw.in/pdf_upload/pdf_upload-378739.pdf (sha256 `304d33a66c60819daf6e18cfdc9fa880bf3d3a26095951e82f13a5964f13744c`).
  Official Department page (linked by PIB): https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/acts-rules/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules-2020
  (and PDF https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules.pdf). This build container could
  not reach `*.nic.in` (tunnel reset / HTTP 503), so the Gazette reproduction was used. → **CHECKPOINT A**.

**Rule 6(3), duties of sellers on a marketplace (verbatim):**
> No seller offering goods or services through a marketplace e-commerce entity shall refuse to take back goods, or
> withdraw or discontinue services purchased or agreed to be purchased, or refuse to refund consideration, if paid,
> if such goods or services are defective, deficient or spurious, or if the goods or services are not of the
> characteristics or features as advertised or as agreed to, or if such goods or services are delivered late from
> the stated delivery schedule:
> Provided that in the case of late delivery, this sub-rule shall not be applied if such late delivery was due to
> force majeure.

**Rule 7(4), inventory e-commerce entities (verbatim):**
> No inventory e-commerce entity shall refuse to take back goods, or withdraw or discontinue services purchased or
> agreed to be purchased, or refuse to refund consideration, if paid, if such goods or services are defective,
> deficient spurious, or if the goods or services are not of the characteristics or features as advertised or as
> agreed to, or if such goods or services are delivered late from the stated delivery schedule:
> Provided that in the case of late delivery, this sub rule shall not apply if such late delivery was due to force majeure.

(Some commentaries cite this as "7(6)"; the Gazette text numbers it **7(4)**.)

**The return policy must be displayed. This is the basis for "change of mind depends on the policy":**
- Rule 5(3)(c) (marketplace must display): "information relating to return, refund, exchange, warranty and
  guarantee, delivery and shipment, modes of payment, and grievance redressal mechanism…"
- Rule 6(5)(g) (marketplace seller must provide for display): "accurate information related to terms of exchange,
  returns, and refund including information related to costs of return shipping in a clear and accessible manner"
- Rule 7(1)(a) (inventory entity must display): "accurate information related to return, refund, exchange, warranty
  and guarantee, delivery and shipment, cost of return shipping…"

**Useful for the action step (publishing notes only):**
- Rule 4(5): the grievance officer "acknowledges the receipt of any consumer complaint within forty-eight hours and
  redresses the complaint within one month from the date of receipt of the complaint." Rule 6(4)(b) imposes the same
  on marketplace sellers.
- Rule 5(3)(b): marketplace must give "a ticket number for each complaint lodged".
- Rule 4(10): accepted refund requests must be paid "within a reasonable period of time, or as prescribed under applicable laws".
- Rule 8: "The provisions of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (35 of 2019) shall apply for any violation of the provisions of these rules."

## 3. Consumer Protection Act, 2019, s.2(47)(viii) (context only; not on screen) [SECONDARY]
Unfair trade practice includes "refusing, after selling goods or rendering services, to take back or withdraw
defective goods or to withdraw or discontinue deficient services and to refund the consideration thereof, if paid,
within the period stipulated in the bill or cash memo or receipt or in the absence of such stipulation, within a
period of thirty days". Text read via Indian Kanoon https://indiankanoon.org/doc/117738049/ . India Code
(https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/15256) was unreachable from this container. → **CHECKPOINT C**
before quoting this section in any public copy.
