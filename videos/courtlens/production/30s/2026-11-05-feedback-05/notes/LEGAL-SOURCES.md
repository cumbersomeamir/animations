Research date: 2026-11-05. Language of the video: Hindi. Scope: an online checkout that adds an item or donation the
user did not choose. The example (sports shoes ₹999 + a pre-ticked ₹49 donation) is fictional.
Status key: **VERIFIED-PRIMARY** = Gazette text from the Department of Consumer Affairs (DoCA) host;
**VERIFIED-OFFICIAL** = official government page.

## 1. Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023 [VERIFIED-PRIMARY]
- Issued by the **Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)** under **s.18 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019**.
  Notification F. No. CCPA-1/1/2023-CCPA-(Reg), New Delhi, **30 November 2023**; Gazette of India Extraordinary,
  Part III Sec. 4, ID CG-DL-E-30112023-250339.
- Official PDF (DoCA "Consumer Protection Act" page):
  https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/The%20Guidelines%20for%20Prevention%20and%20Regulation%20of%20Dark%20Patterns,%202023_1732707717.pdf
  (sha256 `5961a07f1bc0778ee362165b262cd29cd99291ac76bd8b79bb2fa2bd50aa0472`)
- **Clause 3, application:** "(i) all platforms, systematically offering goods or services in India; (ii) advertisers;
  (iii) sellers."
- **Clause 4:** "No person, including any platform, shall engage in any dark pattern practice."
- **Clause 5:** engaging in any practice in Annexure 1 is a dark pattern practice.
- **Clause 2(e)**, "dark patterns": deceptive UI/UX design "amounting to misleading advertisement or unfair trade
  practice or violation of consumer rights".
- **Annexure 1(2), "Basket sneaking" (verbatim):**
  > "Basket sneaking" means inclusion of additional items such as products, services, payments to charity or
  > donation at the time of checkout from a platform, without the consent of the user, such that the total amount
  > payable by the user is more than the amount payable for the product or service chosen by the user:
  > Provided that the addition of free samples or providing complimentary services or addition of necessary fees
  > disclosed at the time of purchase, shall not be considered as basket sneaking.

  The Explanation defines necessary fees, e.g. delivery charges, gift wrapping and government taxes, "explicitly
  disclosed to the consumer at the time of purchase". Illustration (a) is "automatic addition of paid ancillary
  services with a pre-ticked box or otherwise to the cart".
- **Clause 7:** the CCPA's decision on interpretation is final.

## 4. Related, not used on screen
The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 (G.S.R. 789(E), in force 1 Jan 2027) require e-commerce
entities to comply with these guidelines, run a yearly self-audit and display a compliance certificate. That isn't in
force yet, so it is not narrated.
