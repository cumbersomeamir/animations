## Caption (English, matches the final voiceover)
Deposit gone, for wear and tear? You moved out. The landlord blames faded paint.

Tamil Nadu's tenancy law says: keep the flat as you got it, except normal wear and tear. Repainting? The landlord's
job, whatever the agreement says. Damage you cause, like a broken window, can be deducted. The rest is due when the
landlord takes the empty flat back.

Photograph every room, moving in and out. Get deductions in writing.
