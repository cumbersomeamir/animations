# Storyboard and timing: courtlens-30s-english20-01 (English, Tamil Nadu, India)

Batch 20. Follows the 11 Nov rules, the 15 Nov first-frame addendum and the **18 Nov feedback** ("too much information
at once, too much text, motion graphics can be more appealing, super cool thumbnail"). Built on the english-19 engine.

## How the 18 Nov feedback is applied
- **One central visual for the whole film:** a cut-away rented-flat diorama in one-point perspective (ceiling, side
  walls, floor, back wall with window and door, pendant lamp). A camera dollies and zooms through it. There are no
  separate "slides".
- **Less text:**
  - one short label per beat (NORMAL WEAR, LANDLORD'S JOB, DEDUCTED, AT HANDOVER, IN WRITING)
  - no sub-lines under the stamp
  - no law text on screen
  - captions are the only sentence-length text
- **Motion that explains:**
  - padlock rattle; keys drop and bounce
  - red marker rings draw on the stains
  - rewind to move-in day, then a day/night time-lapse that fades the walls
  - a paint-roller wipe repaints the wall
  - a cricket ball cracks the window; banknotes fly from the envelope to fix it while the amount rolls down
  - the envelope springs back; keys arc onto the peg
  - camera flashes; polaroids IN / OUT; a pen writes
- **Thumbnail = frame zero:**
  - the padlocked ₹45,000 deposit envelope in front of the worn flat
  - a red ring on the stain
  - giant "DEPOSIT GONE FOR WEAR & TEAR?" + series chip
  - voice at 0.05 s
