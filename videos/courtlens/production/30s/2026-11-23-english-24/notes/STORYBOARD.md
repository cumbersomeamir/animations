## How the feedback is applied
- **One tangible object transformation:** the rent coin multiplies ×1 → ×100 into a tall gold stack.
- **One condition per shot:** covered events (theft / fire / collapse icons); staff fraud (badge stamped FRAUD); the
  ×100 formula; acts of God ✕ (storm, flood, quake); no bank-sold insurance (policy pushed back ✕); payoff.
- **Text:**
  - captions only in y≈280–460
  - one short pill per beat: 100 × RENT, NOT LIABLE, NOT FROM THE BANK, OWN INSURANCE
  - props carry one word each (FRAUD, LOCKER INSURANCE)
- **Thumbnail = frame zero:**

    empty red velvet jewellery box in the foreground
  - "LOCKER ROBBED. WHO PAYS?" (4 words)
