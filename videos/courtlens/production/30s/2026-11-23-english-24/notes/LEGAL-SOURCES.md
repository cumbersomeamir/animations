## Source (official, current)
**Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Responsible Business Conduct) Directions, 2025**, RBI/2025-26/170,
DOR.MCS.REC.No.89/01-01-032/2025-26, 28 Nov 2025, "Updated as on October 1, 2026", section C (Safe deposit lockers).
- URL: https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13140
- Fetched 2026-11-23; saved HTML sha256 `bfcd0d0995725165bfa3f44016727872b777e53ad766a3e0c0f7d07b1af120c8`.
- Applicability (para 3): commercial banks, i.e. banking companies other than Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks and
  Local Area Banks, plus corresponding new banks and SBI. Other bank types have their own consolidated directions,
  which were not read.

### Paragraphs used (verbatim)
- **Para 256:** the bank shall have a Board-approved policy on its responsibility "for any loss or damage to the
  contents of the lockers due to its negligence as banks owe a separate duty of care … guarding against unauthorised
  access to the lockers and providing appropriate safeguards against theft and robbery."
- **Para 257 (C.8.2):**
  > A bank shall not be liable for any damage and / or loss of contents of locker arising from natural calamities or
  > acts of God like earthquake, floods, lightning and thunderstorm or any act that is attributable to the sole fault or
  > negligence of the customer. The bank shall, however, exercise appropriate care to its locker systems to protect its
  > premises from such catastrophes.
- **Para 258 (C.8.3):**
  > It has the responsibility to ensure that incidents like fire, theft / burglary / robbery, dacoity, building collapse
  > do not occur in the bank's premises due to its own shortcomings, negligence and by any act of omission /
  > commission. As the bank cannot claim that it bears no liability towards its customers for loss of contents of the
  > locker, in instances where loss of contents of locker is due to incidents mentioned above or attributable to fraud
  > committed by its employee(s), the bank's liability shall be for an amount equivalent to one hundred times the
  > prevailing annual rent of the safe deposit locker.
- **Para 260 (C.9.2):**
  > A bank shall clarify in its locker agreement that as it does not keep a record of the contents of the locker … it
  > would not be under any liability to insure the contents of the locker against any risk whatsoever. The bank shall
  > under no circumstances offer, directly or indirectly, any insurance product to its locker hirers for insurance of
  > locker contents.

## Scope notes (kept out of the narration)
- **The 100× figure** is the liability amount the Directions set for those events. The video does not claim that any
  particular claim will succeed, how it is processed, or any timeline.
- **Acts of God:** the bank must still "exercise appropriate care" to protect its premises (para 257). There is also no
  liability for the customer's sole negligence (not narrated).
- **Lender types:** Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, Local Area Banks and co-operative banks fall under separate
  directions (not read). The narration says "RBI's locker rules"; the scope is noted here.
- **Example numbers:** ₹4,000 yearly rent × 100 (= ₹4,00,000) is illustrative, not a real tariff.

## Fact classification
- **Regulatory facts:** paras 256–258 and 260, as quoted (read 2026-11-23, version updated 1 Oct 2026).
- **Illustrative:** the vault, the locker numbers, the jewellery box, the event icons, the staff badge, the coin stack,

- **Practical suggestion:** check your yearly rent; buy your own insurance for costly valuables.
