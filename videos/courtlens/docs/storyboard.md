# Courtlens — 30 s portrait storyboard (v1)

Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 900 frames. Style: editorial paper-cutout world with an
expressive stick-figure advocate (black coat + white bands). Palette is taken from the
verified store icon: deep navy, ivory/marble, gold, cyan AI glow, on warm paper.

Product claims are limited to the verified listing line: "Court records, case tracking and an
AI assistant for advocates." Any phone screen shown is a **stylized illustration** (labelled on
screen) because no verified screenshots are in the approved assets.
