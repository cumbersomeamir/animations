A tablespoon of honey or of sugar: which adds more calories?

Gram for gram, honey is lower. It's partly water: 304 calories per 100 grams, sugar 387. But spoons measure volume. Thick honey fills every gap; sugar crystals trap air. A tablespoon of honey weighs 21 grams; sugar, about 12 and a half. So per spoon: honey about 64 calories, sugar 48. Log the spoon you use.
