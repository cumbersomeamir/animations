What is inside 100 g of papaya?

USDA FoodData Central lists per 100 g of raw papaya flesh (seeds and skin removed):
• Energy: 43 kcal
• Fibre: 1.7 g
• Vitamin C: 61 mg
• Water: 88 g

Water has no calories, which is why papaya is light. Fibre aids digestion, and vitamin C helps your body absorb iron (MedlinePlus). A cup of 1-inch papaya cubes is about 145 g, or about 62 kcal.
