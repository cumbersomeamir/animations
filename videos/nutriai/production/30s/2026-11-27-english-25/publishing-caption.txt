Peeling an apple? Wait. What are you throwing away? 🍎

Let's compare, gram for gram. A hundred grams of apple with its skin has 2.4 g of fibre. The same hundred grams, peeled, has just 1.3. Much of the fibre sits in that thin skin, and fibre aids digestion, helping keep you regular. So peel a medium apple, and about half its fibre goes with the peel.
