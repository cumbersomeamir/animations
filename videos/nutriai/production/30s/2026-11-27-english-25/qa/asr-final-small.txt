nutriai-30s-english25-01.mp4 Peeling an apple, wait, what are you throwing away? Let's compare gram for gram. A hundred grams of apple with its skin has 2.4 grams of fiber. The same hundred grams peeled has just 1.3. Much of the fiber sits in that thin skin, and fiber aids digestion, helping keep you regular.
