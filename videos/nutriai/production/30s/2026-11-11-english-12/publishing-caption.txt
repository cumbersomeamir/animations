3 cups of popcorn: just 24 grams. So how does a kernel get so big?

Inside, a drop of water turns to steam, bursts the hull, and puffs the starch into a light foam. So 3 cups, air-popped: about 90 calories. Popped in oil, 165; with a tablespoon of butter, 265; a tablespoon of sugar, over 300. Same bowl, more than triple: count the grams and the toppings, not the size.
