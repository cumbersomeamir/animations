Rice, per 100 grams: 365 dry, 130 cooked. Did cooking burn calories off?

50 grams of dry rice soaks up about 90 grams of water, and weighs about 140. Water adds weight, not energy: still about 180 calories, spread over more grams. Weigh it cooked but log the dry number, and you'd count nearly 3 times too much. Match the label to the rice on your scale: dry with dry, cooked with cooked.
