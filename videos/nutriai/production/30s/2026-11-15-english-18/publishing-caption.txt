Jewels you can eat: what's really inside a passion fruit?

About half is shell. One fruit gives just 18 grams of pulp and seeds. Per 100 grams, that's 97 calories, so one fruit has about 17. The seeds are eaten too, and the pulp carries 10 grams of fibre per 100: four times an apple. So one fruit adds about 17 calories and 2 grams of fibre: log the pulp, not the shell.
