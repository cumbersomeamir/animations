Fuzzy brown outside, what's really inside a kiwi? A hundred grams of the green flesh has just 61 calories and three grams of fiber. Its standout is vitamin C, 93 milligrams, more than an orange's 53. One peeled kiwi is about 69 grams, about 42 calories, and about 70% of the daily value for vitamin C. Log the grams you eat. Track your food and nutrition with Cal Meter.
