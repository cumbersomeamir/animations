Fuzzy brown outside: what's really inside a kiwi?

A hundred grams of the green flesh has just 61 calories and 3 grams of fibre. Its standout is vitamin C: 93 milligrams, more than an orange's 53. One peeled kiwi is about 69 grams: about 42 calories, and about 70% of the daily value for vitamin C. Log the grams you eat.
