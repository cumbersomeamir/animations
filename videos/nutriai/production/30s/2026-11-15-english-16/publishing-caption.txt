Dark chocolate or milk chocolate: which has more calories?

Dark does: 598 per 100 grams, milk 535. Yet it has less than half the sugar: 24 grams, against 52. More cacao means more cocoa butter: 43 grams of fat, against 30. And fat has 9 calories a gram, sugar only 4. 30 grams each: about 180 versus 160. Log the grams, not the shade.
