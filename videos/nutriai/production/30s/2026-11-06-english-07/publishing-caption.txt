One large boiled egg: about 78 calories and 6 grams of protein.

Fry it with one measured teaspoon of oil, and that teaspoon adds up to 40 calories. The protein stays about 6 grams; the extra energy comes from the oil, an ingredient of its own. So measure the oil, and log it next to the egg instead of guessing.
