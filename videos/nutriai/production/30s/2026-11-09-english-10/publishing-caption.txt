100 grams of cooked chickpeas, 100 roasted: more than double the calories.

Cooked ones are 60% water; roasting drives most of it off. So 100 grams shrinks to under half, same calories, same protein. Per 100 grams, roasted has about 380 calories and 20 grams of protein. Snack tip: 30 grams roasted is about 70 grams cooked, around 115 calories.

Cooked = boiled, no salt; roasted = dry-roasted, no oil.
