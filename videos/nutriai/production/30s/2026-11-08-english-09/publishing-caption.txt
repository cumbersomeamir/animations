100 grams of avocado, 100 of cucumber: same weight, about 10 times the calories.

Avocado: about 160 calories, 15 grams of fat, 7 of fibre. Cucumber: about 15 calories, and 95% water. Fat packs about 9 calories a gram; water has none. Neither is bad: calorie for calorie, 100 grams of avocado equals about a kilo of cucumber.
