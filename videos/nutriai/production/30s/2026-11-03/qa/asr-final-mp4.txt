calMeter-30s-pomegranate-01.mp4 | What's inside 100 grams of pomegranate aerols, the juicy seeds? Set the peel and pale membranes aside, then weigh the aerols. That's about 83 calories, 4 grams of fiber and 16 micrograms of vitamin K. Fiber aids digestion, and vitamin K helps your blood clot. Bottled juice is different, it has almost no fiber. So weigh the edible portion, not the whole fruit. Track your food and nutrition with calmetre.
