What is inside 100 g of pomegranate arils?

USDA FoodData Central lists per 100 g of raw pomegranate arils (the juicy seeds; peel and membranes removed):
• Energy: 83 kcal
• Fibre: 4 g
• Vitamin K: 16 µg

Fibre aids digestion, and vitamin K helps your blood clot (MedlinePlus). Bottled pomegranate juice has only 0.1 g fibre per 100 g. Peel and membrane are about 44 % of a whole pomegranate's weight in USDA's data, so weigh the edible portion, not the whole fruit.
