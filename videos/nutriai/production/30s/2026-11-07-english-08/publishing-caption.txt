30 grams of grapes, 30 grams of raisins: same weight, very different calories.

Grapes are about 80% water; drying removes most of it. So 30 grams of grapes is about 21 calories, raisins about 90, with 4 times the sugar. Drying adds no sugar: those raisins started as about 130 grams of grapes. Same sugar, just far less water.
