Skim milk loses its fat. Does it lose its calcium too? 🥛

Let's skim a glass and see. The cream rises and is lifted off, but the calcium stays in the milk. A cup of whole milk has about 280 milligrams of calcium. Skim has about 300. Calcium helps build and maintain healthy bones and teeth. So either cup gives more than a fifth of a day's calcium.
