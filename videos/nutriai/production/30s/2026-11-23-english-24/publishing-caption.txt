A carrot's orange isn't just colour. What does it turn into? 🥕

Part of the answer is in your eyes. The orange is beta-carotene, and your body converts it into vitamin A. Vitamin A, in turn, makes pigments in your eyes that help you see in low light. A hundred grams of raw carrot gives more than 90% of a day's vitamin A. And one medium-sized carrot, about 60 grams, covers more than half.
