One coconut, two foods. Which part packs the calories? 🥥

Its water: about 40 calories. Its meat: about 1,400! Per 100 grams, coconut water is 95% water: just 19 calories. The meat is only half water, and a third of it is fat: 354. So one small piece, 45 grams, is about 160: as much as 3½ glasses of coconut water.
