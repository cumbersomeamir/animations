Green peas and edamame look like twins. Their numbers don't. So what's different inside?

Edamame are young soybeans, which store protein and oil; peas store mostly carbs. Per 100 grams, cooked: 12 grams of protein versus 5, 5 of fat versus almost none. 121 calories against 78. Snack tip: a cup of edamame brings about double the protein of peas, for about 60 more calories.
