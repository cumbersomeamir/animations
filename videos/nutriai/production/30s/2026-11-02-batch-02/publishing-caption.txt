What is inside 100 g of guava?

USDA FoodData Central lists per 100 g of raw common guava (edible flesh and seeds; USDA trims the skin):
• Energy: 68 kcal
• Fibre: 5.4 g
• Vitamin C: 228 mg

These are reference values; other guava types and individual fruits differ. Fibre aids digestion and vitamin C helps the body make collagen (MedlinePlus). Guava is a useful food, not a cure. One whole guava is about 55 g of edible fruit in USDA's data, so weigh the portion you actually eat.
