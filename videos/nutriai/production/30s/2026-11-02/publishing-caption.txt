What is actually in 100 g of mango?

Measured as raw edible flesh (skin and stone removed), USDA FoodData Central lists per 100 g:
• Energy: 60 kcal
• Carbohydrate: 15.0 g, including 1.6 g fibre
• Vitamin C: 36.4 mg

These are database averages; individual mangoes vary. Weigh the portion first, then read the nutrients.

Source: USDA FoodData Central, "Mangos, raw", FDC ID 169910 (SR Legacy), per 100 g edible portion
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/food-details/169910/nutrients
Refuse (seeds and skin): USDA National Nutrient Database SR28, NDB 09176
