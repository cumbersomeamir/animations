nutriai-30s-production-01.mp4 | What is inside 100 grams of mango? We mean raw edible flesh, with the skin and stone removed. That portion has about 60 calories, 15 grams of carbohydrate, and 1 .6 grams of fiber. It also contains about 36 milligrams of vitamin C. These are food database averages, not an exact reading of every mango. Start with the portion, then understand the nutrients.
