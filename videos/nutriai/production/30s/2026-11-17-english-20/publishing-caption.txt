Pistachios in the shell: how much do you really eat? 🥜

Let's crack open 100 grams. 47 grams of that is shell. Just 53 grams of kernels. Per 100 grams of kernels: about 570 calories, 46 grams of fat, 21 of protein, and 10 of fibre. So 100 grams in the shell is about 300 calories, not 570. A handful of 49 kernels: about 160.
