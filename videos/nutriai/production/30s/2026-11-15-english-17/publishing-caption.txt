Huge watermelon wedge, tiny raisins: which has more calories?

Neither: both are about 85. Yet the wedge holds 286 grams of flesh; 60 raisins, just 28. Watermelon is 91% water, and water has no calories. Per 100 grams: raisins, 299 calories; watermelon, 30. So a small handful of raisins counts like a whole wedge: log grams, not size.
