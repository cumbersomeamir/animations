Same 30 grams: peanuts or peanut butter. Which has more calories?

Almost a tie, because peanuts are already about half fat by weight. The jar adds sugar, hydrogenated oil and salt; sodium jumps from 2 milligrams to about 140. 30 grams is about two level tablespoons. A heaped spoon holds more, so weigh one once.
