Sweet, sure. But what's really inside a pineapple? 🍍

Skip the crown, peel and core, and step inside. First, a fibre path: 1.4 grams per 100 grams, and fibre aids digestion. Next, the star: 48 milligrams of vitamin C. Your body uses it to make collagen for skin and blood vessels, and to absorb iron. One cup of chunks: about 80 milligrams, nearly a full day's vitamin C.
