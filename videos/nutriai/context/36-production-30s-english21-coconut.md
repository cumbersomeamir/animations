## Design (same approved full-frame style and 2026-11-18 feedback as english-20; shared engine copied unchanged)
1. Hook (coral; frame 0 = thumbnail): a cracked coconut character (water-filled half with eyes and smile), its lid flying off, water splashing, palm fronds in the foreground; "WATER OR / MEAT?".
2. Whole coconut (violet): the coconut cracks; its water pours into a glass on a scale (206 g, ≈ 40 kcal); its meat chunks fly into a bowl on a second scale (397 g, ≈ 1,400 kcal).
3. Water per 100 g (sky): a glass with a straw and a face fills a scale to 100 g; a bracket marks 95 % water above a thin sugar band; JUST 19 kcal.
4. Meat per 100 g (green): a meat chunk with a face lands on a scale (100 g); it squeezes out water drops into one beaker (½ WATER) and fat drops into another (⅓ FAT); 354 kcal.
5. Portion (pink): a calorie balance (pivot labelled kcal): a 45 g piece tips it (≈ 160 kcal); glasses land one by one until it levels at 3½; the coconut peeks in and winks; CalMeter seal + white wordmark.

## Facts
- Measured (USDA SR Legacy): coconut water FDC 170174: 19 kcal, 95.0 g water / 100 g; 1 cup = 240 g; "coconut yields" 206 g. Coconut meat, raw FDC 170169: 354 kcal, 47.0 g water, 33.5 g fat / 100 g; medium = 397 g; piece 2"×2"×½" = 45 g.
- Calculated: per coconut ≈ 39 kcal (water) vs ≈ 1 405 kcal (meat); meat 18.6× the energy density; piece ≈ 159 kcal = 3.49 glasses (45.6 kcal each); fat supplies ≈ 79 % of the meat's energy (not narrated).
