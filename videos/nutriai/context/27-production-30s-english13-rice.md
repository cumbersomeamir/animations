## Facts (measured / calculated / assumption)
- Measured (USDA SR Legacy): long-grain white rice raw FDC 169756: 365 kcal, 11.6 g water / 100 g; cooked without salt FDC 169757: 130 kcal, 68.4 g water / 100 g.
- Calculated: non-water matter 88.4 vs 31.6 g / 100 g → × 2.80; 50 g dry → ≈ 140 g cooked (+ ≈ 90 g water); ≈ 182 kcal both; energy per g of non-water matter 4.13 vs 4.11 kcal; dry label on 140 g cooked ≈ 511 kcal (2.8×).
- Assumption: plain rice boiled in water, cooked to USDA's water content, no solids lost; real yields vary (shown with "≈").
