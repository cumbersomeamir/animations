## Facts
- Measured (USDA SR Legacy): watermelon, raw FDC 167765: 30 kcal, 91.4 g water / 100 g; wedge (1/16 melon) 286 g edible flesh (refuse 48 %: rind, seeds, cutting loss). Raisins, dark, seedless FDC 168165: 299 kcal, 15.5 g water / 100 g; 1 oz = 28.35 g = 60 raisins.
- Calculated: wedge 85.8 kcal; ounce of raisins 84.8 kcal; weight ratio 10.1×; energy density ratio 9.97×; 261 g of water in the wedge.
- Basis: water is not an energy-yielding component (FAO FNP 77).
