## Facts
- Measured (USDA SR Legacy): dark chocolate 70–85 % cacao solids FDC 170273: 598 kcal, 42.6 g fat, 24.0 g sugars / 100 g. Milk chocolate FDC 167587: 535 kcal, 29.7 g fat, 51.5 g sugars / 100 g. Other dark bands (context): 45–59 % 546 kcal, 60–69 % 579 kcal.
- Regulation: 21 CFR 163.111, chocolate liquor (ground cacao nibs) has 50–60 % cacao fat.
- Standard factors (FAO FNP 77): fat 9 kcal/g, carbohydrate and protein 4 kcal/g.
- Calculated: +12.9 g fat × 9 − 13.5 g carbohydrate × 4 ≈ +63 kcal = USDA gap; 30 g: 179.4 vs 160.5 kcal.
