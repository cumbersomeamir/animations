# 08 — Educational strategy (active direction from 2026-11-02)

Supersedes campaign-style requirements for educational pilots. Earlier work is preserved: 30 s campaign WIP (`source/`, 05-progress), 15 s shader test checkpoint (`tests/15s/`, 06/07).

## Goal
Grow a niche audience with genuinely useful short lessons. App posts come occasionally and later. Every episode must teach one thing a viewer can use without ever downloading anything.

## Niche
Ingredient-by-ingredient calories and nutrient education: "what is in 100 g of X (edible portion)", measured, sourced, rounded honestly.

## Format rules (every episode)
- 15 s vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps). One concept only. Structure: question / physical puzzle (0–3 s) → mechanism demonstrated by the performer (3–12 s) → concise takeaway (12–15 s).
- Values from ONE authoritative database entry (default: USDA FoodData Central), with its exact preparation (raw/cooked) and portion basis (per 100 g edible portion). Cite it on screen in small type and in `SOURCES.md` with access date and a claim-to-source table. No health-cure claims, no invented values, never confuse whole-item weight with the edible portion.
- Recurring performer: **Gram**, a living 100 g calibration weight (see `education/pilot-01/character/`). Gram balances the measured food against himself, so every lesson starts from a physically measured 100 g. Gram performs: weighs, cuts, sorts, stacks, stamps, reacts. He never stands beside text.
- Look: dimensional dark stage (near-black + icon leaf greens), realistic appetising food hero in its natural colours, labelled nutrient layers in true proportion, measured portions with household equivalents.
- Sound: Gram's original synthetic voice (Piper LJ Speech voice, public-domain dataset, pitch/formant-shifted), original synthesized music + action-timed SFX.
- Branding: a small unobtrusive "NutriAI" signature only. No feature tour, no forced store CTA, no slogans. Never imply the scene is app UI or experiment data.
- Change situation, camera and choreography every episode. Keep Gram's design, palette, voice and the balance mechanic consistent.
