## Facts
- Measured (USDA SR Legacy, per 100 g, both cooked from frozen, boiled, no salt): edamame FDC 168411 121 kcal, 11.9 g protein, 5.2 g fat, 72.8 g water; green peas FDC 170017 78 kcal, 5.15 g protein, 0.27 g fat, 79.5 g water. Mature seeds: soybeans 36.5 g protein / 19.9 g fat (FDC 174270); split peas 61.6 g carbohydrate / 3.9 g fat (FDC 172428).
- Calculated: one cup edamame (155 g) ≈ 188 kcal, 18.4 g protein; one cup peas (160 g) ≈ 125 kcal, 8.2 g protein; 2.24× protein, + 63 kcal.
- Reliable definition: edamame = soybeans harvested immature (SDSU Extension; ATTRA/NCAT).
