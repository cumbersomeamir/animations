## Hindi voice: what was available and what was used
- Checked installed TTS: kokoro-js 1.2.1 bundles Kokoro-82M v1.0 **Hindi** voices (hf_alpha, hf_beta, hm_omega, hm_psi; Apache-2.0). Its JS phonemizer contains only English espeak-ng data (`phonemize('…','hi')` → "Invalid language identifier"), so it cannot phonemize Hindi alone.
- Solution, all local and free: Kokoro's own Hindi G2P route. **misaki 0.7.4 `EspeakG2P(language='hi')`** on espeak-ng (espeakng-loader 0.2.4, phonemizer-fork 3.3.2) in an isolated venv (`requirements-g2p.txt`, `src/g2p.py`). Its IPA feeds the same cached Kokoro ONNX model through `generate_from_ids` (`src/tts.mjs`). Every IPA symbol was checked against Kokoro's tokenizer vocab (`missing_ipa: []`).
- **Voice:** blend **0.7 hf_alpha + 0.3 hm_omega**. This is the Hindi analogue of the accepted female-led 0.7/0.3 blend; no cloning, no celebrity. Speed is **1.04**, with **0.98** on the three number lines for clarity.
- Candidates compared by local Whisper-small transcription: hf_alpha, hf_beta, and the alpha/omega blend. The blend gave the clearest consonants (बड़ा, देखिए, लीजिए).
