## Skill/recipe adjustments to reuse in future Hindi episodes
- Budget roughly **68–70 Hindi words** for 30 s at speed 1.04 with numbers at 0.98; measure with `node src/tts.mjs` and trim before building scenes.
- Avoid ड़/ढ़-heavy words and sentence-final bare numbers. Add "रुपये" after a final amount; respell "डाउनलोड" as "डाउन लोड" in `speak` (G2P only).
- Run Whisper large-v3-turbo (q8, cached) on per-line WAVs before rendering, and once on the final MP4 audio. Whisper-small is unreliable on Hindi numbers.
- Keep falling or dropping elements' start positions below the caption band (y > 600). Never use the "✓" glyph (Hind lacks it); use models.check.
