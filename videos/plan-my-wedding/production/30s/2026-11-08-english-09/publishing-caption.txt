Beautiful vows, and the back rows heard nothing.
This made-up sound quote lists two speakers and a playlist. No wireless mic, no backup power, and nobody named to run it.
So before you book, confirm three things in writing:
1. Microphones: how many, which are wireless, and spare batteries.
2. Power: where it plugs in, and what backs it up if it cuts out.
3. Operator: who stays to run it from the vows to the last speech.
