200 photos delivered isn't 200 edited photos.
In this made-up quote, the fine print says 50 are fully retouched; the rest get basic colour correction. Delivery takes 12 weeks, raw files cost extra, and use is personal only.
So before booking, ask three questions:
1. How many are fully edited?
2. When will they arrive?
3. Which files and usage rights are included, in writing?
