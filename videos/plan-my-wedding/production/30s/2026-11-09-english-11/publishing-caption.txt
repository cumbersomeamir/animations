The budget adds up. The due dates don't.
In this made-up plan, the venue, caterer and photographer each want a deposit, then a balance. The deposits are spread out, but all three balances land in the same month, just before the wedding.
So before you sign, list every payment with its due date, and start saving early for that heavy month. Ask what's refundable if plans change, and get the whole schedule in writing.
