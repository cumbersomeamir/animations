## Simplifications (disclosed)
- The scene is an **illustration**, not experiment data. Scales of Kip, tank and hull are illustrative; the
  hull's wall is thin clay-like steel, and the morph is a storytelling device (real steel is not hand-shaped).
- The ball's forces are shown in a "rewind + pause" moment while fully submerged, so only weight and the water's
  push are drawn (no floor contact force, no drag). The pause is clearly signalled (pause glyph, frozen water).
- Density ratio uses OpenStax's rounded 7.8 (real steels vary slightly).
- The flag Kip plants is treated as negligible mass.
