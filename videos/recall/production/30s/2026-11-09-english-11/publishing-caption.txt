Why is the food steaming but the plate still cool?

Microwaves tug the water molecules inside food back and forth, and that makes heat. Glass and microwave-safe ceramics barely absorb them; the hot food warms the plate. But water soaked into porous clay or an iron-rich glaze can make pottery heat itself. To test a dish, heat it empty beside a cup of water for one minute. If it stays cool, it's safe.
