The elevator starts up, and the scale jumps from 75 to 84 kilos.

Did you gain nine kilos? No. A scale reads how hard it pushes up on you. To speed you up, it must push harder than gravity pulls. At steady speed, the push matches gravity again. Slowing at the top, it pushes less, so you read lighter. Your mass never changed. Only the push did, while your speed was changing.
