A mirror for heat.

That's the shiny wall inside a thermos. Why does it need one? Heat escapes three ways: through solids, through air, and as infrared light. A thermos has two walls with a vacuum between them. With almost no air there, heat can barely conduct or flow across. But infrared crosses a vacuum, like sunlight through space. So the walls are shiny, bouncing it back. That leaves the top as the weak spot, so keep the lid on.
