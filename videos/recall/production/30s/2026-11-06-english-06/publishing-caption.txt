The dot is right, so why is the arrow wrong next to a speaker?

GPS places the dot. The arrow comes from a tiny compass sensor that feels Earth's magnetic field. That field is weak. Up close, a speaker's magnet or a steel desk can bend it, and the arrow swings. Step away from magnets and steel, then calibrate by waving a figure eight.
