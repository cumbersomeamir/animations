How does it see smoke?

Burnt toast, and it screams. But what's in that plastic box? Many alarms hide a small chamber with a tiny light. The light aims away from the sensor, so normally it sees nothing. When smoke drifts in, particles scatter the light, and some hits the sensor. Enough light, and the alarm sounds. Thick steam can set it off too, even with no fire. So test yours once a month, using the test button.
