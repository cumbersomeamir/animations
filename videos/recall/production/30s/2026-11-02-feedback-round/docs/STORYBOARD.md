# Storyboard — "Why can a QR code still scan with a logo in the middle?"

The style is Low-poly Everyday Discovery (context/15). There are 17 shots with hard cuts in a 30.000 s portrait frame at 30 fps. Three locations alternate:
1. the café, with the person's problem
2. the phone screen, a readable physical display seen in POV inserts
3. the giant 3D QR, the mechanism

Every shot has camera motion. Captions sit at y 340–520. Labels stay in the centre region (x 110–970, y 600–1440).
