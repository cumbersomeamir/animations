That hole is on purpose.

It decides which layer holds the cabin in. Up high, cabin air pushes out much harder than the thin air outside. The thick outer layer takes that push. The hole lets cabin air into the gap behind the middle layer. Equal pressure on both sides means that layer carries no load. It's the backup. See fog between the layers? That's moist cabin air that came in through the hole.
