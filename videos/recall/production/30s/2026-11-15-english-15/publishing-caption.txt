Where's this water from?

It isn't leaking: the drink is orange, but the drops are clear. The air around you contains invisible water vapor. Near the icy glass, that air cools below its dew point. Vapor molecules then stick to the cold glass faster than they escape, forming drops. More humid air means more vapor, so muggy days mean a sweatier glass. An insulated cup stays dry: its outer wall doesn't get that cold.
