That is my voice?

Your friends hear this voice every day. You hear yourself two ways. Through the air, from your mouth to your ears. And through bone: your skull carries the vibrations to your inner ears. That inner path changes the sound, and only you get it. A microphone only catches the air path, like everyone else's ears. Plug your ears and hum. It gets louder: that's the bone path.
