This straw isn't broken.

So why does it snap at the water's surface? Light from the straw speeds up as it leaves the water, and changes direction. Like a mower rolling from grass to pavement: one side speeds up first. Your brain traces the light back in a straight line, so the underwater part looks higher. That's why a pool looks shallower from above: about three quarters of its real depth.
