Same slider, opposite jobs.

Pull it one way, the teeth lock. Pull it back, they open. How? Inside, the slider's channel is shaped like a Y. Closing, its slanted walls squeeze both rows together. Each tooth has a bump and a hollow, so each bump nests in the next hollow. Nested, they hardly pull apart sideways. Opening, a wedge between the arms splits them apart. And many sliders hold still until you pull the tab.
