Why does your phone ignore you once you put on gloves?

Your phone's screen is capacitive: it senses the electric charge of your fingertip. Ordinary knit gloves don't conduct, so they isolate your finger and nothing registers. Touchscreen gloves weave conductive thread into the fingertips to carry your touch through. Resistive screens respond to pressure instead, so a gloved finger or plain stylus works.
