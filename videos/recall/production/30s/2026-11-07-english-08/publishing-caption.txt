Your eyes can't see this remote's light. So why can your phone camera?

Remotes blink infrared, light just past the red end your eyes detect, in a quick code the TV reads. Many camera sensors respond to that infrared, so the blinks show up on screen. Remote not working? Aim it at the camera and press a button. No flicker? Try new batteries, or another camera.
