Leave the fridge open on a hot day, and the kitchen gets warmer.

So how can a cold box heat a room? A fridge doesn't make cold. It pumps heat. Cold fluid in the inside coils evaporates, soaking up your food's heat. A compressor squeezes it hotter than the room, and coils outside the box release that heat. Door open, that heat just circles back, plus the motor's own. That's why air conditioners dump their heat outdoors.
