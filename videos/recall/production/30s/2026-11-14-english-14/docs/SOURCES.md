## Verbatim quotes
- **S1:**
  - "Heat pumps, air conditioners, and refrigerators utilize heat transfer from cold to hot. They are heat engines run backward."
  - "Thus the heat transfer to the hot reservoir is Q_h = Q_c + W"
  - "The mission of air conditioners and refrigerators is for heat transfer Q_c to occur from a cool environment, such as chilling a room or keeping food at lower temperatures than the environment."
  - "A working fluid such as a non-CFC refrigerant is used. In the outdoor coils (the evaporator), heat transfer Q_c occurs to the working fluid from the cold outdoor air, turning it into a gas." (heat-pump heating mode; in a fridge the evaporator is inside)
  - "The electrically driven compressor (4) increases the temperature and pressure of the gas and forces it into the condenser coils (1) inside the heated space. Because the temperature of the gas is higher than the temperature inside the room, heat transfer to the room occurs and the gas condenses to a liquid."
  - "(In a cooling cycle, the evaporator and condenser coils exchange roles and the flow direction of the fluid is reversed.)"
  - "What is considered the benefit in a heat pump is considered waste heat in a refrigerator."
- **S2:**
  - "A fridge can be though of as a "heat pump". It takes air inside the fridge and cools it by removing heat from it." (sic)
  - "This heat has to go somewhere, though, and is usually blown out the back of the fridge by a little fan."
  - "…so even if you open the fridge door to let the cool air escape the fridge will still be heating the room more than cooling it."
  - "The average room temperature in the room will rise."
- **S3:**
  - "A refrigerator is an open system that dispels heat from a closed space to a warmer area, usually a kitchen or another room."
  - "…which compresses a coolant, increasing its temperature above the room's temperature. Heat flows from this coolant to the air in the room"
  - "The coolant expands, and it cools down below the temperature inside the refrigerator. Heat flows from the refrigerator to the coolant"
  - "This equation shows that a refrigerator must exhaust more heat to the room than it removes from the inside."
- **S4:**
  - "An air conditioner is a system that is used to cool down a space by removing heat from the space and moving it to some outside area."
  - "Refrigerant then passes through the condenser coils, transferring heat from the refrigerant to the outside air."
  - "…there is an outdoor unit (the condenser) and an indoor unit (the evaporator)."
