Sunglasses on, and your phone goes black. Turn it, and it's back. Why?

Many screens give off light that vibrates one way, set by a filter. Polarized sunglasses filter one direction too, cutting glare off water and roads. When the filters cross, the light is blocked; turn the phone, and it passes. So just rotate the phone. And if it darkens, your sunglasses are polarized.
