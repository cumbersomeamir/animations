Why does your phone rush to 80% then crawl to 100%?

At first, the charger pushes a big, steady current into the battery. Near full, it holds the voltage steady and lets the current shrink, like pouring slower near the top of a glass. That gentler finish helps the battery last. Keep it cool, too: when it's too warm, charging past 80% can pause.
