Which is heavier: a kilo of steel or a kilo of feathers?

Asked properly in terms of mass, it's a tie: one kilogram is one kilogram. The feathers just take up far more space, because a pile of feathers (air gaps included) has a much lower density than steel. Flip the question to two boxes of the same size and the steel box has far more mass. The trick is to notice what the question keeps equal: mass or volume.
