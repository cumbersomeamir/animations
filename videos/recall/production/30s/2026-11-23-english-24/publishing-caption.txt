This window is a metal net.

Light gets through. So why don't the microwaves? Microwaves are waves about twelve centimeters long. The holes are only about a millimeter wide, over a hundred times smaller. To waves that long, the net is like solid metal, so they bounce back inside. Light waves are about two thousand times smaller than the holes, so they slip through. So if the door is bent, or won't close firmly, stop using it.
