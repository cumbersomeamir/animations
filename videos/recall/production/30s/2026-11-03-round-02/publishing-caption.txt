Why does a microwave leave cold patches in food?

Inside the oven, microwaves reflect off the metal walls and form a standing-wave pattern, with spots where food heats strongly and spots where it barely heats. The turntable moves food through that pattern for somewhat more even heating. In thick food, the outer layers are heated mainly by the microwaves, while the middle heats mostly as warmth spreads in from those hot outer layers. So stir or rotate food midway, arrange it evenly, and give it standing time so the heat can even out. Food safety guidance also recommends checking the temperature in several places with a food thermometer.
