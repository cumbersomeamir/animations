This drop is floating.

Make the pan hotter, and it lasts longer, not shorter. On a warm pan, water touches the metal and sizzles away in seconds. But on a very hot pan, the underside flashes into steam. That steam cushion holds it up, and steam carries heat poorly, so the drop skates instead of boiling. Kitchen test on an empty pan: sizzling means not yet; skating beads mean ready to sear.
