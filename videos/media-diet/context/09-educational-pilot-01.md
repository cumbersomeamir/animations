## Brief
- Topic narrowed to ONE concept: *a message is not beamed straight to your friend's phone; it travels as
  addressed packets that routers hand along hop by hop, usually via the messaging app's servers.*
- Not covered on purpose (would not fit 15 s honestly): TCP/IP layering, DNS, encryption internals, cellular
  vs Wi-Fi details, packet loss/reordering, why apps use servers. Encryption is mentioned only in the on-screen
  disclaimer: "Simplified diagram. Real routes, storage and encryption vary by service."
- Accuracy limits honoured: no claim that all services are peer-to-peer or all server-relayed ("usually");
  no claim about which intermediaries can or cannot read contents; hop count (5) is illustrative.
- App presence: 30 px "Media Diet" signature with icon, last 2.4 s only. No CTA.
