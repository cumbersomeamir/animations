These rules are approved by the user and apply to every Media Diet educational app-channel episode from 11 Nov 2026 on. Where they
conflict with older notes (e.g. the old caption band y 340–520), THESE rules win. Keep the established Layered Tech Cutaway style,

## Process
- Work in this app's existing Claude Code conversation, its mapped account, repo cumbersomeamir/animations, isolated branch
  claude-video/media-diet. Read the approved context, style skill and previous topics first. Never borrow another app's visuals,
  voice, CTA or assets. Never overwrite earlier outputs. Educational app-channel episodes are separate from any Discover short film.
- Deliver an actual 30.000 s 1080×1920 H.264/yuv420p MP4 with AAC, English voiceover + English captions (no Hindi), established
  narrator, no Blender or paid APIs, existing approved motion skill and assets.
- Review previous episodes to avoid repeating a topic; if a proposed topic was covered, pick a genuinely different one meeting the
  same story criteria. If a fresh-news claim cannot be verified, pick another verified story — never present rumour as news.
- Record the selected exact topic and sources in project notes BEFORE rendering.

## Theory of Marketing (story structure)
thumbnail → hook → optional super hook → build-up + proof → payoff → CTA.
- Hook answers "why should I care"; build-up sustains ONE specific question; proof makes the answer credible; payoff gives useful
  knowledge; CTA gives a relevant next step.
- Suspense must come from a real unanswered question, never from false claims. Do not repeat the answer in every scene; no padding
  with generic statements.

## Pacing (adapt naturally, deliver 30 s)
0–3 s immediate striking image + hook · 3–5 s optional escalation · 5–21 s visual explanation and proof, with scene changes ·
21–27 s resolve the question with a concrete takeaway · 27–30 s concise app-relevant CTA.

## Thumbnail
A separate attractive vertical thumbnail (1080×1920) tied to the hook, with 2–4 large words.

## Visual rules
- One visual idea at a time; no paragraph cards. On-screen labels large and brief; the voice explains the detail.
- Captions readable within **x = 100..900, y = 230..1500**, avoiding platform overlays.
- Never show caption, diagram labels and an explanatory paragraph competing at the same time.
- Reveal diagrams sequentially; deliberate depth, lighting and transitions; no decorative distracting lines; meaningful motion that
  explains the topic.

## Facts
- Verify factual claims from primary/reliable sources before the final script. Distinguish measured facts, estimates and
  illustrative assumptions. Sources go in project notes, not on screen clutter.
- Never invent prices, financial returns, news, app functionality or scientific certainty.
- No boilerplate warning/disclaimer narration; omit risky procedures rather than turning the story into warnings. Keep essential
  legal qualifications concise and accurate.
- For news: verify event date, official evidence and availability; explicitly distinguish announcement, prototype and shipped
  product. Reject vague trend roundups and fabricated flying-car claims.

## Quality benchmark (feedback on recent news episodes)
Recent news picks felt weak/random. Benchmark = the Chicago Bean episode (context/25): a clear question, physical stakes and a
first-principles explanation. Hook poses a specific "how can that work?" question over actual authorised footage or a faithful
editorial reconstruction; build the mechanism, proof and the key engineering constraint; the payoff resolves the opening question.

## Delivery + QA
- Only completed MP4s go in the user's review folder (when downloaded); production source, citations, prompts and QA stay outside it.
- Render; full-decode; check duration/codecs; inspect transitions/frames; check English narration, caption sync, clipping and
  missing assets. Attach the finished MP4 and thumbnail; report limitations honestly; never claim a watched/listened check that was
  not performed (this container cannot play audio — use ASR + stem levels and say so).
