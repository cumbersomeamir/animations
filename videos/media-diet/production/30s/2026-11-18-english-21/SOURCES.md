## Primary
1. Quaise Energy press release, "Quaise Energy Signs 50 MW Geothermal Power Purchase Agreement with Google", **17 Nov 2026** —
   https://www.quaise.com/news/quaise-energy-signs-50-mw-geothermal-power-purchase-agreement-with-google :
   Google signed a power purchase agreement for 50 MW of superhot geothermal power from Project Obsidian (Central Oregon), to support
   Google's data center operations in The Dalles, Ore., "beginning as early as 2030"; "always-on, carbon-free energy"; "The initial phase of
   development will use conventional drilling technology"; "The second phase … is expected to be the first in the world to use millimeter
   wave drilling"; superhot geothermal "up to 10x more clean power per well than traditional geothermal systems" (company estimate).
2. Quaise Energy, GRC 2026 release (Sept 2026; conference 20–23 Sept, Houston) —
   https://www.quaise.com/news/quaise-energy-makes-news-at-us-geothermal-conference-with-announcement-of-up-to-25-million-us-funding-talk-on-companys-progress-to-date-and-awards-to-two-employees :
   oil-and-gas drills "can't withstand the formidable temperatures and pressures found that far down"; millimeter waves "can literally melt
   and vaporize rock"; Obsidian's first well to about 15,000 ft (~4.6 km), rock up to ~400 °C.

## Secondary (dated)
- New Atlas, David Szondy, **17 Nov 2026** — https://newatlas.com/energy/google-quaise-rock-vaporizing-geothermal-tech-data-center-power/ :
  the gyrotron is "a high-power vacuum tube … adapted by researchers at MIT to heat plasma in nuclear fusion reactors. It generates
  high-frequency millimeter-wave radiation that heats molecules inside crystalline rock until it melts and vaporizes, allowing a purge gas
  system to clear the hole without a physical drill bit touching the rock"; conventional rigs struggle past ~200–260 °C (3–5 km) where heat
  "destroys drill bits, seals, and downhole electronics"; 374 °C pushes water supercritical.
- New Atlas, **10 May 2026** HQ/field tour (author not recorded; photos credited "JS @ New Atlas") — https://newatlas.com/energy/quaise-newberry-volcano-marble-falls-hq-tour/ :
  105 GHz millimeter waves; 100 kW gyrotron on a mobile unit; "a geothermal gradient of about 77 °F per mile (25 °C per km)"; record
  millimeter-wave borehole depth reported then as 118 m (later company statements describe progress toward ~1 km; **not used in narration**
  because the current figure could not be verified).
- Interesting Engineering, 18 Nov 2026 (Tesla Powercore, candidate B) —
  https://interestingengineering.com/energy/tesla-powercore-solid-state-power-platform
