Planning a meetup, but one friend isn't on WhatsApp? On November 5, WhatsApp updated Events. Create an event, add a photo, and share one link with everyone. Your friend opens it and can RSVP without downloading the app. Replies land in the new Events Home, with reminders.

One limit: guests without WhatsApp see the address only after replying. It's rolling out to everyone over the coming weeks.
