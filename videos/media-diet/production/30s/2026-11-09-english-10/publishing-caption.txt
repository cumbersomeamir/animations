Drowning in school emails about your kids' events? On November 6, Meta added spaces to its Muse app. Before, you dug through every email and copied each date by hand. Now, describe a school dashboard: Muse builds it and adds those events to your calendar. Connect your apps, and it keeps itself up to date in the background.

It's live wherever Muse is available, but you can't share spaces yet.
