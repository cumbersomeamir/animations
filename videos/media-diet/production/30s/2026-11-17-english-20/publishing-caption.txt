24 hours to say hello. On November 18th, Voyager 1 hits one light-day from Earth. So how do we still hear its fridge-bulb radio? Radio spreads out like light: twice as far, a quarter of the power. By Earth, less than a billion-billionth of a watt arrives. Its dish narrows the beam, and 70-meter dishes catch it. The catch: its nuclear power drops about 4 watts every year. Out there, bigger ears beat a louder voice.
