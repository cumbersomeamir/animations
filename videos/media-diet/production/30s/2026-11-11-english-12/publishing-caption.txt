An electric car just broke 500 km/h. Why is 500 so hard? Air. Drag grows with speed squared. Power to beat it grows with speed cubed. 400 to 500 km/h? Nearly double the power. Its answer: four motors, 2.2 megawatts. The catch is heat, so temperatures were set before every run. 1% faster costs about 3% more power. And it was one way, not a two-way average.
