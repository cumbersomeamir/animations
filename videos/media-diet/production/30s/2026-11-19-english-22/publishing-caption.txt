Five minutes. What's the catch? BYD's new SUV claims 10 to 70 percent in five minutes. Why not just pour in a megawatt? But in a demo, the last 27 percent took almost four minutes. Lithium ions must squeeze into the anode; rushed, they plate as metal. And heat grows with current squared. So the battery sets the pace, slowing when full or cold. Five-minute claims mean the middle, not empty to full.
