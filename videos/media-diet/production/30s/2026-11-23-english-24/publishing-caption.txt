The safety net spread the crash. Microsoft just explained how Outlook went down worldwide. A hidden bug froze servers that got too busy. When one froze, its traffic shifted to the others. The neighbors overloaded, froze, and passed it on. In two hours, over 80 percent of North American servers froze. Then traffic spilled into every other region. Rerouting needs spare room, so Microsoft plans walled-off server pools.
