## Schematic / creative elements (not claims)
Paper valley, ridge, tower, houses, phone prop and its screen glyphs, satellite cut-out, dish hut, response-centre
building, route dashes, tent, backpack, signpost and all distances/sizes are illustrative and not to scale. The satellite
is drawn gliding slowly along an arc ("orbiting"): Apple's network uses low-earth-orbit satellites that move (A2, A3);
Google's page says its satellites are "in fixed positions above the earth" (G1) — the film names neither and shows "a
satellite". Cell-tower range is shown only as a small relative circle. India is not on Apple's (A1) or Google's (G1)
lists as of 2026-11-03; the film makes no country-specific claim (noted in the publishing caption only).
