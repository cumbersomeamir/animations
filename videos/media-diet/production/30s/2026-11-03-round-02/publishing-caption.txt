No mobile signal, but you need help? On supported phones and in supported countries, an emergency text can go via satellite.

Your phone sends a short text up to a satellite, which passes it down to a ground station and on to emergency services or a relay centre. The satellite is far away, so you need a clear view of the sky and replies take longer than on a normal mobile network. Try your phone's satellite demo before you go off-grid.

Availability depends on phone model, country and network. Apple's and Google's official availability lists (checked 3 Nov 2026) do not include India.
