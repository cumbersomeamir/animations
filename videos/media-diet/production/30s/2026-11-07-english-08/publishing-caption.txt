Can a photo of a recipe fill your shopping cart? Since November 6, some Amazon shoppers can text Alexa for Shopping. Before: read the recipe, search each ingredient, one by one. Now: text a photo of the recipe, and the ingredients land in your cart. One text replaces all those searches.

The catch: it's invite-only in the United States, expanding over the coming weeks.
