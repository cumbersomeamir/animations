# Facts file — claim → source → quoted wording (Hindi episode hindi-03, checked 2026-11-03)

News item: **Apple iOS 27.2 (released 26 Oct 2026)** adds support for **Google's SynthID** invisible watermark to images
made or changed with Apple's generative tools in Photos and Image Playground; Google says its **SynthID Detector** (global,
English) can check content from partners including Apple. Status: **released** (not an announcement).
