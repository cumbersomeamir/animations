What does airplane mode actually switch off?

It always cuts the cellular radio. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth may go off too, but depending on your phone and settings you can often turn each back on separately. That is why wireless headphones or an airline's Wi-Fi can still work, but only if the airline allows it. Phones also remember your last choice, so check which connections are actually on, and follow the crew's instructions.
