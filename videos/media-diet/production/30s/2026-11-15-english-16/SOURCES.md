## Energy physics
- ICCT, 14 Jul 2022 — https://theicct.org/aviation-global-expecting-electric-jul22/ : best-in-class lithium-ion packs ≈ 250 Wh/kg;
  fossil jet fuel ≈ 12,000 Wh/kg, "nearly 50 times higher"; short-hop commuter flights are where electric aircraft fit first.
- NASA (NTRS 20230017408, EATS 2024 all-electric study) — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20230017408/downloads/EATS_2024_all_electric_150_pax_v10.pdf :
  "electric propulsion is roughly twice as efficient in comparison to the thermally driven counterparts".
