How do noise cancelling headphones create quiet?

Two things work together. Microphones pick up the sound around you and the electronics play an opposing "anti-noise" signal, which reduces part of the noise, especially steady, low rumbles like an engine. Voices and sudden, sharp sounds are harder to cancel. At the same time, the ear cushions and the seal block sound physically, so a snug fit helps both.
