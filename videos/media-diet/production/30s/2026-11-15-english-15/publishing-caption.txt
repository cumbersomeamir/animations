How do you catch this? On November 14th, SpaceX's tower caught a Starship back from orbit. Braking on engines alone would need about seven kilos of fuel per kilo of ship. So the air does the braking: it falls on its belly, heat shield forward. Then it flips upright and lights its engines. The arms close on two pins that stick out about a foot. So coming home is a heat problem: the shield must survive every trip.
