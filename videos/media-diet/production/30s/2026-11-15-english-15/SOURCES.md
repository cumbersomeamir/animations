**Exact topic:** SpaceX Starship Flight 15. Launched Friday 13 Nov 2026 from Starbase, Texas; the Ship 42 upper stage reached orbit,
deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites, flew about 22 hours / 14 orbits, re-entered and on Saturday 14 Nov 2026 was caught by the arms of
the Starbase launch-and-catch tower — the first catch of a Starship upper stage. The Super Heavy booster aborted its own catch and
was diverted to the Gulf of Mexico. **Status:** a test flight of a development vehicle (demonstration, not an operational product).

## The estimate
Rocket equation: m_propellant / m_ship = e^(Δv / v_e) − 1. Δv ≈ 7.4–7.6 km/s (orbital speed at ~260 km is 7.75 km/s; IE reports
~17,000 mph ≈ 7.6 km/s), exhaust speed v_e ≈ 3.4–3.7 km/s (specific impulse 350–380 s, assumed range for methane–oxygen engines).
Result 6.3–8.2 → "about seven kilos of fuel per kilo of ship" (fuel = propellant incl. oxygen; gravity losses ignored, which would add more).
