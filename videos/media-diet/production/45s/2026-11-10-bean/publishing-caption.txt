Chicago tried to move the Bean seven inches. The Bean declined. How many people would it actually take? The real one is structurally supported, so imagine a loose Bean. Assume friction resists half its weight, and each person pushes 300 newtons. That's 490,000 newtons: about 1,600 perfectly coordinated people. Perfectly is doing a lot of work.

Opposite pushes cancel. Angled ones only partly help. Most people can't reach it. Seven inches is a distance, not the starting force. The real Bean needs an anchoring calculation. Chicago brought the squad. The Bean brought a foundation.
