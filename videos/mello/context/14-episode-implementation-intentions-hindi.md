## Research (primary sources checked 2026-11-04)
1. **Gollwitzer, P. M. (1999).** Implementation intentions: Strong effects of simple plans. *American Psychologist, 54*(7), 493–503. https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.54.7.493
   - Full text read from the University of Konstanz repository (KOPS): https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/cd4fd110-827b-4d7d-b857-3ed414c7f0f0
   - Implementation intentions "link anticipated critical situations to goal-directed responses ('Whenever situation x arises, I will initiate the goal-directed response y!')".
   - They "delegate the control of goal-directed responses to anticipated situational cues, which (when actually encountered) elicit these responses automatically".
   - "Action initiation becomes swift, efficient, and does not require conscious intent."
2. **Gollwitzer, P. M., & Sheeran, P. (2006).** Implementation intentions and goal achievement: A meta-analysis of effects and processes. *Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, 38*, 69–119. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1 (abstract via KOPS: https://kops.uni-konstanz.de/entities/publication/2e749bfb-8533-437c-8203-7e788c910c5f)
   - Findings from 94 independent tests: "Implementation intentions had a positive effect of medium-to-large magnitude (d = .65) on goal attainment."
   - A strong goal intention alone does not guarantee achieving the goal.

How the script uses them:
- **"सिर्फ़ इरादा अक्सर काफ़ी नहीं होता"** (an intention alone often isn't enough) reflects the 2006 framing that a goal intention does not guarantee attainment.
- **"संकेत आते ही, फ़ैसला पहले से तय होता है"** (when the cue comes, the decision is already made) glosses "delegate the control … to anticipated situational cues".
- **"कई शोधों में … आसान हुआ, हालाँकि हर बार नहीं"** (in many studies follow-through got easier, though not every time):
  - The 94-test meta-analysis supports "many studies".
  - "Not every time" is the needed qualification: the effect is an average, not a guarantee.
  - No percentages or effect sizes are spoken.

Not claimed: guaranteed behaviour change, screen-time numbers, health outcomes, or that the app builds if-then plans.
