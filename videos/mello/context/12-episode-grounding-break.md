## Research (sources checked 2026-11-03)
1. **NHS inform (Public Health Scotland), "Grounding exercises"** (last updated 9 Dec 2025): https://www.nhsinform.scot/healthy-living/mental-wellbeing/breathing-and-relaxation-exercises/grounding-exercises
   - "Grounding is a technique that can help reduce symptoms of anxiety and stress when they feel overwhelming."
   - "Grounding aims to take your mind off of uncomfortable symptoms or thoughts."
   - The 5,4,3,2,1 method: 5 things you can see, 4 you can feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste.
2. **Cwm Taf University Health Board, Psychological Therapies Department, "Grounding: Stabilisation Pack"** (PDF hosted by NHS Forth Valley): https://nhsforthvalley.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Grounding.pdf
   - "Grounding is a way of helping you to gain some distance from your thoughts, feelings, and memories by bringing you back to the present moment."
   - Exercise 1: "Say aloud one thing that you can see, one thing you can hear, and one thing you can feel with your body … I can feel my hands touching the chair."
3. **Hull University Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust, "5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique"** (HEY1318): https://www.hey.nhs.uk/wp/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/HEY1318-2022-5-4-3-2-1-Grounding-Technique.pdf
   - Its example for "things you can hear" is "a clock ticking".

How the episode uses them: the 3-see / 2-hear / 1-feel count is a **shortened sense count** of the NHS 5,4,3,2,1 method. The Cwm Taf pack itself uses an even shorter 1-see / 1-hear / 1-feel version. The video does not present 3-2-1 as a named protocol.

Not claimed: a cure, a treatment, symptom reduction, statistics, or that the app teaches this exercise.

## Episode-specific adjustments (decisions made in this round)
- **Desk clock instead of a wall clock.** A wall clock fell into the top caption zone in S1/S5. The desk clock stays at desk height in every shot.
- **Tabs only in close shots.** In the S5 wide shot the numbered tabs would be unreadably small, so only the rings pulse there; the caption carries "Three, two, one."
- **S4 angle.** Every rear or side angle on the feet put the thigh behind the captions. The low front-right angle keeps the desk top behind the caption band.
- **Crane timing.** The crane runs about 4 s (15.8–19.7 s) with a sine ease and a leading aim point. The head never sits behind the captions, and the peak per-frame change stays well below a hard cut (14.8 against 20–45).
- **Shadow frustum.** Widened from ±4.2 to ±5.5. The crane-end elevation was raised so the floor-to-wall cove (a darker shading band) stays above the caption band.
- **Caption size.** Two-line captions are capped at 96 px, the accepted size. At 104 px they overflowed the band by 8 px.
- **S4 hold.** The first full cut had a static S4 hold (seconds 13 and 15 nearly motionless). A slow push-in was added and the video re-rendered.
