## Post caption (ready to paste)
Why does one unfinished task follow you into bed?

In studies, unfinished goals kept intruding on other tasks. Writing a specific plan made those intrusions stop. In a sleep lab, people who wrote a to-do list fell asleep faster than those listing finished tasks. Not a cure, but the loop can wait.

So park it: jot down the next step, and when you'll do it. Email Sam, tomorrow at nine.
