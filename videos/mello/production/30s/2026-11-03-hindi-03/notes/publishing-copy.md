## English gloss (for review only, not for posting)
Dropped something and felt everyone was looking at you? That's the spotlight effect. In a well-known study (Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky, 2000), students wearing an awkward T-shirt thought about half the people would remember the shirt; in reality only about a quarter did. Next time, ask yourself: what are these people actually busy with? Feeling embarrassed is normal, but probably no one noticed that much. Take a mindful break with Mello, on Google Play.
