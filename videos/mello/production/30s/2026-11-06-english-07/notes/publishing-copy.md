New rule: this podcast only plays on walks.

It's called temptation bundling: pair something you want with something you keep putting off. In a gym study, people who saved page-turner audiobooks for workouts went more often, though the boost faded.

Pick one treat: a show, a playlist, a podcast. Then set the rule: it only plays when your walking shoes are on. Want the next episode? Lace up.
