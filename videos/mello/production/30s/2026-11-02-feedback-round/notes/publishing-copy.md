## Post caption (ready to paste)
A thought is not automatically a fact.

Try this: put one phrase in front of it.
"I'll mess this up" → "I'm noticing the thought that I'll mess this up."

The thought can still be there. You're observing it instead of treating it as a prediction. Then choose one small, useful action.

This is a cognitive defusion technique from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT).

Take a mindful break with Mello, on Google Play.

Sources: Luoma & Hayes, "Cognitive Defusion", in Empirically Supported Techniques of Cognitive Behavior Therapy (Wiley); NHS Northern Cancer Alliance, "ACT in Cancer Care" (Dr Jenny Johns, 2023).
