## Post caption (ready to paste)
Stuck halfway through?

Motivation can sag in the middle of a goal. In one study, proofreaders caught more typos at the start and end than in the middle. Other studies found people stuck with goals more when they watched the smaller number. Early on, that's what's done. Near the end, it's what's left. Progress feels faster.

So at first, count what you've done. Past halfway, count what's left.
