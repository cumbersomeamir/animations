## Post caption (ready to paste)
Why do Mondays feel new?

Psychologists call it the fresh start effect. In one study, people picked a day for a goal reminder. When March twentieth was labeled the first day of spring, over three times as many chose it. A second study found the same for the first day of summer break.

A new beginning can make past slip-ups feel further away. So when starting feels hard, pick a nearby day that feels like day one, and begin there.
