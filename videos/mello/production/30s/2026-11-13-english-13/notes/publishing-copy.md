## Post caption (ready to paste)
Try not to picture a pink elephant.

Too late. Why does fighting a thought bring it back? In one study, people told to avoid a white bear kept ringing a bell for it. One theory: a part of your mind keeps checking for it. Afterwards, it tended to return more: a small, repeated effect.

So what actually helps? Don't fight it. Notice it, then give your mind one specific job.
