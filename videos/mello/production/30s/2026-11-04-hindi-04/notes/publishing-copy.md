## English gloss (for review only, not for posting)
"I'll stop scrolling" — an intention alone often isn't enough. Instead, make an if… then… plan: if it's after dinner, then the phone goes on the shelf for ten minutes. Finishing dinner is the cue; putting the phone on the shelf is the action. When the cue comes, the decision is already made. In many studies, such plans made follow-through easier, though not every time. Take a mindful break with Mello, on Google Play.
