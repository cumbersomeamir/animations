## Post caption (ready to paste)
Mind racing? Try a short grounding break.

Name 3 things you can see.
Notice 2 sounds you can hear.
Feel 1 physical sensation, like your feet on the floor.

It won't solve what's worrying you, but it brings your attention back to the present. Then return to what you were doing.

Take a mindful break with Mello, on Google Play.

Based on the NHS 5,4,3,2,1 grounding method (shortened here to 3-2-1).
