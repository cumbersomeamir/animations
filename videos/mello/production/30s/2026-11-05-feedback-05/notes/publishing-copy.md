## English gloss (for review only, not for posting)
"It's just a ten-minute job…" and an hour went by. This is called the planning fallacy: we picture the task ahead as perfect. But in reality, a lot comes up along the way. So instead of guessing, look at how long it actually took last time. A task like this took fifty-five minutes last time, so block a full hour in your calendar. And after the task, write down the actual time. Take a mindful break with Mello, on Google Play.
