## Post caption (ready to paste)
Why can you untangle a friend's problem faster than your own?

Researchers call it Solomon's paradox. Across three experiments, people reasoned more wisely about someone else's conflict than their own. But when they reflected from a distance, the gap disappeared. Self-talk using your own name also led to better speeches and less distress.

So when you're stuck, ask yourself by name: Alex, what would you tell a friend?
