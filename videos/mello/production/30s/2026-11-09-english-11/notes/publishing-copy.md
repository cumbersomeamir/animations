Your phone is within reach all night. Your book isn't.

In one study, moving snacks half a metre farther away meant fewer people ate any. They weren't less noticeable; reaching just felt like more effort. That's friction. It won't break a habit alone, but it changes what's easy.

Tonight, charge your phone across the room, and put a book where it used to be. Now the easy reach is the book, not the scroll.
