Too hard on yourself?

After an awkward message, the replay can get harsh. In one experiment, people who took a kind, understanding view of something they regretted felt more motivated to make amends. In another, after failing a test, they studied longer. Kindness led to more effort, not less.

So answer the replay kindly, then take one calm step, like a short apology.
