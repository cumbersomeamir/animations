Decision: the proposal — a tiny lift carrying twelve stars in three trips of four (3 groups of 4 = 12) — is covered
twice: feedback-01 taught "three groups of four still make twelve" and batch 22 used three lifts of four (= 12) in a lift
setting. As instructed, an unrelated subject in the genre was chosen: the perimeter of a square (measuring the distance
around a shape whose four sides are equal, using the four times table). It differs from batch 23 (time in fives on a
clock at a cloud station) in core question ("How far around?" vs "Is it time yet?"), setting (a square rooftop garden
on top of an enchanted vertical city vs a cloud-train station) and mechanism (a length around a shape, using equal sides
vs a time interval on a clock face). No earlier episode measured length or used shapes; the fact 4 × 8 = 32 is also new
to the channel. Kept from the brief: the funny star as the hero, the enchanted vertical city, the approved character
context (the english-20 mouse as race starter, the baker at the CTA; a tiny glass lift rides the tower as a cameo),
correct arithmetic, the equation only at the payoff. Frame-0 words "HOW FAR AROUND?" (3 words; truthful).

## Entertainment plan (feedback applied)
A little star at the corner of a huge square roof shades its eyes to look along the edge; the mouse raises a chequered
start flag; the star boings along the top side (one spring per hop, hop arcs drawn behind it), flops flat at the corner
("Oof!") with tired puffs; then — "It's a square!" — the glowing measured side swings round the square's centre onto the
other three sides like a clock hand (equal-side ticks pop); the star cartwheels a speed lap with afterimages while the
narrator counts by eights; confetti and the flag at the finish; the payoff sign pops up in the roof garden, a light runs
all the way round the edge (the perimeter) and the sides pulse one by one; wink; page turn. Beat-synced cuts (100 bpm)
between the star close-ups, a tracking hop shot and the wide rooftop; blurred tower tips and a balloon in the foreground.
