## Entertainment plan (feedback applied)
Mischief and slapstick: the octopus holds four baskets up with four arms while the other four wave; it tips them all
out — oranges bounce and roll off the blanket into two messy piles; one empty basket spins up and lands on its head
("bonk!", dizzy eyes, camera bump) and stays as a hat. Rubbery arms stretch to carry four oranges per row; a thinking
pause with a hat-scratch; two arms "measure" four across and four down (oranges light up with rings); a dashed square
pops; the octopus presents the square with two arms, then does a "square dance" and winks. Sound: toks, roll, bonk,
stretch squeaks, plops, hum, ticks, chimes (all synthesised); sunny C-major score that rests for the pause.
