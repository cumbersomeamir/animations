Decision: the brief's strategy is "start from ten groups and take one group away" (9 × 7 = 70 − 7). That is the
same strategy english14-01 already taught (9 × 8 = 80 − 8, with the tenth row folding away), and the requested hook
"ONE GROUP ESCAPED" names the same move. To avoid a repeated topic, the episode keeps everything else requested —
a playful creature (an original cut-paper mouse), exactly seven in every group, nine groups of seven = 63, a fun
reversible visual story (the mouse packs the rows, they unpack, they pack again), the approved Pop-up Counting
Storybook format and the TableVerse CTA — and teaches the one not-yet-covered idea the same picture holds:
nine groups of seven read both ways, 63 ÷ 7 = 9 and 9 × 7 = 63 ("To divide, ask: what times seven makes 63?").
That is the shortcut children can see in the nine groups.
Hook words changed from "ONE GROUP ESCAPED" to "HOW MANY PACKS?" because nothing escapes in this story and the
curiosity words must be truthful.
