## Story (original)
A bright tabletop pop-up bakery with deep violet/purple TableVerse accents and a small friendly baker puppet.
1. Book opens; the bakery pops up; four folded baskets pop up on the shelf.
2. Exactly three apples visibly slide into EACH basket (12 apples total, all visible from the start in the crate).
3. Each completed basket glows once while the total sign builds 3 → 6 → 9 → 12.
4. "4 groups of 3 = 12" / "4 × 3 = 12".
5. The spread turns; the SAME twelve apples are rearranged into three tins of four: "3 groups of 4 = 12".
6. Takeaway: the arrangement changed, the number of apples did not.
7. Invitation: draw equal groups together (a drawn 3 × 4 example, accurate).
8. CTA (last 3–4 s): TableVerse, addressed naturally to parent and child.

## Narration (user draft, timed naturally)
"Our baker has four baskets. Each basket needs three apples. Let's fill them: three, six, nine, twelve.
Four equal groups of three make twelve. That is what four times three means. Now watch the same apples move:
three groups of four still make twelve. The arrangement changed; the number of apples did not.
Try drawing equal groups together. Play and learn tables with TableVerse."

## Fact check (original sources)
- Multiplication as equal groups: Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, Grade 3, 3.OA.A.1 —
  "Interpret products of whole numbers, e.g., interpret 5 × 7 as the total number of objects in 5 groups of
  7 objects each." https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/OA/A/1/ (retrieved 2026-11-02).
  → supports "Four equal groups of three make twelve. That is what four times three means."
  Note: this is the CCSS reading of a × b as "a groups of b"; some curricula read the factors in the other
  order. Because the episode also shows 3 groups of 4 = 12, the takeaway holds under either reading.
- Same total in a different arrangement: CCSS 3.OA.B.5 — "If 6 × 4 = 24 is known, then 4 × 6 = 24 is also
  known. (Commutative property of multiplication.)" https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/3/OA/B/5/
  (retrieved 2026-11-02). → supports "three groups of four still make twelve".
- App claim: only the verified listing sentence (assets/listing.json): "Learn multiplication tables through
  stories, practice and fun challenges." The CTA line "Play and learn tables with TableVerse" stays within it.
