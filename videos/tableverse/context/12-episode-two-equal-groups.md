## Facts (official CCSS Mathematics PDF, https://corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Math_Standards1.pdf,

- Arrays model multiplication — 3.OA.A.3.
- Doubling a known product is in the standards' own example — 3.OA.B.5: "3 × 5 × 2 can be found by 3 × 5 = 15, then
  15 × 2 = 30 … (Associative property of multiplication.)" → 6 × 8 = (3 × 2) × 8 = 2 × (3 × 8) = 2 × 24 = 48.
  The split form 6 × 8 = 3 × 8 + 3 × 8 is the distributive property in the same standard.
- Table patterns explained by properties — 3.OA.D.9.
- Takeaway generalises exactly: for any n, 6 × n = 2 × (3 × n). Measured facts = arithmetic; bakery, sheet, hinge
  are illustrative. No app capability claimed.
