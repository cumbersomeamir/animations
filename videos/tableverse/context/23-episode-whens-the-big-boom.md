## Entertainment plan (feedback applied)
Two cut-paper frog drummers (Big Frog with a violet bow tie and an orange drum; Little Frog with an orange cap and a cyan
bongo) sit on a big lily-pad stage in a dusk pond, sticks raised, eyeing each other. Each shows off one drum hit in its own
close-up. A glowing firefly then hops along twelve numbered lily pads, landing on one pad per beat (soft "tik"); a frog
hit pops a dot under that pad — orange row for Big Frog, cyan row for Little Frog. On 6 both drums land together: BOOM,
a paper starburst, both frogs leap, water splashes, the baker cheers, and the column under pad 6 glows. Close-up on the
beat line: the dots pulse "2, 4, 6" and "3, 6"; the payoff sign 2 × 3 = 6 hangs down. Prediction: dashed dots wait under
pad 12; the band plays 7–12 and BOOMs again on 12 — and the lily-pad stage sinks under the paper wave with bubbles, then
pops back up with two cheering frogs. Page turn to the TableVerse card. Beat-synced cuts at 100 bpm; the music's bars
