A robot vacuum can dodge a sock, then swallow a cable. Why? 🤖🧦

A camera spots what it was trained to name: socks, shoes, sometimes cords.
A laser measures shape, but a thin cable lying flat barely registers.
And the lists differ by model: one Roomba dodges cords, but not socks.
So check your model's list, and clear cables before its first mapping run. Some switch avoidance off while learning.
