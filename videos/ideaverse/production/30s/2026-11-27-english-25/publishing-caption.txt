A thief grabs your phone and runs. The screen locks itself. How? 📱🔒

Android's Theft Detection Lock uses AI and the motion sensors.
It recognizes the jolt of a grab, then running, biking or driving away.
That pattern locks the screen in a moment.
To avoid false alarms, it may not trigger with a steady Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connection.
Check it's on: Settings, Google, All services, Theft protection.
