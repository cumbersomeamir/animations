A messy handwritten shopping list? Snap a photo of it. 🛒📝

Ask an AI app: turn this into a checklist grouped by supermarket aisle. Add: skip crossed-out items, and flag anything you can't read.
Here, bread was crossed out, so it is gone. And is that a six or an eight? It gets flagged.
Before you shop, check the list against your photo, and fix anything it got wrong.
