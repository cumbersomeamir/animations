You pressed too late, but your phone still caught the jump. How? 🐕📸

The camera starts shooting when you open it, keeping recent frames in a loop.
The shutter just picks frames it already took.
Live Photos and Top Shot keep a second and a half on each side.
Top Shot even has a model suggest a sharper frame.
Missed it? Open the photo's motion frames and pick the jump yourself.
