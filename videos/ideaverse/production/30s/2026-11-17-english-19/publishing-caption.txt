A phone shoots the moon with crisp craters. Where does that detail come from? 🌕📱

On Samsung Galaxy phones, Scene Optimizer first recognizes the moon.
It merges more than ten shots to cut the noise.
Then a deep learning engine enhances the details even further.
Samsung says it's still reducing confusion between the real moon and a picture of it.
For only what the lens caught, turn Scene Optimizer off before you shoot.
