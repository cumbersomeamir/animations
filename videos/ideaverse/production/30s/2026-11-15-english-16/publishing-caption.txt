Nobody said the wake word. So why did the speaker wake up? 🔊❓

On standby, a tiny on-device model checks short snippets of sound for the wake word.
Nothing is sent until a snippet sounds close enough.
Researchers played TV at smart speakers, and every show woke one up.
"Maybe I don't like the cold" woke a Google speaker.
So if a Google speaker wakes too often, lower its sensitivity in the Home app.
