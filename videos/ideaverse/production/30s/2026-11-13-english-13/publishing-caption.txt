In a noisy room, which voice should a hearing aid keep? 🦻

Some new ones have an AI chip that pulls speech out of noise.
The idea: slice sound into tiny bits of pitch and time, and keep the bits that look like speech.
Clatter fades. Voices stay.
But a second voice is speech too, and when two people talk at once, their bits overlap.
So around someone with hearing aids, take turns: one voice at a time.
