In total darkness, Face ID still knows it's you. 🔒

Yet a photo of you can't fool it. Why?
Thousands of invisible dots land on your face, mapping its shape in 3D.
It compares that shape with the face stored at setup.
A flat photo has no depth, so it fails.
Twins, look-alike siblings and kids under thirteen are more likely to unlock it. If that worries you, use a passcode.
