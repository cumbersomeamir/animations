A tiny phone lens can't really blur a background like this. So how? 📸

Portrait mode uses AI to find the person, then estimates how far away everything else is. Then software adds the blur: farther means blurrier.
Frizzy hair or sunglasses can fool it, so check the edges.
And since the blur is added later, you can often turn it down, or off, afterwards.
