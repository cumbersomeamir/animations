This AI never drives a car, yet it can cut your stops at red lights. 🚦

Google's Green Light reads anonymous Maps driving trends.
Where cars keep stopping, it suggests new timing to city engineers.
A little more green, or lights that turn green in a wave.
In Boston's pilot, one crossing saw 33% fewer unnecessary stops.
Results vary. If one light always stops you, report it to your city.

Download the Ideaverse app.

(The city, cars, timings and screens in the video are illustrations. The 33% figure is the City of Boston's reported pilot result for one intersection, Atlantic Ave & Richmond St, evaluated with INRIX.)
