Turn a messy voice note into a clear to-do list.

1. Record your thoughts as they come: "email the client, buy groceries, book the dentist next week."
2. In an AI tool that supports voice input, say or paste:
   "Turn this into a checklist. Keep every task. Use only deadlines I mentioned. Flag anything unclear."
3. Check the list against your note before you rely on it.

The AI organised the tasks. Doing them is still up to you.

(The note and tasks in the video are a made-up example.)
