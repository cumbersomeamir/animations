Stuck on a noisy bus with a voice message you can't hear? 🚌🔇

Turn on Live Caption on Android, or Live Captions on iPhone. AI listens to the audio and types the words on your screen.
But it can get it wrong. Your friend said three fifteen. The caption says three fifty.
So for times, names and numbers that matter, check with the sender before you act.
