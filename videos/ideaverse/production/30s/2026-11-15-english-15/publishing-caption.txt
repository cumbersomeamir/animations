Your phone can shoot a room almost too dark to see. How? 🌙📷

Night Sight, on a Pixel, takes a burst, not one photo.
The grain differs in every frame, so lining them up and averaging cancels it out.
A neural network then cleans up what's left.
But anything that moves won't line up. It blurs.
So brace the phone, and ask people to freeze until it's done.

Download the Ideaverse app.
