A useful AI prompt has three layers:

1. Goal: what you want done
2. Context: who it's for and what matters
3. Output: the shape you need, such as a short table

Example: "Explain this electricity bill to a first-time renter, identify the charges, and return a short table."

Then check important numbers against the original document. A better prompt helps direct the answer; it doesn't make every claim true.
