Your iPhone charges to 80% overnight, then waits. Why does it stop? 🔋🌙

It's protecting the battery.
Batteries age faster when they sit at full charge.
Machine learning on the phone learns your charging routine.
After two weeks of long charges in one place, it holds at 80%,
and finishes shortly before you usually need it.
Need it full now? Touch and hold the notification, then tap Charge Now.
