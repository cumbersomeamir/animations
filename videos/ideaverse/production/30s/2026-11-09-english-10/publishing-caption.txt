The road ahead looks clear. So why does your map warn of a jam? 🚗🗺️

Speeds from phones already on that road show the traffic right now.
Then it adds how that road usually flows at this hour, to predict what's coming.
It isn't perfect: a sudden crash can still throw the estimate off.
For an important trip, set a leave time in advance to see the estimated journey time.

Download the Ideaverse app.
