A tune stuck in your head, but no words? Just hum it. 🎵

In the Google app, tap Search a song, then hum for 10 to 15 seconds.
It turns your hum into the shape of the melody and compares it with songs. No perfect pitch needed.
You get the likeliest matches, and the top one can be wrong.
So play a few seconds of each before you trust the name.
