How does your phone read the words in a photo? 📸

First it finds where the text is, then it recognizes each letter and word by matching their shapes to patterns it learned from many examples. That's what lets you copy a bus time or translate a menu straight from a picture. A blurry photo can be misread, so double-check times and prices.
