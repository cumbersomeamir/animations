This frame never happened: one ball, two places. 🏀🎞️

AI slow motion made it up.
It takes two real frames and guesses how each pixel moved.
Then it moves both frames to the middle and blends them into a new one.
But on a fast bounce, that guess can fail, and the blend shows both balls.
Neither real frame shows this.
Smooth isn't proof. To judge a real moment, step through the original frames.
