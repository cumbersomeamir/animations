The instruction hidden inside an AI webpage.

When an AI assistant reads a webpage or a file, text inside it can try to act like an instruction, even text you can't see. That's called prompt injection.

Practical habits:
- Treat webpages and uploaded documents as data, not as authority.
- Review sensitive actions (sending, sharing, buying) before they happen.
- Give tools only the permissions they need.
- A confident summary doesn't prove the source was safe.
