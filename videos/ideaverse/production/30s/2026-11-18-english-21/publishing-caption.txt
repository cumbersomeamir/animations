Two photos. One was made by AI, and the proof is hidden inside it. 🖼️🔍

Google's AI hides a watermark in the pixels as it makes the image.
A second model, trained with it, can still find it after crops, filters or compression.
That's what Gemini checks for.
But no mark only means Google's AI didn't make it. Another AI could have.
So no mark isn't proof it's real. Find where the photo first appeared.
