How does your inbox know what's spam? 📬

Spam filters are AI you use every day: they learn patterns from lots of example emails (suspicious links, pushy sales wording, messages people report) and give each new email a spam score. The mail service then decides where it goes. They can still get it wrong, so if an important email is missing, check your spam folder and mark it "not spam".
