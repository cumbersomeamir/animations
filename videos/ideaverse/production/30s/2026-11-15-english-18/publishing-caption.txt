Your card works fine at home, then gets declined on holiday. Why? 💳✈️

Your card network scores each payment for fraud, using hundreds of clues.
A new country. A new shop. A bigger amount than usual.
Each alone looks fine. Together, the score climbs past your bank's line.
So sometimes a real payment gets blocked.
If that happens, open your bank's app, confirm it was you, then pay again.
