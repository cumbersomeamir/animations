None of these photos is called dog. So how did search find him? 🐶🔍

Your photos app looks at each picture and tags what it sees: dog, beach, cake.
Search matches your word to those tags, not only file names.
Mistakes happen: this goat got tagged as a dog.
So search for what was in a lost photo: a receipt, a beach, a cake.

Download the Ideaverse app.
