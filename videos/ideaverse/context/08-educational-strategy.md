# 08 — Educational strategy (active direction from 2026-11-02)

Supersedes campaign-style requirements for educational work. Earlier work is preserved untouched:
30 s stickman WIP (src/, 05-progress.md, commit 75b7b37) and 15 s kinetic-reel test (tests/15s/, 06/07, checkpointed).

## Goal
Grow a niche audience with short, genuinely useful lessons. Each video must teach one thing even if the viewer never
installs Ideaverse. Occasional relevant app posts come later; in lessons the app appears only as a small signature.

## Channel niche
AI news, developments and practical AI literacy. Evergreen literacy lessons; news items only when sourced and dated
(never a fabricated "announcement").

## Format rules
- 15 s portrait 1080x1920, 30 fps. One concept. Open on a question/physical puzzle → demonstrate the mechanism →
  concise, useful takeaway. Narrow the idea rather than speed up narration.
- Recurring original performer: DOCKET, a robot reporter with a filing-cabinet body (see education/pilot-01/CHARACTER.md).
  Same design every episode; situations, camera and choreography change.
- Medium: dimensional navy/mint "experimental newsroom" stage, browser Three.js rendered locally; physical props that
  perform the mechanism (drawers, chains, meters, stamps), not text slides.
- Palette from the Ideaverse icon: navy #1B2333, mint #6CDA7E, white. Provisional functional accent: amber #FFC24B
  for "uncertain/unverified" only.
- Every factual claim mapped to a public, dated source (SOURCES.md). Illustrative scenes labelled; never shown as app
  UI, experiment data or a real report. No feature tour, no forced store CTA, no slogans, no product claims.
- References (quality bar only, not watched in this session, not copied): instagram.com/battysticky (recurring expressive
  performer, dark dimensional stage, physical demos), instagram.com/breakingbreach (dimensional objects, metaphors,
  mechanism explanations). No copying of their characters, scripts, logos, assets, palettes or voices.
