| claim | type | source |

| Theft Detection Lock uses AI and the motion sensors | documented | Android Help, "Protect your Android device against theft", https://support.google.com/android/answer/15146908 — "To detect if someone unexpectedly takes your device and runs away, Theft Detection Lock uses AI, your device's motion sensors, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth." |
| it recognizes the jolt of a grab, then running, biking or driving away | documented | Google blog (15 May 2024), "Android's theft protection features keep your device and data safe", https://blog.google/products/android/android-theft-protection/ — "Theft Detection Lock … uses Google AI to sense if someone snatches your phone from your hand and tries to run, bike or drive away. If a common motion associated with theft is detected, your phone screen quickly locks"; Android Help — "if someone grabs the phone out of your hand and they run, bike, or drive away, the Theft Detection Lock may activate." |
| that pattern locks the screen in a moment | documented | Same — "your phone screen quickly locks"; Help — "it automatically locks your device's screen to protect its content." |
| to avoid false alarms, it may not trigger with a steady Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connection | documented | Android Help — "To limit interruptions during regular device use, Theft Detection Lock may not trigger when: Your device has either a stable Wi-Fi connection, Bluetooth connection, or both. | check it's on: Settings, Google, All services, Theft protection | documented | Android Help — "Open your device's Settings app. Tap Google All services Theft protection. Turn on Theft Detection Lock. If the setting for 'Theft Detection Lock' is grayed out, your device doesn't support this feature."
