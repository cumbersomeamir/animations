## Meme storyboard (mutes cleanly: captions + props carry every beat)
1. 0–2.55 hook: DOCKET types "राशन की लिस्ट बना दो" ("make a grocery list"); caption "दो लोगों का राशन माँगा था…" ("I asked for groceries for two…").
2. 2.55–5.45 punchline: the reply scrolls an endless list; sacks/boxes rain into an original cart (चावल 25 kg, चीनी, बिस्किट ×48,
   आलू, चायपत्ती); **freeze-frame** 4.4–5.38 on "AI ने पूरा मोहल्ला बुला लिया!" ("AI invited the whole neighbourhood!").
3. 5.45–10.2 escalation: आटा 50 kg slams in (camera shake) → 4 oil cans → 3 watermelons; DOCKET double-takes; the third
   melon bounces off the pile and knocks the clipboard out of DOCKET's hand.
4. 10.2–10.98 **freeze-frame** zoom on DOCKET's deadpan stare.
5. 10.95–14.45 payoff: phone close-up — the vague prompt ringed amber; four questions pop: कितने लोग? कितने दिन? बजट? घर में क्या है? ("How many people? How many days? Budget? What's already at home?")
6. 14.45–15.85 wide: the absurd pile flies out of the cart.
7. 15.85–21.6 phone close-up: constraint chips appear on the words — 2 लोग · 3 दिन · ₹600 · घर में: चावल-दाल ("2 people · 3 days · ₹600 · at home: rice and dal"); send; a modest list

8. 21.6–23.4 cart: four small items land; price tags tick ✓ one by one ("दाम खुद जाँचिए", "check prices yourself").
9. 23.4–30 CTA: DOCKET waves; caption with icon "ऐसे काम के AI तरीके सीखने के लिए / [icon] आइडियावर्स ऐप डाउनलोड कीजिए" ("To learn useful AI tricks like this / download the Ideaverse app").
