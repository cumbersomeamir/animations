## The one concept
Fluent, confident wording is not evidence. A generated answer can sound certain and still be unsupported, so check
its support against a reliable source and treat it as unconfirmed until it matches.
Accuracy guardrails: do NOT imply all AI lacks retrieval (the answer in the scene HAS a support drawer and a source tag);
do NOT imply citations guarantee truth (its support chain has a missing link). Evergreen, not a news item.
