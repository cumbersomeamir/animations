**Logline:** A village potter wants to start the day's work, but her old wheel breaks, so someone far away funds a verified new wheel and watches her begin.

**World:** one painted valley, watercolour on warm paper. Dawn yard (cream sky, forest-green hills, terracotta pots) ↔ a dusk balcony in town (deep forest-green night, warm window light). **Colour arc:** grey-green dawn with a broken wheel → gold light carried across the sky → full warm morning. Accents from the icon: forest green `#05483A`, cream `#FCF6EE`, gold star `#F0B344`.

**Motif:** the gold four-point star of the Aarambh icon. It glints in the dawn sky, leaps from the supporter's phone, flies to the yard, *becomes* the new wheel, and returns on the end card as the icon's star.

**Potter's arc:** determined → surprised → sad → curious → surprised → starstruck → happy/proud. **Supporter's arc:** thinking → determined → love.

**Rhyme:** opens on a wheel that wobbles and fails; ends on a wheel spinning true (and the same star on the icon).
