Stephanie Kwolek: a thread that saved lives

This thread has helped save police lives, and it began as a cloudy liquid. How did a liquid get that strong? In 1965, Stephanie Kwolek had it spun into fibre. Its rod-like chains lie side by side, like dry spaghetti, not a tangle. Pull, and they share the load: by weight, five times stronger than steel.

Lesson: strong isn't bulletproof; ask which threats a vest is rated for.
