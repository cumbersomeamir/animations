Mary Anning: a monster in the cliff?

As a girl, fossil hunter Mary Anning dug a five-metre skeleton from a cliff. Locals called it a monster; scientists guessed a crocodile. What was it? Her brother found the skull; she spent months freeing the body. Experts later named it Ichthyosaurus: an extinct sea reptile. Her finds inspired the first scene of ancient life drawn from fossils.

Yet papers often skipped her name. Lesson: credit the finder.
