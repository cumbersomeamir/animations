As a girl, fossil hunter Mary, anning dug a five meter skeleton from a cliff. Locals called it a monster, scientists guessed a crocodile. What was it? Her brother found the skull. She spent months freeing the body. Experts later named it ichthyosaurus, an extinct sea reptile. Her finds inspired the first scene of ancient life, drawn from fossils.
