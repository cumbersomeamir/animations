Gideon Sundback and the zipper slider: design the transition (U.S. Patent 1,219,881, 1917)

Gideon Sundback made Whitcomb Judson's earlier fastener reliable. His 1917 patent uses identical, cup-shaped teeth that alternate between two tapes. The slider's Y-shaped channel steers both rows together, so each tooth nests in the next. Pull it back, and the diverging channels part them again, tooth by tooth.

Lesson: design the transition between states, not just the two endpoints.
