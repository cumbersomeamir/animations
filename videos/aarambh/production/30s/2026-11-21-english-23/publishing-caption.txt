Inge Lehmann: she heard the impossible

Earthquake waves arrived where they shouldn't. Inge Lehmann noticed. What was hiding inside? The core slows waves and bends them away, leaving a quiet band. Yet in 1929, stations there recorded quake waves. In 1936, she proposed an inner core where waves speed up, sending some there. Later evidence showed it is solid.

Lesson: a measurement that disagrees with a prediction can reveal hidden structure.
