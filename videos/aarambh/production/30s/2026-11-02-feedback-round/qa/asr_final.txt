In 1941, Burr's stuck to Swiss engineer George de Mistral's clothes. Under a microscope, he saw tiny hooks catching looped threads. With a French weaver, he spent years copying that structure, one strip of hooks, one of loops. Press them together and they grip, pull, and the flexible hooks let go. The lesson, study how nature solves a problem, then test the mechanism. Follow our ramp for the ideas behind great inventions.
