Marie Curie: two sciences, two Nobel Prizes.

In 1903 she shared the Nobel Prize in Physics with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel, for research on radiation phenomena. In 1911 she received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the discovery of the elements radium and polonium. One line of research — radioactivity — carried her from one science into another.
