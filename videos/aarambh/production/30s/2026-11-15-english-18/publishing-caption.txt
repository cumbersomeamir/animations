John Snow: one handle, many lives

In ten days, cholera killed over 500 people near one London pump. Many doctors blamed bad air. How did John Snow find the cause? He traced where the dead had lived: nearly all near the Broad Street pump. Brewery workers drinking beer escaped; a workhouse with its own well barely suffered. The handle came off, but attacks were already falling.

Lesson: map who got sick, then ask who was spared.
