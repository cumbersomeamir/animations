Elizebeth Friedman: she read their secrets

Smugglers radioed orders in secret code. Elizebeth Friedman read them anyway. How, without the key? Their codes swapped each letter the same way every time, so patterns showed. She and an aide cracked over 12,000 messages. Nazi spies used Enigma, which changed the swap every key press; her team still broke it.

Lesson: patterns that repeat leak secrets; never reuse a password.
