The Brooklyn Bridge's chief engineer couldn't visit it for 11 years. So how did his plans reach the builders? Washington Robling got the bends in pressurized casens under the river. He dictated from home. His wife, Emily, rode it down and carried it to the assistant engineers. She learned the engineering. On site, she was treated like the chief engineer.
