Emily Warren Roebling: she carried the plans

The Brooklyn Bridge's chief engineer couldn't visit it for 11 years. So how did his plans reach the builders? Washington Roebling got the bends in pressurized caissons under the river. He dictated from home; his wife, Emily, wrote it down and carried it to the assistant engineers. She learned the engineering; on site, she was treated like the chief engineer.
