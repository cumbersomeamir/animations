Vivien Thomas: the hands they forgot

Blue babies lacked oxygen. Vivien Thomas helped fix it, then went uncredited. Too little of their blood reached the lungs. A shunt rerouted an artery to the lungs, so more blood picked up oxygen. Thomas perfected it on about 200 dogs, then coached the surgeon. On paper he was a janitor; the 1945 report left out his name.

Lesson: practise where mistakes are safe, then guide the real thing.
