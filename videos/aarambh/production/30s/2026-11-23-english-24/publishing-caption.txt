Tu Youyou: the clue was the temperature

An extract against malaria kept failing. Tu Youyou found out why. What was ruining it? It was made with heat. An ancient recipe said: soak the herb, wring out the juice. No boiling. Her team extracted it cool, in ether. In 1971, it cleared parasites in mice. That led to artemisinin, and a 2015 Nobel Prize.

Lesson: when a good idea fails, test the step you assumed was harmless.
