Grace Hopper handed out a nanosecond you could hold. It was a wire about 30 centimeters long. Why that length? Nothing travels faster than light, about 300 ,000 kilometers a second. In one billionth of a second, even light covers only 30 centimeters. As computer parts shrank, she used a grain of pepper for a picosecond.
