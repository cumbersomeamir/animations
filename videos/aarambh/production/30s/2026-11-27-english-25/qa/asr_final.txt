How far away is a star? Henrietta Levitt found a clue in its blink. A faint star might be weak or just far away. She studied blinking stars in one distant cloud, all about equally far. There, the brighter one's blinked slower, so a blink gives true brightness and faintness then gives distance. Hubble later used her rule to show Andromeda is another galaxy.
