Henrietta Leavitt: a blink measures space

How far away is a star? Henrietta Leavitt found a clue in its blink. A faint star might be weak, or just far away. She studied blinking stars in one distant cloud, all about equally far. There, the brighter ones blinked slower. So a blink gives true brightness, and faintness then gives distance. Hubble later used her rule to show Andromeda is another galaxy.

Lesson: to see a real pattern, compare like with like.
