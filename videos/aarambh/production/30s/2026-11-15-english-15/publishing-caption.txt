Frances Oldham Kelsey: stopped at the door

In 1960, an FDA reviewer refused a pill sold in dozens of countries. It was thalidomide. Why did she say no? Frances Kelsey found its safety evidence too thin. The company kept pressing; she kept asking for reliable evidence. Then researchers linked it to severe birth defects; it was never marketed in the U.S.

Lesson: popularity isn't proof of safety; ask what the evidence shows.
