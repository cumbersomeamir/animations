Percy Spencer: from radar magnetrons to microwave cooking (U.S. Patent 2,495,429)

Radar engineer Percy Spencer recalled a candy bar melting in his pocket near a magnetron. Retellings change the snack; the documented record is his 1945 patent. It sends magnetron microwaves down a guide to food on a conveyor. His test: an egg hard-boiled with 2 units of energy, instead of 36.

Lesson: when something odd happens, test it and measure it.
