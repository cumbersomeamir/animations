Samuel Morse: a portrait painter's telegraph (U.S. Patent 1,647, 1840)

In 1844, a message flashed by wire between Washington and Baltimore. It ran on the telegraph of Samuel Morse, a former portrait painter with little electrical training. His 1840 patent describes a register: a magnet that presses a pen onto moving paper. Short pulses of current leave dots; longer ones leave lines.
