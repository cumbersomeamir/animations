Most British soldiers who died in the Crimean War weren't killed by wounds, so what killed them? In Florence Nightingale's chart, blue is death from preventable disease. After a sanitary commission flushed sewers in 1 ,855, the blue shrank, even the next winter. Strong evidence not certainty. One chart can't rule out other changes.
