Florence Nightingale: not the wounds

Most British soldiers who died in the Crimean War weren't killed by wounds. So what killed them? In Florence Nightingale's chart, blue is death from preventable disease. After a sanitary commission flushed sewers in 1855, the blue shrank, even the next winter. Strong evidence, not certainty: one chart can't rule out other changes.

Lesson: before calling it proof, ask what else changed.
