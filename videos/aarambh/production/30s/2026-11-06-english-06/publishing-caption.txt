Mary Anderson and the windshield wiper (U.S. Patent 743,801, 1903)

On a snowy New York trolley ride, Mary Anderson noticed a problem. Drivers had to open their windows, sometimes stopping to clear the glass. Her 1903 patent put rubber blades on an arm outside the glass. Turn a handle inside, and springs press the rubber as it sweeps. A counterweight kept the swinging arm balanced.

Lesson: the hassles everyone accepts are problems waiting for a tool.
