Ada Lovelace and Note G: the mathematician behind the first published computer algorithm.

In August 1843, Ada Lovelace published extensive notes on Charles Babbage's proposed Analytical Engine. Her Note G set out step by step how the engine could calculate Bernoulli numbers. The Science Museum calls it "the first published algorithm, sometimes credited as the first computer programme". The engine was never completed: only a trial piece was finished before Babbage died in 1871. Lovelace also wrote that the engine "might compose elaborate and scientific pieces of music".
