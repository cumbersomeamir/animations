Josephine Cochran: the chipped china that led to a dish-washing machine (U.S. Patent 355,139, 1886)

Hand-washing was chipping Josephine Cochran's heirloom china. Her 1886 patent cleaned dishes with water pressure, instead of scrubbing. Wire racks held plates, glasses and knives in place inside a crate. A hand lever pumped soapy jets while the crate slowly turned; then hot water rinsed, and the dishes dried from their own heat.

Lesson: notice everyday friction; it can inspire an invention.
