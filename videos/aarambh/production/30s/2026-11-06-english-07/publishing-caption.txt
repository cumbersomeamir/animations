Garrett Morgan's traffic signal improvement (U.S. Patent 1,475,024, 1923)

Many early traffic signals showed only two commands: stop and go. In 1923, Cleveland inventor Garrett Morgan patented an improvement. A hand crank first raised the arms, stopping traffic in every direction. That pause let cars already crossing clear the intersection. Then the signal turned, and the arms lowered slowly for the other street.

Lesson: improve a system by adding the step it was missing.
