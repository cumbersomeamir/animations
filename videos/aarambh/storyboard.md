# Aarambh — 30s portrait storyboard (1080×1920, 30 fps, 900 frames)

**Story (one tool, cause → effect):** a tailor's hand-stitched thread snaps → her need (a sewing machine) becomes a request card → the card is stamped *Verified* → a supporter funds it from a phone (stylized UI) → the machine is packed, cycled across town, delivered → she treadles the machine and finished kurtas fly onto the rack → thread stitches the screen into the Aarambh end card.

**Device:** a five-node checkpoint tracker (Need → Verified → Funded → Delivered → At work) persists at the top from the moment the request exists, lighting one node per story beat. It is a stylized illustration of "follow their impact", not a captured app screen.

**Style:** editorial paper cut-outs (flat shapes, hard paper shadows, light grain) + ink stickman rigs with faces. Palette from the verified icon: green `#05483A`, cream `#FCF6EE`, gold `#F0B344`; saffron `#EF8F43` (earlier brief colour) for fabric only; kraft for the box.
