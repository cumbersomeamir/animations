### Verbatim excerpts
- **S1:**
  - "The lockstitch uses two threads, an upper and a lower."
  - "a rotating hook (or other hooking mechanism) catches the upper thread at the point just after it goes through the needle"
  - "carries the upper thread entirely around the bobbin case"
  - "pulls the excess upper thread (from the bobbin area) back to the top"
  - "Ideally, the lockstitch is formed in the center of the thickness of the material."
- **S2:**
  - "using two threads, one passed through a needle and one coming from a bobbin or shuttle."
  - "Each thread stays on its own side of the material while being sewn"
  - "interlacing with the other thread at each needle hole"
  - Chain stitch: "the machine uses a single thread" … "The stitch is not self-locking".
- **S3:** "a lockstitch is formed by concatenation of an upper, or needle thread with a lower, or bobbin thread. a looptaker is provided to pick up a loop of the needle thread carried through the work material by the sewing needle, enlarge the loop and cast it about a thread carrying bobbin supported internally of the looptaker. a sewing machine take-up lever is provided for the needle thread, to supply the needle thread required for the enlarged loop, and to take up the excess needle thread when the loop has been cast about the bobbin. When the excess needle thread is taken up by the take-up lever, it carries with it a bobbin thread, about which it is now looped."

## Deliberate simplifications (disclosed on screen as "simplified cutaway")
- The hook is drawn as a rotary hook in a side cross-section. Many domestic machines use an oscillating shuttle instead. Both carry the loop around the bobbin case (S1: "rotating hook (or other hooking mechanism)").
- A rotary hook turns twice per stitch. The video shows the one pass that carries the loop, then pauses the hook. Nothing is said about speed or count.
- In 3D, the loop passes over the front face of the bobbin case and behind it. In 2D this is shown as the gold loop tracing round the case, with the back limb dimmed.
- Feed dogs appear only as small teeth. Fabric advance (one stitch) is shown but not narrated.
- The take-up lever and thread guides are placed for legibility, not to scale. Hook timing and tension are not covered.
- The workshop machine is a generic, unbranded illustration. Bindu's size changes ("shrinks into the cutaway") are a storytelling device.
