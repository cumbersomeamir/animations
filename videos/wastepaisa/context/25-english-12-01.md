## Research (accessed 11 Nov 2026). Measured facts, market reports, estimates and assumptions are kept apart
- **F1 (technical, reliable industry body):** Australian Stainless Steel Development Association, "200 series stainless steels – high
  manganese (CrMn)", Dr Graham Sussex, 2013 (updated 18 May 2023):
  https://www.assda.asn.au/blog/262-200-series-stainless-steels-high-manganese-crmn
  - 304 contains 8.0–10.5 % nickel; 201/202-type CrMn grades about 1–6 % (Table 1). Nickel "is still the most costly component".
  - CrMn grades are not magnetic and stay non-magnetic when cold worked, so they "appear to be stainless" and are "often assumed to be
    304"; ferritic grades such as 430 are magnetic. Identification: portable XRF, or Mn/Mo drop-test kits.
  - Fabrication and end-of-life CrMn scrap "are not readily distinguished from conventional CrNi grade scrap", and CrMn offcut scrap
    "will have lower value".
- **M1 (market report, dated and located):** BigMint, "India: Stainless steel scrap prices surge on supply crunch, nickel rally",
  1 May 2026: domestic 304 scrap **₹138,000/t DAP Delhi**; **utensil scrap ₹71,000/t** (city and basis not stated); BigMint import
  assessments 304 $1,500/t, 201 $730/t, 430 $660/t.
  https://www.bigmint.co/insights/detail/india-stainless-steel-scrap-prices-surge-on-supply-crunch-nickel-rally-746308
- **M2 (latest 304 figure):** BigMint, 16 Oct 2026: 304 scrap ₹148,000/t DAP Delhi, ₹150,000/t DAP Mumbai (no utensil figure, so the
  same-date May pair is used for the comparison).
  https://www.bigmint.co/insights/detail/india-stainless-steel-scrap-prices-weaken-amid-falling-nickel-subdued-buying-799798
- **M3 (household rate, undated):** Kabadiwala Online, Delhi NCR price list: stainless steel scrap ₹60–90/kg, "Food grade / 304 SS
  fetches higher prices"; "Prices vary for bulk". https://kabadiwalaonline.in/price-list/
- **T1, T2 (vehicle hire, from context 24):** Bangalore Tata Ace local tariff 5 h/50 km ₹1,900, 8 h/80 km ₹3,000 (+5 % GST); TruckGuru
  ≈₹2,000 per short trip.
- **P4 (labour, from context 17):** Delhi unskilled minimum wage ₹710/day.
- **Not used:** retail "today's rate" aggregator pages (undated, grade definitions unclear; one states 316 has no nickel, which is wrong).
