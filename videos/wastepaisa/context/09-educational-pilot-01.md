## Creative choices (not facts)
Tinku, the lab set, the cut stroke, the stretchy arm and the tug-of-war are illustrative
metaphors. Real separation happens in industrial pulping, not by hand. The ✓/✗ signs are
illustrative, not data about any real facility.
