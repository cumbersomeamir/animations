## Research (accessed 17 Nov 2026). Facts, reported figures and assumptions are kept apart
- **R1, PIB feature, Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs, "Temple waste recycling, driven by Swachh Bharat, is turning flowers into jobs
  and sustainability"** (20 Sep 2024; primary, read in full via pdftotext):
  https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2024/sep/doc2024920398201.pdf
  - Ujjain's Mahalakaleshwar Temple, with 75,000–100,000 daily visitors, "produces an astounding 5-6 tonnes of floral waste each day".
  - That waste goes to a "3-ton-per-day processing plant".
  - "Women from the Shiv Arpan Self-Help Group (SHG) have taken the lead, crafting over 30 million incense sticks and other eco-friendly
    items." Source for L3.
  - Also: Phool.co "collects approximately 21 tonnes of floral waste each week" and turns it into incense products; Siddhivinayak's
    flowers become natural dyes.
- **R2, CSIR-CIMAP technology** (Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser livelihood portal; India Science & Technology portal):
  https://psa.gov.in/livelihood/9888
  - "CSIR-CIMAP, Lucknow developed an innovative process to produce eco-friendly incense sticks and cones" from flowers offered at
    places of worship; TRL 9.
  - Context for the method; the portal gives no prices or yields.
- **R3, The Better India on Aaruhi Enterprises, Gurugram** (6 Mar 2024):
  https://thebetterindia.com/343922/temple-flower-waste-recycling-startup-homemaker-turned-entrepreneur-incense-sticks-gurugram-poonam-singh
  - Process: flowers are requested from temples, sorted, sun-dried, powdered with a pulveriser, then mixed into dhoop sticks.
  - About 1,000 kg of floral waste a month; 5 women employed. Products range from "Rs 50-350".
  - It also reports monthly earnings "more than Rs 2 lakh". That figure is in the notes only, not in the video (no lakh claims).
  - Source for L4's steps and for the ₹50 price used in L7's lever.
- **R4, The Tribune, Jammu** (12 Dec 2019): nearly 24 women sort, cut, dry and process temple flowers into "over 6,000 incense sticks in a
  day with the help of machines". Context.
- **Prices:** no verified wholesale price for flower dhoop cones. The base price (₹40 a pack) is an assumption set below the ₹50 low end
  reported in R3; the lever uses ₹50.
