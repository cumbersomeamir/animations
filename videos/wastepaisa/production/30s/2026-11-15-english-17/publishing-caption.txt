From broken to bought.

Broken tile looks like rubble. So how does it become something people buy? Chandigarh's Rock Garden is studded with broken crockery and tile. Sort the shards by colour, glue them on a base, and grout the gaps. Seal it, add felt, and it's a coaster set or a small tabletop.

Its price must cover materials, your labour and selling costs; the rest is profit. So price one set before you make ten. (The price split in the video is illustrative; no prices are quoted.)
