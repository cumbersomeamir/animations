Who buys old frying oil? Never another kitchen.

Let it cool, then pour it into a labelled can. Give it only to an authorised collector, and log it.

Example (costs vary): say a ₹2,000 van trip picks up one kitchen's 25 litres: ₹80 a litre. Pool 500 litres from twenty kitchens: ₹4 a litre. Then a licensed plant turns it into biodiesel.

Sources:
FSSAI Guidance Note 06/2018, Handling and Disposal of Used Cooking Oil: cool, separate labelled container, authorised collectors only, records above 50 litres a day, oil above 25% polar compounds is unfit
