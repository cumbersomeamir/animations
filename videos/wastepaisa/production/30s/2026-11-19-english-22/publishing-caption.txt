Trash with a buyer.

India grows about 20 billion coconuts a year. Which shells sell? A Kerala shell-craft firm exported 40,000 pieces to Denmark. Sand an uncracked half, polish it with coconut oil, drill a drain, add a rope.

A plant shop pays ₹199: revenue, not profit. After shell, oil, rope, tools and labour, about ₹70 is left. The finish is what the buyer pays for. (The unit figures are assumptions for illustration, not market prices or a promise. One online listing for a handcrafted coconut-shell plant hanger was seen at ₹650.)
