What's inside that old cable?

Under the plastic there may be copper or aluminium (data cables have thin wires and lots of insulation), and the metal changes its scrap value. A buyer also looks at insulation, grade and weight. Keep cable types separate, and ask how each is priced before you sell.

Never burn insulation to get at the metal: the smoke is toxic, and in places such as the UK it is a crime.
