Drop hard plastic in water. What floats sells for more.

Why would floating change the price? Floaters are polythene and polypropylene, lighter than water; PVC sinks. Buyers cap other plastics, and refuse PVC and metal. So sort floaters by their stamped number: 2 or 5.

Rates (vary): sorted HDPE lists near ₹70 a kilo; mixed, ₹15 to ₹30. That gap pays for sorting, cleaning and transport.
