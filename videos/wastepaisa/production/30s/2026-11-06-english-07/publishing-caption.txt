Why do recyclers pay more for some bottle loads than others?

Keep clear bottles apart; coloured ones often sell for little. Leave out other trash; buyers grade loads by how much is really PET. Then crush them; loose bottles are mostly air, and air costs transport.

Example (prices vary): say 400 kilos sell for ₹8,800. Buying, transport and labour leave about ₹1,500.
