This scrap has a market.

Playgrounds need cushioned floors. Can a scrap tyre become one? Crumb rubber already goes into playground floors and tiles. Shred tyres, pull steel with magnets, blow off fibre, sieve the crumb. Mix the crumb with a binder and press it into tiles.

Say 50 tyres make 100 tiles that sell for ₹35,000. After crumb, binder, labour and press costs, about ₹9,000 is left. (The batch figures are assumptions for illustration, built from listed asking prices: crumb about ₹20–30/kg, PU binder about ₹190–295/kg, 25 mm rubber tiles about ₹120–130 per sq ft. They are not a promise of income, and a tile line needs a crumbing plant or bought-in crumb plus a powered press.)
