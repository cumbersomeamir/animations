## Research (accessed 21 Nov 2026). Facts, listings and assumptions are kept apart
- **R1, NITI Aayog, "Enhancing Circular Economy of Waste Tyres in India"** (January 2026; primary, read via pdftotext):
  https://www.niti.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/Enhancing-Circular-Economy-of-Waste-Tyres-in-India.pdf
  - "Crumb rubber is extensively used in sports and recreational infrastructure, such as running tracks, synthetic turf infill, and
    children's playground surfaces, where it provides cushioning, shock absorption, and slip resistance… It finds applications in flooring
    tiles…" Source for L2 and L3.
  - "Mechanical crumbing is a process in which waste tyres are physically ground into fine rubber particles known as crumb rubber… In
    India, ambient grinding is the prevalent practice due to relatively lower capital investment". Supports the "verified mechanical
    processing" in L4.
  - Context (notes only): about 1.53 MMT of end-of-life tyres go to crumb production, but only about 0.05 MMT of crumb goes to direct
    use in rubber goods; most is an intermediate for pyrolysis and reclaim. This is the opportunity gap; it is not narrated.
- **R2, Gradeall International (tyre-processing equipment maker), "Crumb rubber production from scrap tyres":**
  https://gradeall.com/crumb-rubber-production-scrap-tyres/
  - Process steps: primary twin-shaft shredding; magnetic steel removal (drum or overband magnets); granulation; air-aspiration fibre
    removal (in stages); screening/sieving into sizes. Source for the order and the four actions in L4.
  - "Approximately 100 to 110 passenger car tyres yield one tonne of crumb rubber"; steel is about 10–15 % and fabric about 5–10 % of a
    tyre. Basis for "50 tyres ≈ 0.5 t of crumb" in the batch.
- **R3, crumb asking prices (listing directories and search-result listing snippets, 21 Nov 2026):** 1–4 mm crumb at about ₹20–30/kg
  (for example ₹24.50, ₹25, ₹26, ₹30/kg).
  https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/crumb-rubber.html · https://www.tradeindia.com/products/crumb-rubber-c12073507.html ·
  https://royalrubbers.tradeindia.com/black-crumb-rubber-granules-8087081.html
- **R4, PU binder asking prices (listings, 21 Nov 2026):** polyurethane binder for rubber flooring/granules about ₹190–295/kg (₹190
  two-component, ₹220 single-component, up to ₹290–295 for branded grades). https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/polyurethane-binders.html ·
  https://m.indiamart.com/jbsspuchem/polyurethane-binder.html
  - Manufacturing use: Dow markets VORAMER binder systems specifically for moulded rubber tiles and mats
    (https://www.dow.com/en-us/pdp.voramer-rubber-molded-binder-systems.voramerrubbermoldedz.html). A binder supplier's
    specification seen during research (Leeson Polyurethanes, "12% binder addition" for a granule wear layer) is indicative only: its URL
    was not retained. The batch assumes about 8 % binder by weight.
- **R5, selling examples (listings, 21 Nov 2026):**
  - Sunflex Recycling Pvt Ltd (Ahmedabad), "SBR Gym Rubber Tiles 500mm × 500mm", ₹169.47 per piece, MOQ 25, described as manufactured
    from recycled tyre crumb; the listed thickness is inconsistent on the page, so it is a floor price for a thin tile, not a 25 mm one.
    https://www.exportersindia.com/product-detail/sunflex-gym-rubber-tiles-2622689.htm
  - 25 mm rubber floor/gym tiles about ₹120–130 per sq ft (one 500 × 500 mm tile ≈ 2.69 sq ft ≈ ₹320–350); EPDM-topped 25 mm tiles
    higher (≈ ₹245/sq ft). https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/interlocking-rubber-tiles.html ·
    https://www.tradeindia.com/products/rubber-tiles-c8245808.html
  - All are asking prices on B2B directories, not verified transactions.

### Illustrative batch (on screen as "ASSUMED"): 100 tiles of 500 × 500 × 25 mm (~5 kg each) from one small press line
| Item | ₹ | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Crumb ≈ 500 kg (≈ 50 car tyres' worth, R2) × ₹22/kg | 11,000 | R3 range (₹20–30/kg), low end because a buyer of bulk crumb |
| PU binder ≈ 45 kg (≈ 8 %) × ₹200/kg | 9,000 | R4 range; ≈ 35 % of costs (shown as "≈35% OF COSTS") |
| Labour (mixing, moulding, pressing, demoulding, curing, packing) | 3,000 | assumption |
| Press + other: colour ₹1,000, power ₹1,000, equipment share ₹1,000 | 3,000 | assumption; press, moulds and mixer ≈ ₹6 lakh amortised over many batches (notes only) |
| **Costs per batch** | **26,000** | arithmetic |
| **Revenue:** 100 tiles × ₹350 (≈ ₹130/sq ft) | **35,000** | R5 range for 25 mm tiles |
| **Left before overheads** | **≈ 9,000** | arithmetic; about 26 % of revenue |
- **Revenue vs profit:** ₹35,000 is what the buyer pays (revenue); about ₹9,000 is left after the listed costs.
- **Scale and setting:** this is a batch from an industrial crumbing line and a powered press, not a hand-tool home business. The crumb
  is bought in from a crumbing plant or made on one; the press line needs capital (≈ ₹6 lakh assumed).
- **Not modelled:** unsold stock, rejects, transport, GST, testing and certification, rent, and the time to win buyers. No income is
  promised, and nothing implies lakhs from one tyre.
