Scrap price. Ride price.

Which old cycles are worth fixing? A Mumbai shop has sold over 100 refurbished cycles. Fix the ones with straight frames; strip the rest for parts.

Say ten scrap cycles cost ₹4,500; fixing seven costs ₹7,000. Seven sold at ₹2,000, plus scrap from three wrecks: about ₹3,500 left. And every wreck is a cheap spare-parts shop. (The batch figures are assumptions for illustration, not market prices or a promise.)

The Mumbai shop: Cycle Revolution, Andheri, has sold over 100 repaired and refurbished bicycles at ₹250 to ₹2,500 (a new one costs about ₹7,000); bicycles that can't be fixed are dismantled for spare parts (Free Press Journal, 5 Jan 2024)
