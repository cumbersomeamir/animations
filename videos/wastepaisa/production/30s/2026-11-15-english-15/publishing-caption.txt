One kilo short on fifty is two percent of your money. By law, a scale used in trade must be verified and stamped. Check the stamp's year, the certificate, and the verified test weights traders must keep.

Example: say 500 kilos sell for ₹15,000; after buying and transport, ₹900 is left. A 2% light scale takes ₹300 of that. Check the scale before you check the price.
