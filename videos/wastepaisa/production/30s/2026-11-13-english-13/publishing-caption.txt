Why does a mill pay ₹8 more a kilo for some iron?

The answer is inside the furnace. Thin, rusty sheet oxidises away; thick, clean pieces return more steel. So mills pay by grade: punchings top, light scrap last. Keep heavy pieces apart, dry and free of dirt.

Example (rates change): same truck, same trip cost, but a tonne of punchings brings ₹8,000 more. Mills pay for the steel they get back.

Rates: Jalna mill rate card as published on 13 Nov 2026 (punchings ₹39,500/t, light melting scrap ₹31,500/t, ex GST)
