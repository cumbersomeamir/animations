Why a paper cup isn't just paper.

Many disposable cups have a thin plastic lining that helps stop liquid soaking through the paper. That mixed construction can make recycling more complicated: some facilities can process certain cups, others can't. Some cups use other barrier coatings, so check your local recycler's rules before putting one in the recycling.
