These flowers sell twice.

First as offerings. Then as incense. At Ujjain's Mahakal temple, women turned its flowers into over 30 million incense sticks. Dry the flowers, grind them, add a binder, and mould cones.

Assume a hundred kilos of free flowers make 300 packs. After binder, packing and labour, about ₹1,300 is left. Ten rupees more per pack adds ₹2,700. (The batch figures are assumptions for illustration, not market prices or a promise.)
