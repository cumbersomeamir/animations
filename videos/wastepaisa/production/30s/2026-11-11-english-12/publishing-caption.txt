Same weight. Different value.

So what are buyers paying for? A magnet only catches iron and the cheapest grades. It's nickel: 304 has at least 8%; cheaper grades, far less. So collect 304 offcuts and keep one grade per sack.

Example (rates change): say 500 kilos fetch ₹69,000; buying, sorting and transport leave about ₹7,500. Buyers pay for proven grade and volume.
