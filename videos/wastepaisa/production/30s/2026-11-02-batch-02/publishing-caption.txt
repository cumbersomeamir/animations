Why one aluminium can can become another can.

An empty aluminium can is still useful metal. At recycling facilities, cans are sorted and shredded, then heat removes paints and coatings before the metal is melted, cast and rolled into sheet for new products, including new cans. A can isn't pure aluminium: the body and the lid are different alloys, and the inks and coatings have to be processed too.

Keep cans empty, follow your local sorting rules, and check with your scrap collector. Recycling routes and prices vary by location.
