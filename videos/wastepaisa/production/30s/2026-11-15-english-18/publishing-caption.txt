Banner to bag.

Can an old banner become a bag people buy? Bags cut from used truck tarps have sold in Zurich since 1993. Cut away faded or torn parts, then cut and stitch the panels.

Assume twenty totes; banner, labour and handles cost ₹1,700. If sixteen sell for ₹150 each, about ₹700 is left. The banner is cheap; labour and unsold bags decide the margin. (All rupee figures are assumptions for illustration, not market prices.)
