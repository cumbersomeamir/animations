Coconut shells into charcoal briquettes: where's the money?

Collect discarded shells and dry them; wet loads waste fuel. A drum kiln chars them with little air, then crush, bind with starch and press.

Example (prices vary): say one batch makes 350 kilos: ₹10,500 in sales. Shells, labour, energy, transport and equipment leave about ₹1,500, if shells come cheap.
