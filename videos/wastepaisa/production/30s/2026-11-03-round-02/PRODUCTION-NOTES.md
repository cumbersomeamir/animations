## Sources (accessed 2026-11-03)
- **B1:** ReMA Specifications Circular, *Honey: Yellow Brass Scrap* (effective 2022-07-14), https://www.isrispecs.org/orpheus_resource/yellow-brass-scrap/.
  "Shall consist of mixed yellow brass solids, including brass castings, rolled brass, rod brass," and goes on to tubing and
  miscellaneous yellow brasses **including plated brass**. It excludes manganese- and aluminium-bronze, unsweated radiators,
  **iron**, and excessively dirty or corroded material.
- **B2:** ReMA Specifications Circular, Nonferrous scrap category, https://www.isrispecs.org/orpheus_resource_categories/nonferrous-scrap/.
  Its identification checklist lists **"Brass Base" under Non-Magnetic** scrap categories.
- **B3:** USGS, *Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025: Zinc*, https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-zinc.pdf. Zinc
  consumed in the US went mostly to galvanized steel, "followed by brass and bronze".
- **B4:** USGS, *Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025: Copper*, https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-copper.pdf. Annual
  average copper prices moved 286.7 → 432.3 → 410.8 → 395.3 → 430 (estimated) ¢/lb over 2020–2024, so prices change. "Brass and wire-rod
  mills accounted for approximately 85% of the total copper recovered from scrap." Numbers are evidence only and are not shown on screen.
- **B5 (secondary, corroboration):** Copper Development Association and ASM alloy listings for C84800 cast semi-red brass, which list
  "plumbing fixtures, faucets and general hardware" and a magnetic permeability of 1. These were seen in search results only;
  copper.org returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch. Designing Buildings Wiki, "Brass" (https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/Brass),
  lists door furniture and architectural ironmongery among brass uses. New England Today, "To tell if your brass objects are
  solid or plated, touch them with a magnet" (https://newengland.com/living/how-to/to-tell-if-your-brass-objects-are-solid-or-plated-touch-them-with-a-magnet/).
