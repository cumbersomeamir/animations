The valuable metal hiding in old taps and door handles: brass.

Many old taps, door handles and fittings are brass, an alloy of copper and zinc. A magnet helps you check: brass won't stick, but brass-plated steel will. Clean brass is graded separately from brass with steel parts attached, so sort them apart, then compare quotes from a few local buyers, because prices change.
