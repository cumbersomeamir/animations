Sell a used box by the kilo, or as a box?

Keep them dry: damp cardboard loses strength, so it goes to scrap. Stack intact boxes by size and peel off old labels for resale. Movers and online sellers come back for the same sizes.

Example (prices vary): say resale and scrap bring in ₹9,200; boxes, transport, storage and labour leave about ₹2,500.
