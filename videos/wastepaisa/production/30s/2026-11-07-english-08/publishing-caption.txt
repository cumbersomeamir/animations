Why can a few bits of broken mug cost you a whole glass load?

Keep clear, green and brown bottles apart; clear allows little other colour. Pull out caps and ceramics: ceramic melts hotter than glass and leaves flaws. A processing plant crushes, screens and sorts it into cullet.

Example (prices vary): say ten tonnes sell for ₹90,000; collection, labour, transport and processing leave about ₹10,000.

Sources:
Glass Manufacturing Industry Council, Cullet Quality: The Hidden Key to Efficient Glass Production (ceramics melt at higher temperatures than container glass and cause inclusions)
https://gmic.org/cullet-quality-the-hidden-key-to-efficient-glass-production/
Container glass cullet specifications (PAS 101: clear Grade A allows 4% other colours; ceramics are critical contaminants)
