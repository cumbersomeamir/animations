Same tyre. Different buyer.

One rolls again; the other becomes crumb. India retreads about ten million tyres a year. Retreaders need a sound casing: no cuts or separations. So inspect each tyre, and sort casings from crumb tyres.

Hypothetical: say ten tyres give six casings: after buying, sorting and transport, about ₹1,300 is left. Sold all as crumb, the same ten lose money. (Illustrative numbers, not market prices.)
