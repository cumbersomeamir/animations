Why will a factory buy one old shirt as a rag, but not another?

Rags must absorb oil: keep cotton, drop all-synthetic. Cut off buttons and zips, then grade by colour. A workshop cuts even pieces and bales them for factories and garages.

Example (prices vary): say 300 kilos of rags sell for ₹13,500; purchase, rejects, labour and transport leave about ₹2,500.
