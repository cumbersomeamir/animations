## Research (accessed 8 Nov 2026)
- **W1, US Commercial Item Description A-A-2522A, "Rags, Wiping (Cotton and Cotton-Synthetic)"**, 19 Jan 1994 (replaces cancelled
  Federal Spec DDD-R-30): https://everyspec.com/COMML_ITEM_DESC/A-A-02000_A-A-02999/A-A-2522A_5776/ and the defect table at
  https://broomsbrushesmopssponges.emilspec.com/A-A-2522/page7.html.
  - **Scope:** "cotton and cotton-synthetic blended wiping rags made from unused or reclaimed fabrics".
  - **Grades:** Grade A, 2.0–7.0 oz/yd², white only or mixed colours; Grade B, mixed colours.
  - **Defects:**
    - (p) "Buttons, hooks, eyes, closed safety pins, or any other metallic or plastic fittings".
    - (q) rags "that have not been thoroughly washed, rinsed, and sanitized".
    - (r) "Rags containing fiber content labels that declare 100 percent synthetic fiber".
    - (s) foreign or dangerous objects such as nails, pins, needles and glass, counted as three major defects.
- **W2, State of Louisiana statewide contract, "Wiping Rags" (RFx 300024614),**
  https://wwwcfprd.doa.louisiana.gov/osp/lapac/agency/pdf/8835300-02.docx.
  - "All rags are to be clean and dry, free of buttons, pins, hazardous or abrasive material…"
  - Rags must be free from objectionable odour or mildew.
  - Evidence that institutional and industrial buyers purchase rags to a spec.
- **W3, SMART (Secondary Materials and Recycled Textiles Association),** community presentation
  (https://www.smartasn.org/SMARTASN/assets/File/resources/SMART_community_presentation.pdf).
  - Sorted textiles are about 45 % usable clothing, 20 % fibre conversion and 30 % wiping cloth, graded by quality, condition and type.
  - Wet or mildewed items, or items used with solvents, cannot be recycled.
- **W4, absorbency:** Cotton Incorporated, as quoted in Sciencing, "Why Is Cotton Absorbent?" (https://sciencing.com/cotton-absorbent-6662538).
  - Cotton's cellulose is hydrophilic and pulls liquid in by capillary action.
  - Oil- or petroleum-based man-made fabrics are hydrophobic and "less likely to absorb moisture".
  - The unverified "27× its weight" figure is not used.
- **P1, Indian rag asking prices** (IndiaMART / TradeIndia listings), white priced above coloured:
  - White T-shirt wiping rags ₹60/kg; white banian waste ₹95–100/kg.
  - Coloured banian ₹50–60/kg; Nagpur banyan waste ₹40/kg (200 kg minimum).
  - Mixed cotton banian ₹32/kg; "industrial cleaning" rags ₹20/kg.
  - Sources: https://dir.indiamart.com/impcat/wiping-rags.html, https://www.tradeindia.com/manufacturers/cotton-rags.html.
- **P2, used-garment buying prices:**
  - Household kabadi rate ₹2–6/kg (Kabadidost, reviewed July 2026, https://kabadidost.in/scrap-rates).
  - Plain or mixed bulk used-clothing lots ₹10–35/kg (https://www.exportersindia.com/indian-suppliers/used-clothing.htm).
- **P3, labour benchmark:** Delhi unskilled minimum wage ₹710/day (see context 17).
