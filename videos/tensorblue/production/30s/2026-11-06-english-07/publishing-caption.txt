Two people tap Buy on the last seat at the same moment. Who gets it?

A careless app checks first, both see it free, and it sells twice. A well-built system claims it in one step: only if it's still free. The database runs the claims one at a time; the second finds it taken. So one person gets the ticket, and the other sees "sold out".

Not every app does this, but the fix is simple: check and claim together.

The demo is a real SQLite run with a made-up event: check-then-sell gave seat K12 two tickets; the one-step claim (UPDATE … WHERE buyer IS NULL) changed 1 row for the first buyer and 0 for the second.
