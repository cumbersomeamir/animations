No GPS in this tunnel. Why is the dot still moving?

Your map is guessing, on purpose. It keeps your last speed and heading. That's dead reckoning. Sensors can catch curves, and map matching keeps it on the road.

But guesses drift. In our simulation, the dot ran ninety meters ahead. Some tunnels add Waze beacons, so phones still get location. Building a map? Grow the blue circle while guessing, then snap back to GPS.

Our simulation (our own code, not any app's): a car enters a 600 m tunnel with a gentle S-bend and slows from 60 to 45 km/h halfway through. A guess that only keeps the last speed and heading went 150 m off the road at the bend. Adding a turn sensor and snapping to the road kept it on the road, but it ran 92 m ahead of the slowed car until the next GPS fix. In a version with roadside beacons every 100 m (an illustrative spacing), it never ran more than 32 m ahead. The blue circle in the video is our own simple uncertainty model.
