You deleted this photo on your phone. Why is it still on your laptop?

Some deletes only remove this device's copy. The cloud keeps its own. A synced delete is different: every device removes its copy. Even then, it waits in the trash, often for thirty days. And older backups can keep a copy until they're removed.

For permanent removal: sync the delete, empty the trash, check your backups.

The demo uses a made-up sync system built from real folders (synced with rsync): a device-only removal left copies in the cloud and on the laptop; a synced delete left one in the trash and one in an older backup; only emptying the trash and removing that backup left no copy.
