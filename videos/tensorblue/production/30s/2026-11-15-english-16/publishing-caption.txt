Your route just changed, and you haven't even reached the jam. How did the app know first?

To the app, streets are a graph. Each segment costs its travel time now. Live traffic just raised this one from two minutes to seven. It reruns a shortest-time search from your spot: old route fourteen, detour ten. Google says Maps reroutes using live and predicted traffic.

So a reroute is fresh travel times plus a fast search, rerun as you drive.

Our test city is made up: we ran Dijkstra's shortest-path search on it before and after one street's time went from 2 to 7 minutes. The old route became 14 minutes and the search switched to a 10-minute detour. Real apps don't publish their exact routing algorithms; this shows the general idea.
