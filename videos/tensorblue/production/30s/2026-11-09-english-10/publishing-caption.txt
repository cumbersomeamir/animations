This window is private. So who can still see this visit?

Private mode mainly changes what this device keeps. Close the window, and its history and cookies aren't kept. Downloads and bookmarks you save still stay.

But the website still sees the visit, and so can whoever runs your network, like a school, employer or internet provider.

So it helps on a shared device. It doesn't make you invisible online.

The demo is a real Chromium run with a made-up shop site: after closing, the browser's History and Cookies files kept only the normal window's visit, while the site's log and the network log recorded both the normal and the private visit.
