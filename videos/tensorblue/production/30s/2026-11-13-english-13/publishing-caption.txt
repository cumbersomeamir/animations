Wrong card. The screen moved on. Undo brings it back. How does the app remember?

Every change saves an entry: which card, where it was, where it went. Undo takes the newest entry and runs it backwards. History is limited: Office keeps the last hundred actions by default. Actions that leave the app are harder: Gmail's Undo Send lasts seconds.

Undo is a short history played in reverse: use it soon, before you hit send.

The tasks board in the video is a generic example. Gmail lets you pick a send cancellation period of 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds.
