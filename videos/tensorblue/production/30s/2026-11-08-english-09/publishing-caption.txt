It works on your friend's phone, but not on yours. Why?

Phones get a site's address from a DNS resolver, which caches it. The site moved servers. Your resolver keeps the old address until its time-to-live runs out. Your friend's resolver asked after the move and got the new one.

Compare both phones' addresses. Old one on yours? Stale cache. Same? Look elsewhere.

Moving a site? Lower the TTL first.

The demo is a real run with a made-up site (shop.example) and two caching resolvers, queried with dig: after the move, one resolver still answered the old address from its cache (TTL 27 s left), while a resolver asking for the first time got the new one.
