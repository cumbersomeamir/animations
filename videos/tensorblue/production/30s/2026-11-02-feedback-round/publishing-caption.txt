Should retrying a payment charge you twice?

If the connection drops after you tap Pay, the app may not know whether the payment went through. A well-designed checkout sends each purchase with one unique "idempotency key". If a retry arrives with the same key, the server returns the saved result instead of charging again. A new key is treated as a new request — so keep the same key for retries of one purchase.

One purchase, one key, one receipt. (The checkout, keys and receipt numbers in the video are an illustrative demo, not a specific app or bank.)
