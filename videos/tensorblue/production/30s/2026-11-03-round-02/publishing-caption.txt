Why does a photo get blurry when you zoom in?

Most photos are raster images: a fixed grid of tiny coloured squares called pixels. Zoom in and each pixel just gets bigger, so edges turn into steps; many apps smooth those blocks, which looks blurry. A vector logo (like an SVG) stores shapes as points and curves, so it is redrawn sharply at any size. Zooming can't add detail a photo never captured — which is why the vector format is the better choice for logos.
