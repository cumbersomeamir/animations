This QR code lost a corner, and it still scans.

Every QR code carries extra recovery data: error correction. If squares get torn or dirty, the scanner rebuilds the missing data. Level L recovers about seven percent of its data; level H, about thirty. But error correction can't fix the three corner squares scanners look for.

Printing one? Choose a higher level, and keep those corners clear.

The demo is real: the same tear was applied to a level L and a level H code and read with open-source zbar and OpenCV. Level H still scanned, level L did not, and a small tear on a corner square stopped both. The menu card and example.com address are made up for the demo.
