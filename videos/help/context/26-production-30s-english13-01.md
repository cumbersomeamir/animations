## Sources (accessed 2026-11-13) and claim status
- **M1** University of Missouri, Interdisciplinary Center for Food Security, "Power Up Your Pantry — Supporting client choice" (guide sheet PDF,
  https://foodsecurity.missouri.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/PUYP-Guide-Sheet_Client-Choice-FINAL.pdf ; text via pdftotext; local SHA-256
  480361a6…b2ddaf; "based on survey and other research conducted by the Interdisciplinary Center for Food Security"): "The client choice model
  provides clients with an experience similar to using the grocery store, as opposed to a more traditional food pantry experience where food boxes
  are pre-packaged" · "In the past, the common assumption was that clients would take too much… found that these assumptions were largely
  unfounded. In fact, one of the challenges of the client choice is clients often do not take ENOUGH food!" · "many will take less food than they
  need, often because they believe that other clients need the food more than they do." · "Reduce food waste. Clients will avoid taking foods they
  don't want or can't use and will be much less likely to throw food away or let it spoil." · "pantries can help clients feel more respected and
  dignified." · Central Pantry (Columbia, MO): clients "avoid foods they don't like or can't eat because of health concerns. This also results in
  less food waste".
- **T1** Martin KS, Wu R, Wolff M, Colantonio AG, Grady J. "A novel food pantry program: food security, self-sufficiency, and diet-quality
  outcomes." Am J Prev Med 2013;45(5):569-75, doi 10.1016/j.amepre.2013.06.012, PMID 24139769 (abstract read via NCBI E-utilities efetch; the
  PubMed web page is cookie-walled for the fetch tool): randomized parallel-group study, 228 adults from traditional pantries, Freshplace n=113 vs
  control n=115, 12 months. "The Freshplace intervention included a client-choice pantry, monthly meetings with a project manager to receive
  motivational interviewing, and targeted referrals to community services. Control group participants went to traditional food pantries where they
  received bags of food." "Over 1 year, Freshplace members were less than half as likely to experience very low food security… all outcomes p<0.01."
- **N1** NORC at the University of Chicago, "Feeding America's Child & Family Choice Program" (evaluation commissioned by Feeding America with
  Morgan Stanley funding), https://www.norc.org/research/projects/feeding-americas-child-family-choice-program.html : "clients using full choice

  food waste" (cross-sectional) · "Preliminary analysis… statistically significant association… between lower levels of food waste and pantries
