# 09 — Educational Pilot 01: What delivery video evidence proves — and what it does not

Deliverable: `videos/help/education/pilot-01/help-education-pilot-01.mp4` (15.000 s, 1080×1920, H.264/AAC, 30 fps).
Source of truth for timing: `education/pilot-01/src/timeline.json` (shared by the 3D scene and the audio mixer).

## Learning objective (one concept)
A delivery clip can document **one handoff inside its frame**. Check **when and where** it was filmed — and remember it
cannot show what happens **after** the clip. Takeaway: *one piece of evidence, not the whole story.*

Deliberately narrowed: identity of the recipient, footage authenticity in depth, and use of all funds are true limits too
(see `SOURCES.md`), but each is its own lesson. In 15 s we teach the *frame boundary* + *when/where check* + *after the clip*.
