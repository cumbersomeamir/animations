## Sources (accessed 2026-11-18) and claim status
- **A1** The Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969 (18 of 1969), copy hosted by the Office of the Registrar General, India on censusindia.gov.in
  (local SHA-256 prefix b15a3fc5…): s.8(1)(a) at home "the head of the house … the nearest relative of the head present"; s.8(1)(b) "in a hospital …
  the medical officer in charge or any person authorised by him"; s.12 "give, free of charge, to the person who gives information … an extract";
  s.13(1) within thirty days "on payment of such late fee"; s.13(2) thirty days to one year "only with the written permission of the prescribed
  authority and on payment of the prescribed fee"; s.13(3) after one year "only on an order made by a magistrate"; s.14 a child registered "without a
  name" — the parent "shall within the prescribed period give information regarding the name".
- **R1** Model Registration of Births and Deaths Rules, 1999 (ORGI copy; local SHA-256 prefix 78d1b93b…): rule 5(3) "information … shall be given
  within twenty one days from the date of birth"; rule 5(1) Form No. 1 for a birth, "signature/thumb impression of the informant"; rule 8(4) for
  institutional births the nearest relative "may collect the extract from the officer or person in charge of the institution"; rule 9 late fees only
  after the rule-5 period; rule 10 name "within 12 months from the date of registration", later up to 15 years with a late fee.
- **P1** PRS Legislative Research, Bill Summary, The Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Bill, 2023 (26 Jul 2023; local SHA-256 prefix
  2988e020…): "requires the use of birth and death certificates to prove the date and place of birth for persons born on or after this Bill comes
  into effect … (i) admission to an educational institution, (ii) preparation of voter list, (iii) appointment to a government post"; the medical
  officer in charge of a hospital "must report the birth"; the Registrar "must provide the certificate … within seven days".
- **N1** The Quint, 26 Sep 2023 (local SHA-256 prefix 026fac9e…): "Starting from 1 October 2023, birth certificates will serve as the primary
  document … admission into educational institutions …", reporting the Home Ministry's notification of the date (S.O. 4058(E), per a summary listing;
  the e-Gazette PDF failed TLS verification and was not bypassed).
- **J1** Gujarat High Court, SCA 3502/2025, order 24 Apr 2025 (courtbook copy; local SHA-256 prefix 12cfa147…): the 2023 amendment moved registration
  after one year from the Judicial Magistrate to the Executive Magistrate (District Magistrate); challenge noted.
- **G1** The Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Act, 2026 (No. 12 of 2026), Gazette of India Extraordinary, 6 Aug 2026 (LiveLaw copy;
  local SHA-256 prefix ad06d865…): new s.13(3) — after one year but within two years, "only on an order made by a District Magistrate or Sub-Divisional
