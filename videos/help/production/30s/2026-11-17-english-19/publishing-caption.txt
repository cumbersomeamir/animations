That odd-looking grain in your ration rice isn't plastic.
Viral videos claimed it was.
So what is it?

A fortified kernel: rice flour plus nutrients, shaped like a grain.
It adds iron, folic acid and vitamin B12.
Nagaland's food lab tested a viral sample: fortified rice, not plastic.
Since 2024, government schemes supply it.

So don't pick them out: cook them with the rest.
