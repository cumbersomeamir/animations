A free wheelchair can still be the wrong gift.
Too wide, and they lean to one side. Too deep, and they can't sit upright.
So what makes a chair fit?

WHO guidance: assess each person first, then fit the chair.
The seat should be as wide as the hips.
Behind the knee, leave about two fingers of space.
Then train the user, and check back later.

Before donating one, ask a verified programme what size and condition they need.
