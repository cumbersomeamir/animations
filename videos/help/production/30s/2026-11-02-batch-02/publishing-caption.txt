After a flood, not every family needs the same things. One may need medicine; another, transport or temporary rent.

When local shops are working and essentials are available, well-run cash assistance lets people choose what they need. If markets are disrupted, supplies may be more useful. Before giving, check what a trusted local organisation is asking for.
