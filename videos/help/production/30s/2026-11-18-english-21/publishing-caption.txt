Register a birth within twenty-one days, and it's free.
Wait over a year, and you'll need a magistrate's order.
So why does this one paper matter?

For children born since late 2023, it proves age for school admission.
Hospitals must report births; at home, the family tells the registrar.
No name yet? Register the birth now; add the name later.

So the most useful gift may be help with one form, on time.
