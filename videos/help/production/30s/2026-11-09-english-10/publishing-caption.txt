Rice: yes. Soap: no.

In the US, SNAP benefits are only for food.
Soap, toothpaste and period products aren't food, so benefits can't buy them.

Food banks often ask for toiletries too. Check their list: deodorant, toothbrushes, period products.

Fictional example: a donor adds a sealed toothbrush and deodorant.

Food help often stops at food. Toiletries help fill that gap.
