No address, no access? For Aadhaar, there's an official route.

Many proofs of address assume a home:
an electricity bill, a water bill, a rent agreement.
So what if you sleep in a shelter?

The 2024 Aadhaar list also accepts a certificate on its standard format,
signed by a municipal councillor, a tehsildar or a gazetted officer.

It's not automatic, but it's a real route: ask the councillor's office.

To donate essentials in Lucknow, download Help.
