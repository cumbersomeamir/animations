A fresh salad or a tin: which one can you donate?

Fictional example: a donor packs a food bank bag. First, read the label.
Use by is about safety, on food that spoils quickly.
Best before is about quality, on long-life food like tins.

Food banks often want long-life food, and some ask for months left on the date.

So the tinned food goes in: sealed, and well within its date.
