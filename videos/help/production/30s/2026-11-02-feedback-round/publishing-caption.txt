Why can having less money make the same essentials cost more?

A small packet can cost more per kilo than a bulk bag — but the bulk bag needs more money up front. When rent and bills have already used the cash, the cheaper-per-kilo option can stay out of reach. Researchers call costs like this the "poverty premium". It's about cash on hand, not about bad choices.
