Can a child actually use this school bag?

Check every zip: open it, close it.
Tug both straps. They should hold, not fray.
Match the size: a small child needs a smaller bag.
Clean it: empty the pockets, wipe it, let it dry.

Then ask the organisation which bags and ages they need.

Last test: would you be happy to receive it? Then hand it over ready to use: a gift, not a leftover.
