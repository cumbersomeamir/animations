On its own, a new iron pot cooks nothing.
The pot lasts for years. Flour, dal and gas run out daily.
So what keeps a community kitchen cooking?

In Lucknow, Dihadi Kitchen has relied on donated rations and money.
Amritsar's Golden Temple langar uses about thirty gas refills daily.
Staples can be given in kind; fuel usually needs money.

So ask first: staples to drop off, or fuel to fund?

To donate essentials in Lucknow, download Help.
