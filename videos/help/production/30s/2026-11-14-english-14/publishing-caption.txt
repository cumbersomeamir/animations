This ration card worked at one shop only.
Move for work, and your subsidised grain stayed behind.

Getting a new card took months. So what changed?
India's One Nation One Ration Card.
Same card, in ration shops across India.
One fingerprint check confirms it's you.
Back home, the family still gets their share.

So if someone works far from home, tell them: their ration travels too.

To donate essentials in Lucknow, download Help.

Source:
- Department of Food & Public Distribution, Government of India, via Press Information Bureau: "One Nation, One Ration Card — Nationwide Portability of Ration" (April 2023). Migrant beneficiaries can get their foodgrains from any fair price shop of their choice in all States and Union Territories with their existing ration card, after biometric authentication on an ePoS device, while the family back home gets their part of the entitled ration: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2023/apr/doc2023427188501.pdf
