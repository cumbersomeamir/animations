This fridge has no cashier, and it solves two problems.

Good food going to waste, and neighbours who need it.

So how does an open fridge stay stocked and safe?
One real model: the UK's Community Fridge Network.
Local businesses and residents share surplus food.
A host gives it a home. Volunteers run it, with food-safety rules.

What helps isn't a big photo. It's safe, suitable food that's there again tomorrow.
