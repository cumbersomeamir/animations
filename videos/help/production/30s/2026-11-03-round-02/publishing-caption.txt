Why is good food wasted while families nearby miss meals?

Often it's not a lack of food at all — it's timing, storage and transport. Surplus becomes available at the wrong hour, there's no cold space to hold it, and no vehicle free to move it before it spoils. Food-rescue groups bridge that gap with timely pickups, safe storage and planned routes.

Before donating food, ask a verified local group what it accepts, and when — instead of dropping off things it can't use.
