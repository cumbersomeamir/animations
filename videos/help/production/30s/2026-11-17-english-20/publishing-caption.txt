Past forty-five, the needle's eye starts to blur.
For close-up work, that blur can cost income.
Can glasses really fix that?

An Assam trial gave tea pickers over forty near-vision glasses.
They picked about a fifth more tea than those still waiting.
Presbyopia is a focusing error; the right lens corrects it.
Simple community eye tests find who needs which power.

So give the test and the right glasses, not just any pair.
