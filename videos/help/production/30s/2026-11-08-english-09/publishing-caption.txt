It looks brand new. So why can't anyone wear it?

Fictional example: a shoe collection sorts donations.
Check one: both shoes. A single shoe can't be worn.
Check two: the sole. Bend it. Old soles can crack, even if never worn.
Check three: the size inside, matched to what they asked for.

Pair, sole, size: three checks before shoes go in the box.
