Cash now, for winter blankets? Pause before you give.

Fictional example: a donor checks three things first.
Name: search it with words like scam or complaint.
Registration: look up its number on the official register.
Payment: never cash, gift cards or wire transfers. Pay by card or cheque, and keep a record.
