Even when treatment is free, the bus fare isn't.
Nor is losing a day's wages.
So what really gets people to care?

Among India's poorest, over a third of women call transport a big problem.
Pregnant women and sick infants get free transport to public hospitals, and home again.
Some charities also cover travel, or somewhere to stay.

Before you donate more things, ask a verified local group about travel help.

- National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5), India 2019-21, via the DHS Program data service: women aged 15-49 who said "having to take transport" is a big problem in getting treatment for themselves - 36.6% in the poorest fifth of households, 8.7% in the richest, 21.5% overall: https://api.dhsprogram.com/rest/dhs/data?countryIds=IA&surveyIds=IA2020DHS&indicatorIds=RH_PAHC_W_TRN&breakdown=all&f=json
- National Health Mission, Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK): free transport from home to health institutions, between facilities on referral, and drop back home, for pregnant women and sick infants: https://www.nhm.gov.in/showlink.php?id=178
- Press Information Bureau, 15 Dec 2021 (JSSK entitlements, expanded in 2013 to sick infants up to 1 year): https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1781676
