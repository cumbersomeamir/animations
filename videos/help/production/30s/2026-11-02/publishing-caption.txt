Before donating clothes, ask three questions:
1) What does the person actually need?
2) What size fits?
3) Will it work in the weather where they live?

A coat that's too small can't keep anyone warm. One clean, wearable item in the right size is often more useful than a bag of mixed leftovers. Ask first, then choose.
