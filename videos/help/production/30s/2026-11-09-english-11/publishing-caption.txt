Leftover pills in a donation bag? Take them out.

Food banks often list medicines and vitamins as things they can't accept.

Fictional example: a donor clears out a cupboard.
Unwanted or expired medicines go to a pharmacy, to be disposed of safely.
Sealed essentials, like tins and toiletries, go in the bag.

Medicines to the pharmacy. Essentials to the donation.

Download Help to donate essentials.
