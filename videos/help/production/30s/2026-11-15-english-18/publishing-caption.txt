Same sun, same home. Why is one room hotter?
A dark roof soaks up sunlight and passes heat inside.
Can white paint change that?

A cool roof reflects more sunlight, so less heat gets in.
In Ahmedabad, Mahila Housing Trust coats low-income homes with white reflective paint.
The city added cool roofs to its heat action plan.

Results vary, but suitable paint and labour are practical to fund.
