## 5. Educational accuracy (see `SOURCES.md`)
- One concept, three beats: a clip *can* show one handoff in its frame → check when and where it was filmed → it can't show after the clip → "One piece of evidence. Not the whole story." Each spoken claim maps to S1–S3.
- Not claimed: recipient identity, footage authenticity in depth, use of funds, impact, or anything about how Help verifies proof.
- ✓ marks illustrate *doing* a check, not that a real clip passed it.
- Dignity: the recipient is upright, same scale, smiling, not named, not filmed in distress.
