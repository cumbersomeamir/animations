# 11: Production format (durable; for future production rounds). Updated 2026-11-03

This is reference for later rounds. **No automation has been created.** Each round is started by the user.

## Channel and genre (locked)
- App: Meshline (`com.cumbersomeamir.meshline`). Repo `cumbersomeamir/animations`, branch `claude-video/meshline` only, workspace `videos/meshline/`.
- Genre: **communication, wireless connectivity, and communication without the internet.** Educational, not advertising.

## Visual skill (exact)
- **Transit Map**: https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/styles/transit-map/STYLE.md (module `styles/transit-map`).
- Read-only art direction. The raw STYLE.md fetched 2026-11-02 has sha256 `e6c62bc75071c86b67c30def595e919910d86245504c8c09882d151a3c719d1c`.
- Its engine and scripts were not cloned or run. The implementation is original Canvas2D code (`production/30s/2026-11-02/src/prod.mjs`).
- **Kept from the style:**
  - One printed sheet. Routes grow by pen tip, and stops pop with an overshoot and a ring.
  - The interchange lands with weight.
  - Line form carries meaning: solid = the signal route, hollow = the waiting siding, ink dots = walking transfer.
  - Printed labels with a paper halo. The title is printed on the map (cartouche).
  - A paper veil when one part must lead. The film closes on the whole network.
  - Tuned percussion, a two-note station chime and a felt-tip glide.
- **Adapted:**
  - Stock is charcoal `#111714` with electric-blue `#3157FF` ink and node-white `#F7F7F2` stations, the app palette instead of the style's off-white paper.
  - The camera is 2D (glide, push, pull-back); there is no WebGL depth of field.
  - Captions are screen-space and in a single language (English), not printed bilingual labels.

## Recurring scene rules
- One continuous sheet per film, with no cuts to slides.
- Every claim gets a physical map object (route, rack, queue, callout).
- Bars mean signal strength only. When "signal: full" is shown, all four bars are fully lit.
- Visual metaphors are labelled with the screen-space note "Illustrative map · not real network data". No invented numbers.
- Labels must never clip at frame edges during camera moves. Hide or veil them instead.

## Palette
`#111714` stock/charcoal, `#3157FF` ink, `#8EA2FF` soft blue, `#F7F7F2` node white, coral `#FF6A5C` only for problems (waiting, no spare). Sampled from `assets/icon.png`.

## Caption safe areas (1080x1920)
- Upper centre. Caption **ink** sits in y 350–470 (verified pixel-exactly by `captions_check.mjs`), with side margins ≥ 80 px.
- At most two short lines, Inter Display Bold 52 px, baselines at 398 and 458, on a dark plate (y 334–486).
- Split by word groups. Handoffs never overlap.
- Top 0–300 px stays unobstructed, with a screen-space darkening grade. Essential visuals stay above about y 1640, and the bottom has a grade too.
- Captions are screen-space, so they survive camera moves.

## Audio configuration
- Voice: local **Kokoro-82M ONNX** (`onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX`, Apache-2.0), Meshline blend **0.7 af_bella + 0.3 am_puck**, speed **0.98**, en-us. Generated by `education/pilot-01/audio/voice.py`. Not Piper, and no imitation of anyone.
- Music and SFX are synthesised originals (`production/.../audio/mix30.py` reuses `education/pilot-01/audio/mix.py` helpers). Voice sits upfront with ducking.
- Targets: −14 to −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP (measured with ebur128 peak=true), speech-band voice/bed ≥ +9 dB.
- One ASR pass (faster-whisper base.en, local) for word timings and caption splits. One ASR pass on the final MP4 to report WER.

## Delivery spec
- 30.000 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 900 frames, H.264 yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz stereo.
- Storyboard: hook 0–3 s, setup 3–8 s, mechanism 8–23 s, takeaway 23–29 s, end 29–30 s.
- Output folder: `videos/meshline/production/<len>/<date>/`, containing the MP4, sources, `publishing-caption.txt`, QA evidence and claims.

## Retained style recipe: "exact batch-02 Transit Map world" (re-confirmed 2026-11-03)
- **Sheet:** cached 2x bitmap of charcoal `#111714` stock, with 9000 tooth specks, 900 fibres, a 1-G blue grid at 5 % and a raking-light gradient. The sheet sits on a `#080B0A` table with a contact shadow.
- **Ink:**
  - Main routes are solid `#3157FF`, 0.24–0.26 G wide, with a paper shadow (3, 5 px).
  - Second-mode lines (relay rail and similar) are thin `#8EA2FF`, printed dashed until travelled.
  - Problems are coral `#FF6A5C` (gap ×, fizz sparks, "no contact yet").
- **Marks:**
  - Pen tips: white core, blue ring, soft halo.
  - Stops and stations pop with an overshoot plus a ring.
  - Interchanges and key stations land with weight.
  - Tokens: voice tokens with wave glyphs, or envelopes for messages. Vehicles are white capsules with windows in the line colour.
- **Type:** Inter Display ExtraBold / Bold and Inter SemiBold / Medium. Sheet labels have a paper halo stroke. The `label()` helper multiplies the outer alpha, so veils work.
- **Grammar:**
  - The cartouche title is printed for the hook.
  - Paper veils fade anything that has stopped mattering.
  - Printed callout blocks have a coloured spine.
  - Screen space is reserved for captions plus one instrument (the timeline/clock plate at y 1418–1574).
  - Grades cover the top 0–320 px and bottom 1640–1920 px.
  - The disclaimer sits at y ≈ 1612. The CTA signature (icon + wordmark) appears in the safe area at y ≈ 1460–1530 in the last 3 s, never in the bottom zone.
- **Camera:** 2D sheet camera (centre in G, zoom). One purposeful move per beat (push, ride a moving token with clamps so key rows stay below the caption band, pull back). No idle bobbing. Takeaway holds keep something moving, so a 2 s freeze detector stays clean.
- **Sound:** felt-tip glide under drawing, plucks and stops, a pop plus low thump on landings, the two-note station chime, rail clacks in pairs for vehicles, soft ticks for waiting clocks.
- **Bed:** a *quiet sustained pad with sparse glints and no repetitive beat* (user request 2026-11-03). Voice upfront (speech-band +17 dB or more).
- **Captions:** 52 px Inter Display Bold on a dark plate, ink measured inside y 350–470 and **x 90–990** (tightened 2026-11-03), at most 2 lines, word-group splits from ASR word timings. Single-letter anchors match exactly.
- **Right sidebar and bottom:** keep essential visuals at x ≤ 960 in the y 1000–1700 band, and above y 1640.
