Why can phones in one room talk directly, but not to a friend in another city? 📡

Nearby, Bluetooth radios can link devices directly, without the internet. But radio reach is limited by distance, walls and interference, so a friend in another city is far beyond it. Their messages need a route across the internet, hopping between routers.

Close by: a direct link. Far away: a network route.
