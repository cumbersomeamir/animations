Same distance, one concrete wall, and the link drops. Why?

Through thin drywall, it barely changes. Bluetooth's 2.4 GHz wave is absorbed and reflected by dense concrete. Lab tests measured about 30 dB lost through 20 centimetres. In open air, that's like 30 times the distance.

So a relay belongs in the doorway: two clear hops beat one through concrete.
