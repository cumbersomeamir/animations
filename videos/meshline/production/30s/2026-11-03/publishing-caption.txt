No connection all the way to your friend? A message can still move ✉️

Store-and-forward networking (delay-tolerant networking) keeps a message when no complete route exists right now. One node holds it, a travelling relay picks it up during a later contact, and hands it on when it meets the next node. Delivery is delayed and not guaranteed, but with compatible systems a missing live route doesn't always mean the message is lost.
