Full bars, but your message won't send? 📶

Signal bars are a rough estimate of the radio link between your phone and the nearby cell site. They don't show how busy that site is or how much capacity is left. At packed events, many phones share the same capacity, so data can wait even when the signal looks perfect.

Practical tip: before a big event, agree on a meeting spot with your group in case connectivity gets unreliable.
