Classic Bluetooth hops about 1,600 times a second. Why?

Its band is crowded with Wi-Fi and other gadgets. So connected devices hop together across 79 channels. Each stop is so brief that interference spoils only a few hops. Adaptive hopping also spots busy channels and skips them.

Keep your router's 2.4 GHz band at 20 MHz, to leave Bluetooth room.
