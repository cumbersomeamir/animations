Dead spot? Slide the phone a hand's width, and it can come back. Why?

The radio wave arrives by several paths, bouncing off walls. The echoes arrive out of sync. Here they add up, there they cancel. At Bluetooth's 2.4 GHz, one wave is about 12 centimetres long. So weak and strong spots can be just centimetres apart.

When a nearby link stalls, shift the phone a little before giving up.
