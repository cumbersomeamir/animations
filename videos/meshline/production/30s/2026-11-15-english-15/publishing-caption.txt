Every router here shares one channel. So who gets to talk?

When two transmit together, both get scrambled. So Wi-Fi listens first, then waits a random number of tiny slots. Pick the same number? They clash, and each doubles its waiting range. More neighbours on your channel means more waiting, whatever your internet plan.

Pick a quieter channel, or move to 5 GHz, with far more room.

For private nearby messaging, even offline, try Meshline.
