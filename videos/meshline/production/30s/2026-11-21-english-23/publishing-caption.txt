Why does your phone keep changing its Bluetooth address?

A fixed address would let scanners follow you around. So Android swaps in a new random address at least every 15 minutes. To a stranger's scanner, it is a new device each time. But paired devices share a secret key, so they still know it is you.

Your device name can still give you away, so keep it plain.
