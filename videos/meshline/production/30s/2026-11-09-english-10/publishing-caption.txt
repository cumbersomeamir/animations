Full Wi-Fi bars, but nothing loads?

Those bars measure only the radio link between your phone and the router. The internet is a second link, from the router out to your provider. When that link is down, the bars stay full and nothing gets through.

Quick check: if another device can't load either, it's the connection, not your phone. Then check the router's cables, or restart the router and modem.
