Your speed test looks fast; why does the video call freeze?

Calls send a steady stream of small packets, timed evenly. Missing ones are packet loss: the picture freezes or skips. Uneven arrivals are jitter: the app must wait and buffer.

Call networks target at most one lost packet per thousand, and jitter within 50 milliseconds.

Bandwidth and latency can look fine; check loss and jitter too.
