Your call won't connect, but a text still gets through?

A call needs a path held open for every second you talk. A text is one tiny burst, up to 160 characters. It needs less signal, and the network can hold it, then deliver it in a gap. So emergency guides say: to check in, text instead of calling.

Before a busy event, agree a meeting point, so one short text is enough.
