Internet down? A walkie-talkie can still carry your voice 📻

Two-way radios talk to each other directly over radio, with no website, mobile data or router involved. But both radios need compatible settings (like the same channel), and they only work within usable range. Hills, buildings and interference can weaken the signal, and some radio systems use repeaters to reach farther.

Offline messaging apps can use different radios and rules, so check their actual limits before you rely on them. No internet doesn't mean unlimited communication.
