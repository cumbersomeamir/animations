Why can a detour through another phone beat the direct path?

Radio signals weaken quickly with distance. Cut each hop in half, and the signal arrives about four times stronger. A weak link drops packets, so each one is sent again and again. Two strong hops need fewer sends in total.

But a relay also takes airtime, so a decent direct link still wins.
