How can sending slower make a message reach farther?

Far away, the signal sinks into the noise. Bluetooth's long range mode spends eight symbols on every bit. That extra code lets the receiver fix errors in a weak signal. The cost: eight times slower. The gain: about four times the range.

That is why long range modes suit short messages, not videos.
