It downloads in seconds; why does sending a video take forever?

Many connections have a wide lane down and a narrow lane up. The US broadband benchmark: 100 megabits down, 20 up. So a 100-megabyte video downloads in about 8 seconds, but uploads in about 40.

A text is tiny, so it still slips through.

When sending crawls, check your upload speed, not just download.
