Your message lands instantly; why does the video call lag?

That speed number is bandwidth: how many packets travel together. Lag is latency: how long each packet takes to arrive.

A high satellite hop adds about a quarter second each way, however wide the pipe. Past about 150 milliseconds, conversations stop feeling instant.

So check ping, not just download speed.
