Your phone is beside a tower, yet it uses a farther one. Why?

That tower may not belong to your network. Among your network's towers, measured signal counts, not distance. And when a stronger tower appears, the switch still waits. It must win by a margin, for a set time, to avoid bouncing back and forth.

So the nearest tower is not always yours, and switches take a moment.
