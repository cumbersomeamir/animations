## Deliberately not claimed
- Any number: phones per tower, speeds or capacity values. The meters have no scales and the board is labelled "illustration".
- That congestion is the *only* cause of slow data. S1 also lists interference, building materials and so on; the lesson stays on one concept.
- Anything about Meshline's features or behaviour. No nearby-messaging claims, no protocols, no range. The app appears only as a small end signature.
